New Model

MAGI-2

MAGI-2 possesses world-class video generation capabilities, excelling in areas such as high-precision instruction following, complex composition, spatiotemporal consistency, and long-video stability. It supports the generation of up to 30 seconds of native 1080P audio-video content in a single pass, and offers advanced features including multimodal referencing, precise editing, and autonomous camera planning.

Features

MAGI-2

Cinematic Quality, Close-Up Ready

Achieve authentic film lighting and intricate textures built to hold up even in extreme close-ups.

Studio-Quality Ads, Without the Studio

Produce polished commercials with crisp lighting and lifelike product textures—no studio required.

Every Character, a Method Actor

Capture subtle micro-expressions and natural body language that breathe genuine emotion into every scene.
