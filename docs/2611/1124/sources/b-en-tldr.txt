AI-Assisted Programming and Labor Demand

arxiv.org95 min read

Researchers studying the firm-level adoption of GitHub Copilot across thousands of multinational companies found that adopting the AI coding assistant reduces quarterly technical hiring by 29% among review-induced adopters. The hiring slowdown is heavily concentrated in junior technical roles that involve high shares of coding work, while separations, overall headcount, and vacancy duration remain unaffected. Consequently, firms shift their labor demand toward more experienced workers and roles combining coding with broader design responsibilities. Open article → [https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31083]
