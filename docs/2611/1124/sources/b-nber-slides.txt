  • AI can improve task-level productivity
     → AI capabilities are advancing rapidly, programming is at the frontier

  • Open question: what do these improvements in productivity mean for workers?
     → No near term effects?
     → Substantial employment decline?
     → More employment/work?

  • The central question is not only whether labor demand changes, but how and why
     → Margins: do ﬁrms adjust through hiring or separations?
     → Speed: is adjustment gradual or immediate?
     → Incidence: who is affected?

How Copilot can change demand for technical labor

  • Software production combines complementary tasks across roles
       → Technical roles: software, data, systems/infrastructure, cybersecurity, QA, design engineers; app
             developers

  • Copilot adoption is a software production productivity shock
       → Increases productivity of code-intensive, execution-heavy work (Cui et al., 2026; Peng et al., 2023)

  • The net effect on technical labor demand is ambiguous
       → Productivity: fewer worker-hours required per unit of software
       → Reallocation: tasks shift across workers, roles, seniority levels
       → Scale: lower production costs increase demand
