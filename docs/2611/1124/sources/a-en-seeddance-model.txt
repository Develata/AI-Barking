MAGI-2 Long Takes up to 30 Seconds

Most video models stop at 10 or 15 seconds, so a longer scene means stitching clips and hoping the seams hold. MAGI-2 keeps a single shot going for up to 30 seconds, which leaves room for a walk, a reveal and a change of mood in one take.

Sand.ai conditioned this clip on five classic paintings, among them The Starry Night, Water Lilies and A Sunday Afternoon on the Island of La Grande Jatte. The camera walks along a river and the world turns from Van Gogh’s brushwork into Seurat’s dots, Monet’s water and a gold-leaf garden, in one continuous 30-second take.

MAGI-2 Omni-Reference for Characters and Places

References are how a face, an outfit or a location stays the same from shot to shot. In Sand.ai’s native API a single MAGI-2 request can combine up to 10 images, 5 video clips and 5 audio clips. The generator here starts MAGI-2 from a text prompt.

In this Sand.ai example the board in the corner fixed one character and her robes, plus an iris hillside, a pink rose garden and a lily valley. The clip cuts between close-ups, low-angle views and wide shots, and passes from one garden to the next behind leaves and petals, with the character unchanged.

MAGI-2 Multi-Shot Scenes with Timed Cuts

MAGI-2 follows script-level timing: when a shot starts, how long it lasts, and whether a cut falls on an action or a timestamp. In Sand.ai’s timeline test, all 16 cut points it asked for landed where they were specified.

This 28-second scene is from Sand.ai’s launch post. A clockwork robot winds a music box on an attic desk while a boy sleeps, a cat wanders in, and the shots move from close-ups to the full room without losing the lamp, the window or the robot’s dents.

MAGI-2 Native Audio, Dialogue and Lip-Sync

MAGI-2 generates video and audio in one backbone, so a line of dialogue arrives with the mouth movement that goes with it, and footsteps or water land on the frame where they happen. Sound is on by default and can be switched off for a silent clip.

Sand.ai published this scene from MAGI-2’s 480B pre-trained checkpoint, with prompt rewriting turned off. It was given only the two characters, the boat and three story beats, with no framing, camera angles or cut points. The model chose the wide shot, the over-the-shoulder angles and the close-up on its own.

MAGI-2 1080p at 25 fps in Six Aspect Ratios

There is no lower draft tier: every MAGI-2 clip comes out at 1080p and 25 frames per second, in the aspect ratio you pick, so a test is already a usable cut.

This 30-second sunrise is from Sand.ai’s launch post: mist on the lake, light moving across the peaks and the paddle cutting the water. The page plays a smaller copy; the generator returns the full 1080p file.

MAGI-2 Pros and Cons

Where MAGI-2 is strong, and what to plan around.

Strengths

Length: Up to 30 seconds in one take, with sound.

Resolution: 1080p at 25 fps on every clip.

Sound: Dialogue with lip-sync, effects, ambience and music from the same model as the picture.

Directing: Follows timed cuts in the prompt, and plans its own shots when you give only story beats.

Limits

Resolution: 1080p only, so there is no cheaper 480p or 720p tier for quick tests.

Inputs here: The generator starts MAGI-2 from text; for frames, references or editing, use Seedance 2.5.

Known weak spots: Sand.ai lists artifacts in very fast motion, overly smooth 2D animation in medium and wide shots, and thinner sound in busy scenes.

New model: Released on November 24, 2026; Sand.ai has not yet published the open weights or the technical report.

MAGI-2 FAQ

What is MAGI-2?

MAGI-2 is a video generation model from Sand.ai, released on November 24, 2026. It is a mixture-of-experts model with 480B total and about 28B active parameters, and it generates up to 30 seconds of 1080p, 25 fps video with native audio.

How is MAGI-2 different from MAGI-2 Preview?

MAGI-2 Preview, released in August 2026, has about 114B total and 6B active parameters and makes 10-second clips. MAGI-2 is more than four times larger, was trained on about three times as much data, and goes to 30 seconds. On Artificial Analysis Video Arena, Sand.ai reports its four-task average Elo rising from 998.5 for the Preview to 1150.3.

How long can a MAGI-2 video be?

Between 5 and 30 seconds in the generator here, picked in one-second steps. 10 seconds is the default.

How many credits does MAGI-2 use?

MAGI-2 is charged per second of video at 1080p. A new account starts with some credits; the comparison table on this page shows a 5-second clip, and the generator shows the exact total before you start, so you can pick the model and length that fit your credits.

Does MAGI-2 generate sound?

Yes. Sound is on by default and comes from the same model as the picture: dialogue with matching lip movement, effects, ambience and music. You can switch it off for a silent clip.

Is MAGI-2 open source?

Not yet. Sand.ai says the open-source release and a technical report will follow. The earlier MAGI-2 Preview is available as open weights under the Apache 2.0 license.

Can I use reference images or edit a video with MAGI-2?

The model supports up to 20 references and video editing through Sand.ai’s own API. The generator here starts MAGI-2 from a text prompt; for first frames, reference images or editing a clip, Seedance 2.5 offers all of them.

Is SeedVideo AI affiliated with Sand.ai?

No. SeedVideo AI is an independent platform that runs MAGI-2 through an API. It is not operated by, endorsed by or affiliated with Sand.ai.
