Sand.ai launches the 480B-parameter MAGI-2 video model, achieving global ranking.

Summary

Sand.ai has launched its 480B-parameter MAGI-2 video model, which tops Artificial Analysis’s image-to-video leaderboard, outperforming Seedance 2.5. The model generates 30-second 1080P videos at a cost of 0.5 RMB per second—one-tenth the cost of Seedance 2.5. It accepts up to 20 inputs from images, videos, and audio, and utilizes a Multi-Head Latent MoE architecture. On-chain analysis reveals significant efficiency improvements. Sand.ai plans to open-source the model weights, though no timeline has been announced. Altcoins to watch may include projects offering similar open-source and cost advantages.

ME News reports that on November 25 (UTC+8), Sand.ai officially launched the video generation model MAGI-2, which has a total of 480 billion parameters and ranks first globally on the Artificial Analysis text-to-video leaderboard, surpassing Seedance 2.5. MAGI-2 supports generating videos up to 30 seconds long in 1080p resolution, accepts up to 20 reference inputs including images, videos, and audio files in a single prompt, and plans to open-source its model weights.
In terms of pricing, MAGI-2 charges 0.5 yuan per second for 1080p video generation, approximately one-tenth the cost of Seedance 2.5 at the same specification—generating a 30-second video costs only 15 yuan.
The MAGI-2 base model has increased its parameter count from 114B in the Preview version to 480B, with approximately 28B activated parameters and a pretraining dataset规模 of 4T tokens. The model employs a Multi-Head Latent MoE architecture and supports controlling video content through ultra-long prompts, temporal cues, and action instructions, while also offering post-generation targeted editing capabilities.
Sand.ai stated that the MAGI-2 model plans to open its weights in the future, though the specific timeline and implementation details have not yet been announced. Its applications include e-commerce advertising, brand promotion, and video localization, aiming to advance video models into scalable production workflows through longer generation times, multimodal references, and low-cost generation. (Source: BlockBeats)
