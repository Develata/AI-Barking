The matrix multiplication exponent is nine fourths [/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/The-matrix-multiplication-exponent-is-nine-fourths-November-24-2026/paper.pdf]
 [#the-matrix-multiplication-exponent-is-nine-fourths]

OpenAI · November 24, 2026 edition

The exponent of square matrix multiplication is $9/4$ over every field. The paper proves the matching lower bound for formal arithmetic circuits. At every side length $n\ge2$, at least $\lceil n^{9/4}/2\rceil$ nonscalar multiplication or division gates are required. The geometric border rank of the square multiplication tensor is at least $n^{9/4}$ and its asymptotic rank over the base field equals $n^{9/4}$.

The companion paper on tensor geometry [/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/Euclidean-geometry-in-the-asymptotic-spectrum-of-tensors-November-24-2026/paper.pdf] supplies the geometric framework for the lower bound.

Lean formalization
 [#lean-formalization]

The Lean development proves the square exponent $9/4$ for division-free circuits computing formal polynomial identities over every field. The scope document also states the formalized dual and rectangular exponents. Square-exponent proof [/openai/math/blob/main/lean/OAI/LinearAlgebra/SpectralBooks/ExactExponent.lean#L11-L12] · Formalization scope [/openai/math/blob/main/lean/docs/107.md]
