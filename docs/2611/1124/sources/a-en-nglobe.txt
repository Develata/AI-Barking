Sand AI Launches MAGI-2, a 480B-Parameter Video Model Generating 30-Second 1080p Clips With Native Audio

2026.11.25 16:23 UTC · N GLOBE

📤 Share

Chinese startup Sand AI has released MAGI-2, its flagship video model that generates up to 30 seconds of 1080p video with synchronized sound in a single pass, now available exclusively on Higgsfield.

Chinese artificial intelligence startup Sand AI on November 24 released MAGI-2, its flagship video generation model, which can produce up to 30 seconds of 1080p video with native synchronized audio in a single pass. The model is now available exclusively through US AI video platform Higgsfield, which launched it on November 25.

Caption: Illustration of text-to-video AI generation, not an image produced by MAGI-2. (Image: William Spurlock)

480 billion parameters, picture and sound in one pass

According to Sand AI's official blog, MAGI-2 uses a single-stream Transformer architecture with a Multi-Head Latent mixture-of-experts (MoE) design: about 480 billion total parameters, with roughly 28 billion activated per inference, trained on about 4 trillion tokens — roughly three times the data of MAGI-2 Preview (about 114 billion parameters), released in August. The model generates clips of 4 to 30 seconds at 1080p and 25 frames per second, with dialogue lip-sync, Foley effects, ambient sound and music produced together with the picture, without separate dubbing; it supports up to 20 image, video and audio references, can edit existing video and offers precise timeline control.

Benchmark results

Sand AI reports that MAGI-2 scores an average of 1150.3 across the four tasks of Artificial Analysis' Video Arena v2.1, up from 998.5 for Preview. Higgsfield's November 24 blind-test snapshot, however, ranks MAGI-2 second at 1146 points, behind ByteDance's Seedance 2.5 at 1219, with a 60.5% win rate. The scores are published by the developer or the platform and are indicative only.

From open-source Preview to flagship

MAGI-2 Preview, released on August 5, had about 114 billion parameters with roughly 6 billion active, and its weights were released under the Apache 2.0 licence for local deployment. The flagship is roughly four times larger, but its weights have not been released and it is only available as a hosted service. Competition in video generation is intensifying: OpenAI's Sora, ByteDance's Seedance and Kuaishou's Kling are iterating quickly, with clip length, native audio and controllability becoming the new battlegrounds.

Access and open-source plans

For now, MAGI-2 is available only through Higgsfield's website and API, priced at about $0.08 per second — around $2.40 for a 30-second clip. Open weights have not been released: Sand AI says it will open-source the model and publish a technical report, while Higgsfield says open weights are "coming soon", with no date or licence details. Note that Higgsfield's launch announcement describes the model as having 400 billion parameters, while Sand AI's blog and Higgsfield's product page say 480 billion; we use the developer's figure.
