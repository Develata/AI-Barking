About the launch

Sand AI's MAGI-2 generates clips of 4 to 30 seconds at 1080p and 25 frames per second, with sound, from text, images, video, or audio. It is live on Higgsfield at $0.08 per second, audio included. For creators, the shift from the August 2026 preview is length and access: that release was an open research checkpoint limited to 10-second clips, while this one is a hosted generator with timeline and shot-cut control, edits on existing video, and up to 20 references in a request (10 images, 5 videos, and 5 audio clips). Dialogue with lip-sync, foley, ambient sound, and music come from the same model.

Sand's research post puts the full model at 480 billion total parameters and about 28 billion active, trained on roughly 4 trillion tokens, more than four times the preview (about 114 billion total and about 6 billion active). It supports six aspect ratios. On the Higgsfield Video Arena snapshot from November 24, 2026, MAGI-2 ranked second of six models on that board, with a score of 1146 and a 60.5 percent win rate, behind Seedance 2.5. Sand says it will open-source the model and publish a technical report. The Higgsfield launch post describes the forthcoming weights as a 400 billion parameter release.

The company was founded by Cao Yue, a former principal researcher at Microsoft Research Asia, an author of Swin Transformer, and a recipient of the ICCV 2021 Best Paper Award (the Marr Prize). Sand previously released MAGI-1, an autoregressive video model, and open-sourced MAGI-2 Preview. The launch video is a short narrative with spoken lines, a fall, a chase, and an abrupt change of place, in line with the audio and cut control the release emphasizes.
