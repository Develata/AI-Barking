Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best Text to Video AI model on AA-Video-T2V v2.1?

Wan 3.0 currently leads AA-Video-T2V v2.1 with an Elo score of 1175.

What are the top models on AA-Video-T2V v2.1?

The top models on AA-Video-T2V v2.1 by Elo rating are: 1. Wan 3.0 (Elo 1175), 2. Utopai X (Elo 1173), 3. MiniMax H3 (768p) (Elo 1164), 4. Hyperflow H3 (Elo 1156), and 5. Dreamina Seedance 2.5 (Elo 1154). Rankings are based on blind votes from our validated private evaluators.

Which is the best open weights model on AA-Video-T2V v2.1?

MiniMax H3 (768p) currently leads among open weights models on AA-Video-T2V v2.1 with an Elo score of 1164, followed by Hyperflow H3 (Elo 1156) and Pika H3 Flex (Elo 1123).

Which is the best Text to Video AI model on AA-Video-T2V-Silent v2.1?

HeyGen Video 1 currently leads AA-Video-T2V-Silent v2.1 with an Elo score of 1147.

What are the top models on AA-Video-T2V-Silent v2.1?

The top models on AA-Video-T2V-Silent v2.1 by Elo rating are: 1. HeyGen Video 1 (Elo 1147), 2. MAGI-2 (Elo 1144), 3. Hyperflow H3 (Elo 1141), 4. Utopai X (Elo 1141), and 5. Wan 3.0 (Elo 1138). Rankings are based on blind votes from our validated private evaluators.

Which is the best open weights model on AA-Video-T2V-Silent v2.1?

Hyperflow H3 currently leads among open weights models on AA-Video-T2V-Silent v2.1 with an Elo score of 1141, followed by MiniMax H3 (768p) (Elo 1126) and Pika H3 Flex (Elo 1096).

How are Text to Video models ranked on this leaderboard?

Models are ranked using an Elo rating system derived from blind comparisons judged by our validated private evaluators. Voters compare two videos generated from the same text prompt without knowing which model created each video. Higher Elo scores indicate a model is preferred more often.
