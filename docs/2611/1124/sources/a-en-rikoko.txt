MAGI-2 vs Seedance 2.5: clip cost and limits (Nov 2026)
Copy page

MAGI-2 leads the supplied image-to-video ranking by four Elo points and lists at $4.80 per minute against $34.12 for Seedance 2.5. Here is how to compare price, retries and production fit.

rikokoNovember 25, 20268 min read

Artificial Analysis AA-Video-I2V v2.1 leaderboard, supplied for this review.

TL;DRCompare approved clips, not just the rate card

MAGI-2 has 1,165 Elo in the supplied AA-Video-I2V v2.1 snapshot. Seedance 2.5 has 1,161, so the score difference is only four points.

The same snapshot lists $4.80 and $34.12 per minute. That is a 7.1 times difference in listed price, before retries and editing.

A separate Chinese hosting offer quotes CNY 0.5 per second for MAGI-2. Its claimed one-tenth comparison belongs to that offer, not to the dollar prices on the leaderboard.

MAGI-2 supports up to 30 seconds at 1080p and 25 fps, with synchronized audio. Sand.ai documents motion, animation and complex sound as remaining limitations.

MAGI-2 makes a strong case on price, but a four-point Elo lead does not tell you which model will finish your next ad. The useful comparison is the full cost of a clip you can approve: generated seconds, failed takes, correction time and any separate voice or editing work.

What does the ranking actually show?

The supplied Artificial Analysis AA-Video-I2V v2.1 leaderboard ranks models on human preferences for animating a source image into video with audio. MAGI-2 is first in this view, with 1,165 Elo and an uncertainty of plus or minus 14. Dreamina Seedance 2.5 is second with 1,161, also plus or minus 14. Their plausible ranges overlap, and the chart labels both first and second place with the same range of 1 to 2.

AA-Video-I2V v2.1, supplied with this review. MAGI-2 has 5,740 samples and Seedance 2.5 has 7,162. The chart dates their releases to November and July 2026.

This is useful evidence of preference in one image-to-video evaluation. It is not proof that MAGI-2 wins every prompt, every language or every production task. Sand.ai also reports a four-task average of 1,150.3 Elo across I2V, silent I2V, T2V and silent T2V in Video Arena v2.1. Those are different aggregations. Do not substitute the average for the 1,165 score shown in the image above.

How much does a usable clip cost?

Start with seconds multiplied by the price for your actual provider. Then multiply by the number of attempts. Add editing or regeneration work when only one part of a take needs to change. This gives you a number you can compare with your production budget instead of a generic claim about being cheaper.

The calculation below uses $4.80 per minute for MAGI-2 and $34.12 per minute for Seedance 2.5, exactly as shown in the leaderboard. Each row assumes every attempt generates the full requested duration.

For example, three 30-second attempts at the listed MAGI-2 rate cost 1.5 minutes multiplied by $4.80, which is $7.20. Three attempts at the Seedance 2.5 rate cost $51.18. These are estimates from the displayed rates, not quotes from either company. Taxes, minimum charges and provider markups are not included.

The Chinese reports give a different hosting reference: CNY 0.5 per second on LibTV. At that rate, a 20-second generation is CNY 10 and a 30-second generation is CNY 15. Three 30-second attempts cost CNY 45. For one hour of generated source material with three attempts at every second, the arithmetic is 3,600 multiplied by CNY 0.5 multiplied by 3, or CNY 5,400. That hour is raw footage, not a finished one-hour episode.

Use one formula for your own jobs: generated seconds x attempts x provider rate + correction costs = cost per approved clip. Record a rejected take even if you never download it. It still consumed the budget.

Where does MAGI-2 still need work?

Sand.ai's own November 24 release lists motion and sound limitations. Extreme high-speed movement can produce trajectory or continuity artifacts. Medium and wide shots in 2D animation can make small local movements look too smooth. Complex soundscapes need better layering and physical realism.

The company also distinguishes its pre-trained checkpoint from the post-trained model. In its probes, the pre-trained model showed early signs of visual reasoning, implicit story completion and cross-lingual instruction following with prompt enhancement off. Those behaviors became harder to elicit after post-training on several thousand curated clips, while visual polish improved. Sand.ai calls this a working hypothesis, not a settled explanation.

A third-party tester reported that action scenes needed detailed movement instructions and that overly complex prompts could deform motion. That observation is useful when designing your test set, but it is not a measured success rate. The reported claim of more than 90% usable outputs also lacks a public test protocol in the materials reviewed, so this article does not use it to estimate your costs.

Which model fits your production?

Choose from the work, not from a blanket winner. At the listed per-minute rate, MAGI-2 makes repeated long-form experiments easier to justify. Seedance 2.5 remains worth testing when its particular rendering or motion behavior saves enough correction time to justify its higher generation bill.

How should you run a fair test?

Choose three jobs you actually produce. Include one ordinary product shot and one known difficult case.

Use the same reference files, duration, aspect ratio, dialogue and editing instructions for both models. Keep prompt enhancement consistent.

Run the same number of attempts with the same selection rule. Do not show the best result from one model and the first result from the other.

Score identity, product geometry, movement, prompt events, dialogue and final-second stability separately.

Record every charge and every minute spent repairing a take. Divide the total by the number of clips you would publish.

Keep the prompt and seed where the service exposes them, along with the provider and generation date. That record makes an unexpected success repeatable. It also helps you see whether a model is improving your work or simply producing attractive examples.

Frequently asked questions

Is MAGI-2 better than Seedance 2.5?

It leads the supplied image-to-video preference ranking by four Elo points, with overlapping uncertainty. That is a narrow result in one evaluation. Your references, shots and approval rate should decide the production choice.

Is MAGI-2 one-tenth the price of Seedance 2.5?

That is the reported comparison for a Chinese LibTV offer. In the supplied Artificial Analysis chart, $4.80 against $34.12 per minute is about 14.1%, or roughly one-seventh. Use the prices and currency from the service you intend to pay.

Can MAGI-2 generate a 30-second video with sound?

Sand.ai specifies up to 30 seconds at 1080p and 25 fps with native audio-video generation. It also supports six aspect ratios. Review the full duration, especially the ending and spoken audio.

Can it use reference videos and audio, not only images?

Yes. The official specification allows up to 20 media items per native request: 10 images, 5 videos and 5 audio clips. That is the full model's documented limit, not the limit of the older open Preview.

Can I choose MAGI-2 in rikoko now?

This review does not announce a rikoko integration. Check the model choices shown in your current workspace before planning a job.
