Frequently asked questions

1. What is Magi-1?

Magi-1, developed by Sand.ai, is the world’s first autoregressive video generation model with high quality content output. Magi-1 revolutionizes video generation and production, empowering creators through advanced AI video generation and AI video extender technologies.

2. How Long Can Magi AI Generate Content?

Currently, it supports videos ranging from 1 to 10 seconds, with the option to extend the length indefinitely. Also, Magi AI supports unlimited generation. Our video extender allows you to utilize our continuation feature without time constraints to bring your ideas to real life.

3. What Types of Content Can Magi AI Create?

Owing to Magi AI's autoregressive architecture, it excels in realistic style and 3D semi-cartoon styles. Also, it can definitely be used to support different types of creators.

4. How Can I Use Magi AI as an AI Video Extender to Lengthen My Videos?

With Magi AI, the AI video generation model, you can easily extend the length of your videos by utilizing the 'AI Video Extender' feature available in the website. This functionality allows you to add more time to your videos effortlessly. MAGI AI's infinite video expansion ability enables the seamless extension of video content, along with 'second-level time axis control,' allowing users to perform scene transitions and detailed editing through chunk-by-chunk prompting, catering to the requirements of film production and storytelling.

5. Where Is the Open Source Information for Magi-1?

The code and model weights for the Magi-1 AI video generation model are openly accessible on GitHub and Hugging Face.GitHub: Magi-1 [https://github.com/sandai-org/MAGI-1]Hugging Face: Magi-1 [https://huggingface.co/sand-ai/MAGI-1/tree/main/ckpt/magi/4.5B_base]

6. How Does Magi-1 Ensure Smooth and Natural Video Output?

It uses autoregressive video block prediction and causal attention to produce natural motion and smooth transitions. Magi AI generates videos in segments rather than all at once. Each segment consists of 24 frames and is denoised as a whole. The generation of the next segment starts as soon as the current one achieves a specific level of denoising.

7. How Does Magi-1 Perform in Evaluations?

MAGI AI delivers cutting-edge performance among open-source models, excelling in following instructions and motion quality, which positions it as a formidable competitor to closed-source commercial models like Kling1.6. Additionally, it shows enhanced accuracy in predicting physical behavior through video continuation, significantly surpassing all current models.

8. What Is the Impact of Magi-1's Autoregressive Architecture?

Magi demonstrates exceptional accuracy in predicting physical behavior on the Physics-IQ benchmark through video continuation, significantly surpassing all current models. https://github.com/google-deepmind/physics-IQ-benchmark

9. How Do I Get Started with Magi AI?

Getting started with MAGI AI is super easy! First, register on our website. Next, upload your image, input your creative idea as text prompt, and let our Magi AI do its magic. Once the processing is done, you can download your newly created video. It’s just that simple!

10. What Features Does Magi AI Offer?

The MAGI AI video generation model includes several features: a Transformer-based VAE for rapid decoding and high reconstruction quality, an autoregressive denoising algorithm for efficient video generation, and a diffusion model architecture that improves training efficiency and stability at scale. Additionally, it allows for controllable generation through chunk-wise prompting, facilitating smooth scene transitions, long-term synthesis, and detailed text-driven control.
