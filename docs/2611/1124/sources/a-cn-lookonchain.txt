Sand.ai发布480B视频生成模型MAGI-2，图生视频登顶全球榜单，生成成本仅为Seedance 2.5的1/10

5 hours ago

，Sand.ai 正式发布视频生成模型 MAGI-2，模型总参数量达 480B，在 Artificial Analysis 图生视频榜单中排名全球第一，超过 Seedance 2.5。MAGI-2 支持最长 30 秒、1080P 视频生成，单次可输入最多 20 份图片、视频和音频素材作为参考，并计划开源模型权重。价格方面，MAGI-2 的 1080P 视频生成定价为 0.5 元/秒，约为 Seedance 2.5 同规格价格的 1/10，生成一条 30 秒视频仅需 15 元。据介绍，MAGI-2 基础模型参数量从 Preview 版本的 114B 提升至 480B，激活参数量约 28B，预训练数据规模达到 4T Tokens。模型采用 Multi-Head Latent MoE 架构，并支持通过超长提示词、时间节点和动作指令控制视频内容，同时具备生成后定向编辑能力。Sand.ai 表示，MAGI-2 后续计划开放模型权重，具体时间和实施方案尚未公布。其应用场景包括电商广告、品牌宣传及视频本地化等，试图通过更长生成时长、多模态参考和低成本生成，推动视频模型进入规模化生产流程。

The Nasdaq 100 has pulled back over 20% in this cycle, placing it among the top ten largest drawdowns in its history.

According to data from the BIT Stock Platform, driven by tech stock valuation re-rating and intensified market volatility, the Nasdaq 100 Index has trended lower steadily since hitting an all-time high on October 6, 2026. As of press time, the Nasdaq 100’s intraday maximum drawdown stands at roughly 20.53%, officially entering a technical bear market. A longitudinal comparison with historical data since the index’s launch in 1985 shows the current correction magnitude is nearly on par with that of the 1998 LTCM crisis (-23.0%), late 2018 rate hike sell-off (-23.2%), and spring 2025 tariff shock (-22.9%). However, it still has a buffer of about 8 to 13 percentage points from the 2020 early COVID-19 slump (-28.6%) and the 1990 Gulf War shock (-32.9%). Historical patterns indicate that the Nasdaq 100’s "moderate correction" (with a drawdown of 20%–30%) typically bottoms out within 1 to 4 months, and recovers all lost ground within the subsequent 3 to 8 months. Once the decline exceeds 30% — as seen in events like the 1990 Gulf War, 2022 rate hike cycle, and 2000 dot-com bubble — the index’s recovery period often stretches to 1 year or even several years.
