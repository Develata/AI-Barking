MAGI-2, the largest SOTA open source omni-reference model to date, developed by Sand.ai [https://sand.ai/], is now available exclusively on Higgsfield. The 400B-parameter video model accepts up to 20 media references, with open weights coming soon.

Only Seedance 2.5 ranks higher on the Higgsfield Video Arena, while they differ significantly in cost per second. MiniMax H3 is slightly cheaper, but ranks last on the leaderboard.

Key Features:

High-performance accessible model. MAGI-2 is a 400B Mixture-of-Experts model with only about 28B active parameters per piece of video. It splits each piece evenly across GPUs so every GPU picks experts locally, which makes the model practical to serve.

Thirty seconds in one take. Up to 30 seconds at 1080p and 25 fps, in six aspect ratios.

Faces and text stay sharp. Upscaling to 1080p references original images so small faces and on-screen text stay clear and accurate.

Up to 20 references per request. 10 images, 5 videos and 5 audio clips.

Better in blind tests. Ranks #2 on the Higgsfield Video Arena [https://open.higgsfield.ai/models/sand/magi-2/text-to-video/playground] with a 1146 score and a 60% overall win rate.
