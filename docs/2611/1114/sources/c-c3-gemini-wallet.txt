Personal Intelligence [https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/personal-intelligence-expansion/] in Gemini securely connects information from apps you already use — like Gmail, Photos, YouTube, and Search — to make your experience more personalized and helpful. Today, we’re adding Google Wallet to the list of connected apps so you can get more tailored responses around personal finance.

Understand and plan your finances with Google Wallet

Rolling out today for all Gemini app users in the U.S., you can connect Google Wallet for responses informed by your spending habits. You also have the option to securely link your financial accounts from your credit cards and banks to Wallet via Plaid [https://plaid.com/] to unlock deeper insights.

Jump into Gemini and tag @Google Wallet to ask questions around personal finance, budgeting and more. Here are some things to try:

Visualize spending: Questions like “can we review my spending from last quarter so I can save $1,000 over the next three months?” enable Gemini to generate dynamic charts tracking cash flow, asset allocation, and budgets directly in your chat.

Find forgotten subscriptions: If you ask "what subscriptions am I still paying for, and did any of them go up in price this year?" Gemini finds recurring charges across your linked accounts and flags price increases.

Budget for the holidays: When you ask "how much did I spend on gifts and travel last year? Use that to help me set a holiday budget for this year," Gemini will look at your transactions from the last year and suggest a budget tailored to you.

Google Wallet in Gemini is rolling out across web and mobile, for Gemini app users on both Android and iOS.

Stay in control

We’ve built these tools with security and privacy at the center. If you choose to connect your Wallet (under Connected Apps in Personal Intelligence settings [https://gemini.google.com/personalization-settings]), Gemini can only read information from your accounts; it can’t move, manage, or invest your money. You decide if and when you want to opt in to this experience, and you can turn the connection on or off at any time. Learn more here [https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/16836988?visit_id=639298553969879312-4057695469&p=b_pi_ca&rd=1].

For Personal Intelligence, we’re also making it easier for you to see and edit what Gemini remembers. Memory Summary [https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18617127] gives you a clear, readable snapshot of your saved preferences, interests, and ongoing goals in one place so you always know how Gemini tailors responses for you. You can review and edit anytime. Open Profile > Personal Intelligence [https://gemini.google.com/personalization-settings] > Memory Summary to view, refine, or remove any details whenever you want. Memory Summary is available on web and mobile for all Gemini app users where Personal Intelligence is available.
