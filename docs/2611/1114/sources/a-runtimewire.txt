Apps built with Lovable left passport numbers and 200,000 chat messages readable without a login

Crissy Field scanned 159,041 projects and found email records on 7,403 sites built with Lovable.

 By Ryan Merket [/author/ryan-merket]
 · Published Nov 3rd, 2026, 8:51am CT

Apps built with Lovable left passport numbers, payment information and more than 200,000 chat messages readable without a login, according to research published by Hamburg application-security firm Crissy Field in collaboration with German business daily Handelsblatt. Crissy Field examined 159,041 Lovable projects and found records containing email addresses in 10,560 tables across 7,403 sites.…
