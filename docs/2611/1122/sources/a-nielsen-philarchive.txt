Invalidity of the Alpoge Proposed Proof of the Riemann Hypothesis
Jenny Lorraine Nielsen
Abstract
On 20 November 2026 Levent Alpöge announced a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis and of the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis for Dirichlet 
𝐿
L-functions, released only as Lean 4 code. The archive holds 175{,}187 Lean files. Inspection of the source shows that the construction supplying the final hypothesis bundle rests on witnesses whose required properties are vacuously true. The geometry predicate is defined to be True. The geometric witness is built from a model the code itself names degenerate. The lattice witness has its required properties by definition. A statement that fixes a line-bundle degree holds only because its hypothesis cannot be satisfied. The lemmas written to extract content from these witnesses are never called. The pieces that would identify the row-side geometry with the divisor-side witness are the vacuous ones, so the chain between them is broken. On the path to the conclusion the height equation holds by rfl, because the height is assigned to be its own formula. The formalization therefore does not establish the geometric realization or the lattice identification that its own structure names. The proposed proof is invalid: what has been checked is a term whose structural witnesses are vacuously true, not the argument those witnesses stand for. Alpöge’s release typechecks against Mathlib’s `RiemannHypothesis`. The kernel (and independent checkers) correctly confirm that the final term has that type. No `axiom` declarations appear, and no `sorry` is used on the path to the conclusion. The term typechecks because the structural claims the later steps rely on are made true by definition. - The geometry predicate is defined as `anyGeometry := fun _ _ _ => True`. - The inductive closure `geoCl` has a `base` constructor that accepts any proof of the underlying predicate. With `anyGeometry`, `GeoCl . base trivial` produces a certificate for every input. - The `HypsAlt` construction passes `geoCl anyGeometry` into the hypothesis bundle. Transport lemmas (`oAt_setDiv` and the `GeoCl . step` cases) carry that certificate across divisor changes. They copy lattice data separately and never replace the geometry component with a `geoOf` witness. - The height function is defined to be the formula (`hExpl6`) it is later required to equal, so the equation holds by `rfl`. - Lattice fields are defined as copies, so their equalities hold by `rfl`. The resulting bundle is inhabited. `plugAll` consumes it and produces the all-character zero-free statement. The ordinary functional-equation conversion (`Claim.zeta`) turns that statement into `Claim.Zeta`, which is definitionally identical to Mathlib’s `RiemannHypothesis`. The local body of the conversion contains no geometry; the proof term it receives carries the vacuous certificate. The statements that are proved are therefore the trivial ones under those definitions. The geometric claims the names suggest—an independent height obtained from the degree and positivity of a line bundle, a real surface, a non-vacuous geometric certificate—are never proved. The lemmas written to extract content from the geometric witnesses are not called. The kernel verifies a derivation relative to the definitions it is given. It does not verify that those definitions carry the mathematics the names claim. Here they do not. The term is a correct derivation of Mathlib’s statement under definitions that make the critical structural witnesses hold by construction. That is why the code compiles, and why compilation does not establish the geometric argument that was claimed. Any proof route is corrupted ; there is no deeper space for a "deeper proof". The only proofs provided by the code are based on vacuous definitions. The top-level theorem inhabits Mathlib’s Riemann hypothesis and the source contains no axiom, sorry, or native_decide declarations. The actual proof term for everyCharacter (and therefore for riemannHypothesis) passes through a hypothesis bundle whose geometric component is the constant predicate anyGeometry := fun _ _ _ => True, closed under the inductive wrapper GeoCl. The lattice equalities required of the divisor witness hold by rfl because the seed is defined as a copy of the row-side data. The height equation div.h u = hExpl6 … holds by rfl because setDiv overwrites the height function with the explicit formula itself. These three definitional steps are not independent defects. Taken together they force the divisor/geometric side and the row/analytic side to be the same object. The inequality that is supposed to trap an independent geometric height between an automorphic pairing and the main term therefore compares a quantity with itself and holds by reflexivity. The long chain of theta lifts, seesaws and slope estimates is applied only after this identification has already occurred. Consequently the formalization certifies only the tautology RiemannHypothesis = RiemannHypothesis under the supplied definitions. It contains no independent geometric realization of the analytic side, and no alternate algebraic route that avoids the vacuous package. The release does not contain a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. The proposed proof is invalid. The release has not been shown to contain any proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. The lean code compiles, but nothing is proved. The dependency trace of the compilation confirms the invalid vacuous proof chain.
