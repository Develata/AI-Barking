Yes, 17m lines of Lean is a lot

2026-11-22

The recent proof of the Riemann hypothesis came with a statistic attached: the proof is 17 million lines of Lean. Beyond the philosophical questions of what it means to have a proof longer than any human could comprehend, I’m left with a simpler question: is that as large as it sounds?

To investigate, I hunted for collections of Lean proofs online. I found two and charted the lengths of their proofs.

(All charts have a log x axis, which makes 17m lines look a lot closer to 1m and 100k than it really is.)

Famous theorems in Mathlib

Mathlib maintains a collection of famous proofs. There are over 1k but only 259 have Lean associated, which I chart here.

These are all human generated, and as we can see, they come nowhere close to 17m.

 [/images/lean-proof-lengths/famous-theorems-histogram.png]

Every Lean package on Reservoir

Reservoir [https://reservoir.lean-lang.org] indexes every published Lean package, 938 of which have Lean code on GitHub. This one has a lot more data, including AI assisted proofs. We even have a few that are over 16m!:

88m: Qiuzhen-CFSG/CFSG [https://github.com/Qiuzhen-CFSG/CFSG] this appears to be an AI built proof that is still a work in progress.

36m: CBirkbeck/LeanBridge [https://github.com/CBirkbeck/LeanBridge], a project linking the LMFDB [https://www.lmfdb.org] database of number theory objects to Mathlib. This isn’t really a proof: about 98% of its Lean is files generated by scripts that check the database’s numbers one by one. The hand-written part is closer to 500k lines.

27m: anthropics/fermats-last-theorem [https://github.com/anthropics/fermats-last-theorem] is another Anthropic special: a complete proof of Fermat’s Last Theorem, published on GitHub in September. Its README says “the Lean sources were produced by AI agents building on human-written open-source Lean”, including the human FLT project [https://github.com/ImperialCollegeLondon/FLT] led by Kevin Buzzard, which is about 60k lines and still in progress. The AI version is roughly 450 times longer.

 [/images/lean-proof-lengths/reservoir-histogram.png]

So yes, 17m lines is a lot.
