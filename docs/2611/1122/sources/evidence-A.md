## 三、可能的吠点（说法 vs 证据，仅线索，进正文前须再核）

1. **“Lean 官方认证 / certify”的主体与范围**。说法：中文标题“获 Lean 官方认证”“正式认证”（新浪科技 11-22 00:55、腾讯 11-22 12:55）。证据：声明自称 “Joint statement by the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative.”，写的是“both the statement and the proof … are correct”，对象是 Lean 里这份证明与 Mathlib 的 `RiemannHypothesis` 命题；Zulip 上 Loeffler：“it makes a declaration about formalization, not about mathematics at large”（msg 642648569）；Morrison：“provide the single bit of information "the proof is correct"”（642648880）；声明末句“We look forward to all the activity in the mathematical community that will help explain the new ideas in this proof.” 页面标题与署名出处 A1-1、A1-2。
2. **署名改过**。博客最初署“Lean and Mathlib communities, endorsed by the Lean FRO and the Mathlib Initiative”，11-21 北京 22:26 发布，约 3 小时内即被改过一次又回退（PR #149 11-21 16:41:35Z 合并改为 “Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative”，PR #150 17:36:31Z 回退为原署名），约 43 小时后（11-23 北京 17:38，PR #154）Mathlib 维护者才改为“Lean FRO and Mathlib Initiative”，理由 “to avoid attribution to the Lean and Mathlib communities as a whole”（A1-4）。即“社区联合声明”的说法曾被声明方自己收回。

## 七、对读者

- **能做什么**：任何人都可以在自己机器上复核，但门槛极高。Sutherland 页：“With Lean v4.36.0 installed, nothing needs to be built. You need roughly 100 GB of RAM and 70 GB of disk.”；最快的独立检查 con-leche “needs about 50 GB of RAM and took under four minutes on 64 threads”（`a-sutherland.txt` “Check it yourself” 一节，出处 https://math.mit.edu/~drew/grh/ ）。导出文件压缩 7.7 GB，解压 61,570,595,788 字节。
