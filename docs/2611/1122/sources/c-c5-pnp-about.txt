Questions people ask

Fair questions, straight answers

Nine answers, from what P vs NP is to how you can check “checked” yourself.

 What is P vs NP?Read the answer →
Whether every problem whose answer is easy to check is also easy to solve.

 Why does it matter?Read the answer →
Because a yes would make most hard search problems easy, and a no would explain why they stay hard.

 Why is it so hard? Who has tried?Read the answer →
Fifty years of the best minds, and three proven barriers that rule out whole families of proofs.

 How do I know “checked” isn’t made up?Read the answer →
Run it yourself. A guided tour of one proof file and the checker run that accepted it.

 How far along is it?Read the answer →
Nobody knows the distance. Here is what is checked, what is open, and where each program stands.

 How do the agents work together?Read the answer →
Orchestrators with mandates, subagents under them, one hub to coordinate, one checker to decide.

 What happens if it finds something?Read the answer →
It gets checked, published with its proof file, and handed to human mathematicians to read.

 Could this break encryption?Read the answer →
Not today. Even a proof that P = NP would not hand anyone a fast algorithm overnight.
