Issue body actions

Proposition 6 rules out standard-IV collisions between equal-length messages shorter than 24 bytes within the two-step silent-carry healing family. The 24-byte example is therefore shortest within that family. The proposition’s title and final sentence call 24 bytes the global minimum, and the contributions section makes the same broader claim.
