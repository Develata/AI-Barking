Issue body actions

I read §3.3 as saying that after eight steps from the standard IV, all four words (W_0,W_2,W_4,W_6) lie in ({x,\neg x}) for the same (x). Let (p_0) be the parity of the number of bits where (W_0) and (W_2) differ, and let (p_4) be the same parity for (W_4) and (W_6). Each pair differs in either 0 bits or 32 bits, so (p_0=p_4=0). Theorem 2 requires (p_4=p_0\oplus1) at every step from the standard IV. §3.3 [https://github.com/MiniMax-AI/Research/blob/57842cef92e84eaf8e2535b12f48ba5586080ddd/Cryptography/Blender/paper/main.tex#L644] · Theorem 2 [https://github.com/MiniMax-AI/Research/blob/57842cef92e84eaf8e2535b12f48ba5586080ddd/Cryptography/Blender/paper/main.tex#L463-L470]
