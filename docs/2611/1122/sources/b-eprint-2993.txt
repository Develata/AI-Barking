Paper 2026/2993

Packing Stern into L2: GPU-Accelerated Primal-Shortened ISD for High-Codimension Binary Goppa Key Recovery [/2026/2993.pdf]

Raullen Chai [https://orcid.org/0009-0000-6665-8698], Machinefi Labs

Xinxin Fan, Machinefi Labs

Abstract

Primal-Shortened Information-Set Decoding (ISD) with quadratic polar lifting recently pushed TII McEliece Track-2 key recovery to (TII-228, 2,463 CPU core-hours), with over 99% of runtime spent harvesting weight- codewords. Porting MiniMax's Mode-6 Stern sampler directly to one block per SM leaves 1.0–1.5 GB of hash tables active and stalls on global memory. For , we pack all remaining syndrome bits into one 16-byte ulonglong2 without left-triple indices, share cooperative tables inside GPU L2 cache, batch random systematic reductions per launch, and widen the shortening prefix to cut modeled GPU-hours by 34%–47%.

Across four Track-2 challenges (), every recovered private key passes both the official SageMath checker and a standalone pure-Python verifier. Eight Modal L40S GPUs solve TII-228 () in 18.9 min (2.15 GPU-hours, faster in wall-clock than MiniMax's EPYC cluster). We further recover keys for the previously undocumented challenges TII-237 (, 22.1 L4 GPU-hours) and TII-235 (, 4.30 h across ten L40S GPUs, 39.62 GPU-hours, 17.97 s lift), using 127 of 128 packed bits. These results do not demonstrate practical recovery against Classic McEliece parameters ().

Note: Full CUDA source code (Apache-2.0, derived from MiniMax), all four verified equivalent private keys (TII-245, TII-228, TII-237, TII-235), 131,679 harvested codewords, and a zero-dependency pure-Python verifier are publicly available at: https://github.com/raullenchai/tii-mceliece-gpu
