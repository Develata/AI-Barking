Frequently Asked Questions

How do I control my Ring Drone Cam?

Everything is controlled from the Ring app. You can send Ring Drone Cam:

To a room in your home.

To a viewpoint generated during mapping, or one you've created.

To a point on the map by tapping a spot on your home's map.

On a patrol route: launch it yourself, or schedule it (subscription required).

How long can Ring Drone Cam fly?

Up to 8 minutes per flight. When the flight is over or the battery runs low, it flies back to its dock to recharge.

What are custom viewpoints?

Viewpoints are saved spots in your space that Ring Drone Cam remembers so you see the same view every time you send it there. You can rename, move, or delete viewpoints anytime in the Ring app.

Does Ring Drone Cam record all the time?

No. Ring Drone Cam only records while it's flying.

Can Ring Drone Cam see in the dark?

Yes. Ring Drone Cam has powerful LED spotlights that light up dark rooms and hard-to-see areas while it flies. It doesn't use infrared night vision.

Does Ring Drone Cam have audio or two-way talk?

No. Ring Drone Cam doesn't have a microphone or speaker, so it doesn't record audio or support two-way talk.

Does Ring Drone Cam have a siren?

No. Ring Drone Cam doesn't have a siren.

Can Ring Drone Cam be used outdoors?

No. Ring Drone Cam is designed for indoor use only, between 41°F and 95°F (5°C to 35°C).
