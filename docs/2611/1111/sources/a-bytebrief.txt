Google Agents Find Unmapped Maya Pyramid

SummaryKey points
5 min read

Google SWE points 48 AI agents at NASA Lidar data to find an unmapped Maya pyramid in Mexico within 72 hours. The team analyzed 1,200 km² of jungle, identifying a hilltop site with an oval pyramid at least 8, 12 m tall. Researchers contacted INAH, who confirmed no registered archaeological site exists at that location.
