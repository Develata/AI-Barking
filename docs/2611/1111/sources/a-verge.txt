An AI agent swarm may have unearthed an unmapped Maya site.

Google researcher Pietro Miotti says [https://pietromiotti.com/latest/maya-site/] 48 AI agents combing NASA Lidar data spotted signs of a previously unrecorded ancient site in Mexico. He’s not an archeologist, and the find still needs fieldwork to verify, but it’s a cool glimpse into why scientists are so excited by these tools.

It also highlights why scientists can bristle when outsiders parachute in with AI tools. Archeologist Sarah Parcak said [https://bsky.app/profile/indyfromspace.bsky.social/post/3n2235hwwsk2m] on Bluesky that Tulane’s Francisco Estrada-Belli published the site years ago.

Update: This post was updated to include context from Sarah Parcak about the site’s earlier publication.
