WHAT IS THE AI SAFETY & PRIVACY STANDARD


  The American Federation of Teachers (AFT) and Microsoft Corp. have agreed upon a landmark AI Safety &
  Privacy Standard that establishes protections AI use in education. The Standard is designed to protect students,
  educators and families and allows any school in America to incorporate these protections into their agreements
  with Microsoft and hold Microsoft accountable to them.


WHY THE STANDARD IS NEEDED

Students, families, and educators deserve technology that protects privacy, strengthens safety, and keeps people in
control. These commitments reflect principles Microsoft has long championed. We believe the AFT Safety & Privacy
Standard creates clear guidelines for how AI should be used in schools. This gives parents and educators greater
confidence while requiring AI companies to meet higher standards for safe, responsible and privacy-protecting AI in
schools.

At its core, the agreement turns shared principles into clear, enforceable protections centered on three priorities:

MICROSOFT’S COMMITMENT

For Schools           Beginning November 1, 2026, Microsoft will make the protections offered in the AI Safety &
                      Privacy Standard available to every school district in the United States. Districts can add them
                      to new or existing agreements with Microsoft, with no contract renegotiation or renewal
                      required. The protections are contractually enforceable if those commitments are not met.



For Educators &       Microsoft will provide education leaders and families with clear, plain-language information

                      the safeguards that protect students. Parents deserve straightforward information about the
                      technology used in their schools.
