AI agents flagged a Maya site in NASA LiDAR data that archaeologists had already published

Pietro Miotti says 48 agents scanned 1,200 km2 of NASA LiDAR data and flagged a hilltop absent from INAH records. He later said the site appears in a 2025 peer-reviewed paper.

 By Ryan Merket [/author/ryan-merket]
 · Published Nov 10th, 2026, 2:16pm CT
 · Updated Nov 11th, 2026, 1:18pm CT

Pietro Miotti (@PietroMiotti) says his AI agents flagged a Maya site in Mexico after scanning NASA LiDAR data, and that Mexico's National Institute of Anthropology and History replied that it has no registered archaeological site at the location he submitted. He posted the claim in a thread on X on November 10th and laid out the timeline in his full account.
