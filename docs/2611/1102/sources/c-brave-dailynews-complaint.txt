               B.      Brave’s Products

       30.     Brave’s products either include features powered by Large Language Models

(“LLMs”) or serve as components for developing and operating LLMs. LLMs are a type of AI

system that generates expressive language resulting in text or other data, rather than just analyzing

existing data. LLMs work by predicting words that are likely to follow a given string of text based

on the content used to train them. They use algorithms to weigh the relevance of different parts of

the input data (e.g., a user’s inquiry) when generating text. LLM operators “train” their models on

vast datasets of written material, allowing them to encode patterns and relationships between

words and sentences.

       31.     Once trained, LLMs can generate text by taking a seed input (again, for example, a

user’s inquiry) and iteratively predicting the most likely next word based on the patterns they have

learned. Through this process, LLMs can generate answers to questions about content that is

included in their training data.

       32.     LLMs are also capable of copying content from a search index like the Brave Index

or other sources as input, then outputting, excerpting, summarizing, or answering questions about

that content. For example, LLMs may also be deployed in conjunction with a technique called

“retrieval augmented generation” (“RAG”), also known as “grounding,” “retrieving,” or

“contextualizing.” Through RAG, trained LLMs may be provided with information specific to a

use case or subject matter in order to “ground” their outputs. For example, an LLM may be asked

to generate a text output based on specific external data, such as a document, provided as context.

Using this method, a GenAI product based on an LLM will: (1) receive an input, such as a prompt;

(2) retrieve relevant content related to the input prior to generating a response; (3) combine the

original input with the retrieved documents; and (4) provide the combined content to an LLM,

which generates a response.
