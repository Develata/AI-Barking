PhD Student at MIT CSAIL, Princeton CS '24

Hi! I broadly work on areas where language models are underutilized or inefficient. I am currently a 2nd year PhD student at MIT CSAIL [https://www.csail.mit.edu] advised by Omar Khattab [https://omarkhattab.com] and Tim Kraska [https://people.csail.mit.edu/kraska/]. I am grateful to be a Laude Open Research Resident [https://www.laude.org] and a research fellow at Prime Intellect [https://www.primeintellect.ai] to push forward open science. I am also a recipient of the NSF GRFP [https://www.nsfgrfp.org].

 To understand what I’m thinking about right now, read in order:

Recursive Language Models (RLMs) [https://alexzhang13.github.io/blog/2025/rlm/].

The Mismanaged Geniuses Hypothesis [https://alexzhang13.github.io/blog/2026/mgh/].

Empirical evidence of harnesses inducing locally in-distribution trajectories [https://alexzhang13.github.io/blog/2026/harness/].

Prime Agent [https://github.com/PrimeIntellect-ai/prime-agent], a production RLM harness.

Speculative programmatic tool calling (PTC) [https://alexzhang13.github.io/blog/2026/spec-ptc/].

The input / output "shape" [https://alexzhang13.github.io/blog/2026/shape/] of language models.

Some other notable things worth mentioning:

I used to make a lot of benchmarks: VideoGameBench [http://www.vgbench.com], KernelBench [https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2502.10517], SWE-bench Multimodal [https://www.swebench.com/multimodal.html] .

I am part of the core team running the GPU MODE [https://www.gpumode.com/v2/home] competitions [/misc/] and community [https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCJgIbYl6C5no72a0NUAPcTA].

I graduated as the top undergraduate student of the Princeton CS department [https://www.cs.princeton.edu/news/class-day-department-celebrates-accomplishments-graduates].

I was named one of SciAm [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_American]'s Young American Scientists [https://www.scientificamerican.com/report/young-american-scientists-2026/] of 2026.

I’ve had brief but pleasant stints at a variety of companies: Sakana AI [https://sakana.ai], VantAI [https://www.vant.ai], Snap Research [https://research.snap.com], Apple [https://machinelearning.apple.com], and Claryo [https://www.claryo.co].

Feel free to reach out to talk about anything through my email at [x]@mit.edu where [x]=[已去除]. I also help moderate the GPU MODE [https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCJgIbYl6C5no72a0NUAPcTA] community. As a last fun fact, I used to make and sell PC games, one of which was mildly successful (~100k+ players). [unfinished example] [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6Mmfo44FxhA]
