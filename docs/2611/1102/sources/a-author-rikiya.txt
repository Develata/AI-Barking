Rikiya Takehi

PhD Student at MIT CSAIL

I am a first-year PhD student at MIT CSAIL, advised by Prof. Omar Khattab [https://omarkhattab.com/]. I recently received a B.Eng. from Waseda University, where I was supervised by Prof. Tetsuya Sakai [http://sakailab.com/tetsuya/]. Previously, I was a research intern at NVIDIA Research [https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai/] and Mixedbread [https://www.mixedbread.com/]. Before that, I was a guest researcher at NIST [https://www.nist.gov/] working with Dr. Ian Soboroff [https://www.nist.gov/people/ian-soboroff] and Dr. Ellen Voorhees [https://www.nist.gov/people/ellen-m-voorhees], and I have also collaborated with Prof. Fernando Diaz [https://841.io/] of CMU LTI on retrieval rankings.

My graduate studies are supported by the Toyota PhD Fellowship [https://www.toyotariken.jp/overseas/] and the MIT Distinguished Science and Engineering Fellowship.

I am interested in Machine Learning (ML), Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Information Retrieval (IR). Now, I am especially interested in designing better LLMs / LLM Systems / LLM Agents.
