Takeaways by Bloomberg AI

Goldman Sachs Group Inc. [https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GS:US] has irked some of its staff after it banned most of them from using advanced artificial intelligence tools designed for coding after reviewing productivity and risk concerns.

For months, Goldman allowed bankers to use Anthropic PBC [https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/ANT:US]’s Claude Code and Microsoft Corp. [https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT:US]’s GitHub for work. Now, the bank is limiting the use of those tools to engineers only, according to internal guidance updated in October and seen by Bloomberg News.
