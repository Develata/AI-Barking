Apple Faces Korean Regulator Review Over iPhone 16 AI Advertising

Thursday November 26, 2026 3:19 am PST by Tim Hardwick [https://www.macrumors.com/author/tim-hardwick/]

Apple is facing a formal probe in South Korea over allegations that its iPhone 16 advertising misled consumers about the availability of Apple Intelligence features.

Investigators at the country's Fair Trade Commission (FTC) have referred Apple and Apple Korea for review [https://www.korea.kr/briefing/pressReleaseView.do?newsId=156790945&pWise=mSub&pWiseSub=C4#pressRelease], alleging that several Apple Intelligence features were not delivered as advertised, including Genmoji, Image Playground, and Siri's ability to understand personal context and onscreen awareness.

The allegations focus on whether Apple adequately explained which features were available and when others would arrive. For example, the FTC's report notes that Siri's personal-context capabilities were advertised for Korean availability in early April 2025 but did not arrive until October 2026, approximately 18 months later.

Apple provided MacRumors with the following statement:

"At Apple, we deeply value the relationship with our customers in Korea and have always focused on delivering the best products and services in the world. Since we added Korean language support for Apple Intelligence last year, users have benefited from dozens of powerful features that are integrated across Apple's platforms. And we have continued to build on that commitment with the recent launch of Korean language support for Siri AI, an entirely new version of Siri that's more capable, conversational, and personal. We have cooperated with the KFTC's investigation and disagree with the conclusions in the Examiner's Report. We look forward to presenting a fulsome defense before the Commission."

The Commission has yet to issue a final ruling or impose a penalty.

The case follows similar complaints about Apple Intelligence advertising in the United States and other major Apple markets. In April 2025, Apple removed [https://www.macrumors.com/2025/04/22/apple-intelligence-available-now-text-removed/] its "available now" marketing claim on its Apple Intelligence page, following a National Advertising Division inquiry. The advertising watchdog found that the wording implied that all listed features were available, and that a footnote explaining the phased rollout was insufficiently clear. Apple disagreed with the findings but ultimately complied with the recommendation.

Apple also agreed to a $250 million settlement [https://www.macrumors.com/2026/05/05/apple-class-action-siri-lawsuit-settlement/] in May 2026 over a U.S. class-action lawsuit alleging it misled buyers about the availability of Apple Intelligence and Siri features when the iPhone 16 was released in September 2024.

Original article updated with Apple's statement.
