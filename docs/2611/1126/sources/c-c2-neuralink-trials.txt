Our goal

Advance brain-computer interfaces through rigorous, evidence-based research to safely increase independence and broaden access to this technology.

Find the study that fits you

Active trial

Control devices with your mind [/trials/device-control]

Investigating hands-free control of a computer and robotic arm. For people with quadriplegia from ALS, brain stem stroke, or spinal cord injury.

Learn more

Active trial

Communicate with your thoughts [/trials/speech-restoration]

Studying turning thoughts into your spoken voice from a device. For people with severe speech impairment from ALS, stroke, spinal cord injury, or other conditions.

What participation involves

We design studies to be transparent and supportive for participants and their caregivers.

Zero financial cost

Care, device, and procedure provided

Travel covered

Study travel covered for you and a caregiver

Dedicated team

Support before, during, and after the procedure

Participation is voluntary

You may withdraw from the study at any time

Costs associated with study participation are covered for eligible participants as described in the informed consent.
