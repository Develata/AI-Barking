Last modified: November 24, 2026

These Workspace API Billing Terms supplement the Google APIs Terms of Service [https://developers.google.com/terms#section_1_account_and_registration], which govern use of the Google Workspace Application Programming Interfaces, Model Context Protocol Servers, and other developer tools (collectively, the “GWS APIs”). "Google" has the meaning given at https://cloud.google.com/terms/google-entity [https://cloud.google.com/terms/google-entity].

Google reserves the right to charge fees for use of the GWS APIs (the “GWS API Fees”). 

Billing and payments for the GWS API Fees are managed through Cloud Billing [https://cloud.google.com/billing/docs/concepts] in the Google Cloud Platform.

The following sections of the Google Cloud Terms of Service [https://cloud.google.com/terms], as applicable to the Google Cloud Platform (or "GCP Services" as defined in the Google Cloud Terms of Service), will govern payments, invoicing, billing, taxes, payment disputes, and any related issues pertaining to the GWS API Fees, as if the GWS APIs were a GCP Service for purposes of those sections, and will prevail over any conflicting terms in the remainder of the Google APIs Terms of Service in relation to this subject matter:

• Section 2 (Payment Terms);

• Subsection (d) of Section 12.3 (Unlimited Liabilities);

• Section 14 (Miscellaneous);

• Section 15 (Regional Modifications), as applicable to:

• Governing Law and Dispute Resolution;

• Payment, Taxes, and Invoice Disputes;

• Currency Conversion; and

• Other Regional Modifications; and

• the Pricing and Billing Terms of the Service Specific Terms for GCP Services at https://cloud.google.com/terms/service-terms [https://cloud.google.com/terms/service-terms], including the Supplemental Paid Credit Terms at https://cloud.google.com/terms/supplemental-paid-credit [https://cloud.google.com/terms/supplemental-paid-credit] (the "Supplemental Paid Credit Terms").

The GWS API Fees are specified on the pricing page [https://developers.google.com/workspace/guides/quota-pricing] and may be changed from time to time. Any pricing changes will be effective 30 days after they are posted unless otherwise specified. Continued use of the GWS APIs constitutes consent to those changes.

For clarity, these Workspace API Billing Terms do not govern use of any GCP Service (including those listed on the Google Cloud Services Summary [https://cloud.google.com/terms/services]).
