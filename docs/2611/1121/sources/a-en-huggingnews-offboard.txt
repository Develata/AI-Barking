OpenRouter has removed three inference providers and stripped Zero Data Retention (ZDR) labels from numerous endpoints after security research revealed that some partners routed customer prompts to MiniMax in China. The company stated it has notified affected customers and is integrating the researchers' testing methodology into its ongoing security checks.

The action follows a study by HD Moore, who seeded 2 million trackable "canary" secrets into AI services to monitor for prompt theft. The research identified that providers such as AtlasCloud, Novita, and StreamLake ignored privacy flags or used logging to access data. OpenRouter co-founder Alex Atallah said the company will shift away from relying on provider self-attestation toward technical validation and continuous auditing.
