Truly Subquadratic 3SUM and Truly Subcubic APSP via Triangles in Sparse Lopsided Graphs
Get email updates [/subscribe?contribution=anthropic-3sum-apsp-formalization-2026]

Josh Alman, Virginia Vassilevska Williams

Claude’s original sparse-output thin-matrix multiplication algorithm, as presented in Section 2 and related to the certified 3SUM/APSP theorems. Human-optimized numerical parameters, new reductions, data structures and Hinted OMv extensions retain human credit.

What was already known

Prior general algorithms saved subpolynomial factors; the 3SUM and APSP hypotheses asserted no fixed polynomial improvement.

AI involvement AI-autonomous

Anthropic set Claude a problem about cryptographic constructions. The authors report that Claude instead developed the sparse matrix-product algorithm that led to the 3SUM and shortest-path speedups. They write, The session used 16M output tokens with no human input. Josh Alman and Virginia Vassilevska Williams then simplified and strengthened the algorithm and derived its data-structure version.

Source

Alman–Vassilevska Williams: algorithms and methodology [https://arxiv.org/html/2610.06783v1#Sx1]
Alman and Vassilevska Williams · Acknowledgments and Methodology · 2610.06783v1

Importance Major

Changes the fine-grained algorithmic baseline and the conditional-hardness hypotheses built on these problems.

Technical verification Formal (Lean)

A completed rigorous check is documented for the scoped statement. Anthropic / reported Lean 4 with Mathlib certification: Theorems 19 and 22 and the zero-weight case of Corollary 39, with their dependencies, as specified in the paper’s Lean-certification paragraph. Original discovery and human strengthening are separately credited.

Human understanding Explained

Named human researchers explain or reconstruct the scoped central mechanism in the cited paper/account; credit for that explanation is separate from technical checking. The paper explicitly credits Claude with discovering the core algorithm; the named authors understood, simplified, strengthened and extended it and take responsibility for the paper.
