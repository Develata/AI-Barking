The Science effort at Anthropic focuses on how AI can increase the pace of scientific progress. We work to improve our models’ core scientific capabilities and enable practitioners to use them to make scientific and technological advancements. We also aim to increase the pace of AI-driven science through partnerships, grants, and internal research in areas such as drug development and molecular biology.

Research

The scientific and discovery process consists of feedback loops: designing and conducting an experiment, interpreting the results, revising the question, and beginning again. We’re working to tighten these loops by developing tools that make researchers more efficient or help them develop entirely new approaches, and by building training environments and evaluations that help us improve Claude’s performance on scientific tasks.

AI for Science

We work with universities, research institutes, biotech companies, and academic medical centers—through direct API access, co-developed custom agents, applied AI technical support, and more—to get Claude into the hands of scientists. This allows us to incorporate feedback based on real-world evaluation and applied research across domains, demonstrating the tangible impacts AI is having on science.
