I’m an Associate Professor of Computer Science [https://www.cs.columbia.edu/] at Columbia [https://www.columbia.edu/], and a member of the Theory Group [https://theory.cs.columbia.edu/]. I work on algorithm design and complexity theory, and I especially like using algebraic tools to solve problems throughout computer science.

I was previously a Rabin Postdoc in Theoretical Computer Science [https://toc.seas.harvard.edu/] at Harvard. Before that, I completed my PhD in Computer Science [https://www.eecs.mit.edu/] at MIT, advised by Ryan Williams [http://people.csail.mit.edu/rrw/] and Virginia Vassilevska Williams [https://people.csail.mit.edu/virgi/]. I spent the first half of grad school at Stanford [http://cs.stanford.edu/] until I moved to MIT with my advisors.
