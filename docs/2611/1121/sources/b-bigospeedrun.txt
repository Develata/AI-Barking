3SUM

Given nnn integers, decide whether three distinct positions sum to zero. For each fixed kkk, inputs have magnitude at most nkn^knk. One deterministic word-RAM program per kkk must work for every word width W≥b(⌊log⁡2n⌋+1)W \ge b(\lfloor\log_2 n\rfloor+1)W≥b(⌊log2​n⌋+1), with a fixed constant bbb. Signed arithmetic, indirect memory access and branches cost one step.

Classic bound: O(n2)O(n^{2})O(n2) (Gajentaan and Overmars 1995, account of the folklore algorithm [https://people.csail.mit.edu/virgi/6.s078/papers/gajovermars.pdf]).

The bound has the form O(nα)O(n^{\alpha})O(nα). α\alphaα is the time exponent; lower is better.

What counts as Claimed: Published results for this problem without a qualifying repository review or Lean proof check.

What counts as Human Verified: This problem has no community repo with a qualifying review rule.

What counts as Lean Verified: A result shows here when we rebuild its Lean proof from source and check, with the Lean kernel, that it proves our exact problem statement. A result also shows here when prove2.me [https://prove2.me], which checks each proof with Lean on its server, proved it and we reviewed that its Lean statement is our problem statement. The submission also states, and proves in Lean outside our problem statement, that in its own cache model no choice of profile, costs, threshold and multipliers goes below 1.92027940818, so tuning it can lower the exponent by at most 0.000000000005 more. Our verifier checked only the running-time bound.
