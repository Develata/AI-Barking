Theory Colloquium

 Truly Subquadratic 3SUM and Truly Subcubic APSP via Triangles in Sparse Lopsided Graphs 

Speaker: Josh Alman (Columbia)

Associate Professor of Computer Science

Columbia University

Thursday, November 19, 2026

4:00PM - 5:15PM

Talk: 4:00pm-5:15pm

 Location: Yale Institute for Foundations of Data Science, Kline Tower 13th Floor, Room 1327, New Haven, CT 06511 [https://www.google.com/maps/dir//Kline Tower 13th Floor, Room 1327+New Haven+CT+06511/] and via Webcast: https://yale.hosted.panopto.com/Panopto/Pages/Viewer.aspx?id=ac10cd37-d5a6-48fb-afa0-b506012586ac [https://yale.hosted.panopto.com/Panopto/Pages/Viewer.aspx?id=ac10cd37-d5a6-48fb-afa0-b506012586ac] 

Abstract: I’ll talk about new faster algorithms for 3SUM, APSP, and many more problems. They all follow from one new algorithm for quickly computing certain entries of a matrix product. That algorithm was found by Anthropic using an internal model of Claude. They then contacted me and Virginia Vassilevska Williams to understand and properly write up the algorithm.

I’ll talk about the algorithm itself, what it means for fine-grained complexity, and the experience of being contacted by Anthropic and putting this paper together.
