Gemini 4 Carbon (High) is amongst the leading models in intelligence, but somewhat expensive when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also notably slow per task and very verbose. The model supports text, image, speech, and video input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

Gemini 4 Carbon (High) scores 60 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 43). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 205M tokens, which is very verbose in comparison to the median of 65.1M.

Pricing for Gemini 4 Carbon (High) is $4.00 per 1M input tokens (somewhat expensive, median: $2.00) and $20.00 per 1M output tokens (somewhat expensive, median: $10.00). On average, it costs $7.76 per task to evaluate Gemini 4 Carbon (High) on the Intelligence Index.

At 36.6 minutes per Intelligence Index task, Gemini 4 Carbon (High) is notably slow compared with the median of 12.1 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Gemini 4 Carbon

When was Gemini 4 Carbon released?

Gemini 4 Carbon was released on October 28, 2026.

Who created Gemini 4 Carbon?

Gemini 4 Carbon was created by Google.

How intelligent is Gemini 4 Carbon?

Gemini 4 Carbon has 3 effort levels: high, medium, and low. Gemini 4 Carbon scores 60 (high), 58 (medium), and 54 (low) on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Gemini 4 Carbon (High) is well above average among other models in a similar price tier (median: 43).

How fast is Gemini 4 Carbon?

Gemini 4 Carbon takes 36.6 minutes (high), 29.3 minutes (medium), and 29.8 minutes (low) per Intelligence Index task (based on Google's API; lower is better). Gemini 4 Carbon (High) is among the slowest compared to other models in a similar price tier (median: 12.1 minutes).

What is the latency of Gemini 4 Carbon?

Gemini 4 Carbon has a time to first answer token of 72.86s (high), 32.21s (medium), and 8.69s (low) (based on Google's API). Gemini 4 Carbon (High)'s time to first answer token is at the higher end compared to other models in a similar price tier (median: 12.92s).

How much does Gemini 4 Carbon cost?

Gemini 4 Carbon costs $4.00 per 1M input tokens (somewhat higher than average, median: $2.00) and $20.00 per 1M output tokens (somewhat higher than average, median: $10.00), based on Google's API.

What is Gemini 4 Carbon API pricing?

Gemini 4 Carbon costs $4.00 per 1M input tokens and $20.00 per 1M output tokens (based on Google's API). For a blended rate (7:2:1 cache hit/input/output ratio), this is $2.94 per 1M tokens. Pricing may vary by provider. Compare provider pricing [/models/gemini-4-carbon/providers]

How verbose is Gemini 4 Carbon?

When evaluated on the Intelligence Index, Gemini 4 Carbon generated 205M output tokens (high), 124M output tokens (medium), and 83.9M output tokens (low). Gemini 4 Carbon (High)'s output token count is at the higher end compared to other models in a similar price tier (median: 65.1M).

Is Gemini 4 Carbon a reasoning model?

Yes, Gemini 4 Carbon is a reasoning model. It uses extended thinking or chain-of-thought reasoning to work through complex problems before providing an answer.

What input modalities does Gemini 4 Carbon support?

Gemini 4 Carbon supports text, image, speech, and video input.

What output modalities does Gemini 4 Carbon support?

Gemini 4 Carbon supports text output.

Can Gemini 4 Carbon process images?

Yes, Gemini 4 Carbon supports image input and can analyze, describe, and answer questions about images.

Is Gemini 4 Carbon multimodal?

Yes, Gemini 4 Carbon is multimodal. It can process text, image, speech, and video input and generate text output.

What is the context window of Gemini 4 Carbon?

Gemini 4 Carbon has a context window of 1M tokens. This determines how much text and conversation history the model can process in a single request.

Is Gemini 4 Carbon open source?

No, Gemini 4 Carbon is proprietary. The model weights are not publicly available.

How many parameters does Gemini 4 Carbon have?

Gemini 4 Carbon is a proprietary model and Google has not disclosed the model size or parameter count.

How does Gemini 4 Carbon perform on benchmarks?

Gemini 4 Carbon achieves a score of 60 (high), 58 (medium), and 54 (low) on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. This composite benchmark evaluates models across knowledge work, coding, science, and general knowledge.

Is Gemini 4 Carbon available via API?

Yes, Gemini 4 Carbon is available via API through 1 provider. Compare API providers [/models/gemini-4-carbon/providers]

Where can I use Gemini 4 Carbon?

Gemini 4 Carbon is available through 1 API provider. Compare providers [/models/gemini-4-carbon/providers]
