September 2026 Web Server Survey

Published September 30, 2026

Published September 30, 2026

Netcraft Editorial Team

Protector of Brands | Disrupter of Phishing, Fraud, and Scams

 [../authors/netcraft-editorial-team]

In the September 2026 survey we received responses from 1,470,435,351 sites across 323,147,961 domains and 15,463,120 web-facing computers. This reflects an increase of 29.8 million sites, 11.3 million domains, and 208,114 web-facing computers. 

nginx experienced the largest gain of 12.5 million sites (+5.87%) this month, and now accounts for 15.3% (+0.55pp) of sites seen by Netcraft. OpenResty made the next largest gain of 5.8 million sites (+4.56%).

Google experienced the largest loss of 19.1 million sites (-23.56%) this month, reducing its market share to 4.23% (-1.42pp). Apache suffered the next largest loss, down by 1.2 million sites (-0.69%).
