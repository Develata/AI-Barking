Gemini 4 Neon (Xhigh) is amongst the leading models in intelligence, but somewhat expensive when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also notably slow per task and very verbose. The model supports text, image, speech, and video input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

Gemini 4 Neon (Xhigh) scores 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 43). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 283M tokens, which is very verbose in comparison to the median of 65.1M.

Pricing for Gemini 4 Neon (Xhigh) is $3.00 per 1M input tokens (somewhat expensive, median: $2.00) and $15.00 per 1M output tokens (somewhat expensive, median: $10.00). On average, it costs $6.44 per task to evaluate Gemini 4 Neon (Xhigh) on the Intelligence Index.

At 21.5 minutes per Intelligence Index task, Gemini 4 Neon (Xhigh) is notably slow compared with the median of 12.1 minutes for comparable models (lower is better).

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Gemini 4 Neon

When was Gemini 4 Neon released?

Gemini 4 Neon was released on November 18, 2026.

Who created Gemini 4 Neon?

Gemini 4 Neon was created by Google.

How intelligent is Gemini 4 Neon?

Gemini 4 Neon has 4 effort levels: xhigh, high, medium, and low. Gemini 4 Neon scores 61 (xhigh), 60 (high), 55 (medium), and 44 (low) on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Gemini 4 Neon (Xhigh) is well above average among other models in a similar price tier (median: 43).

How fast is Gemini 4 Neon?

Gemini 4 Neon takes 21.5 minutes (xhigh), 14.8 minutes (high), 8.3 minutes (medium), and 3.7 minutes (low) per Intelligence Index task (based on Google's API; lower is better). Gemini 4 Neon (Xhigh) is among the slowest compared to other models in a similar price tier (median: 12.1 minutes).

What is the latency of Gemini 4 Neon?

Gemini 4 Neon has a time to first answer token of 56.51s (xhigh), 40.64s (high), 20.14s (medium), and 13.93s (low) (based on Google's API). Gemini 4 Neon (Xhigh)'s time to first answer token is at the higher end compared to other models in a similar price tier (median: 12.92s).

How much does Gemini 4 Neon cost?

Gemini 4 Neon costs $3.00 per 1M input tokens (somewhat higher than average, median: $2.00) and $15.00 per 1M output tokens (somewhat higher than average, median: $10.00), based on Google's API.

What is Gemini 4 Neon API pricing?

Gemini 4 Neon costs $3.00 per 1M input tokens and $15.00 per 1M output tokens (based on Google's API). For a blended rate (7:2:1 cache hit/input/output ratio), this is $2.21 per 1M tokens. Pricing may vary by provider. Compare provider pricing [/models/gemini-4-neon/providers]

How verbose is Gemini 4 Neon?

When evaluated on the Intelligence Index, Gemini 4 Neon generated 283M output tokens (xhigh), 123M output tokens (high), 62.9M output tokens (medium), and 28.3M output tokens (low). Gemini 4 Neon (Xhigh)'s output token count is at the higher end compared to other models in a similar price tier (median: 65.1M).

Is Gemini 4 Neon a reasoning model?

Yes, Gemini 4 Neon is a reasoning model. It uses extended thinking or chain-of-thought reasoning to work through complex problems before providing an answer.

What input modalities does Gemini 4 Neon support?

Gemini 4 Neon supports text, image, speech, and video input.

What output modalities does Gemini 4 Neon support?

Gemini 4 Neon supports text output.

Can Gemini 4 Neon process images?

Yes, Gemini 4 Neon supports image input and can analyze, describe, and answer questions about images.

Is Gemini 4 Neon multimodal?

Yes, Gemini 4 Neon is multimodal. It can process text, image, speech, and video input and generate text output.

What is the context window of Gemini 4 Neon?

Gemini 4 Neon has a context window of 1M tokens. This determines how much text and conversation history the model can process in a single request.

Is Gemini 4 Neon open source?

No, Gemini 4 Neon is proprietary. The model weights are not publicly available.

How many parameters does Gemini 4 Neon have?

Gemini 4 Neon is a proprietary model and Google has not disclosed the model size or parameter count.

How does Gemini 4 Neon perform on benchmarks?

Gemini 4 Neon achieves a score of 61 (xhigh), 60 (high), 55 (medium), and 44 (low) on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. This composite benchmark evaluates models across knowledge work, coding, science, and general knowledge.

Is Gemini 4 Neon available via API?

Yes, Gemini 4 Neon is available via API through 1 provider. Compare API providers [/models/gemini-4-neon/providers]

Where can I use Gemini 4 Neon?

Gemini 4 Neon is available through 1 API provider. Compare providers [/models/gemini-4-neon/providers]
