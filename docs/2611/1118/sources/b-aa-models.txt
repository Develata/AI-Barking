Active ParametersPassive Parameters

Total Parameters

The total number of trainable weights and biases in the model, expressed in billions. These parameters are learned during training and determine the model's ability to process and generate responses.

Active Parameters at Inference Time

The number of parameters actually executed during each inference forward pass, expressed in billions. For Mixture of Experts (MoE) models, a routing mechanism selects a subset of experts per token, resulting in fewer active than total parameters. Dense models use all parameters, so active equals total.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the most intelligent AI model?

Claude Fable 5.2 (Max, Default Fallback) currently leads the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with a score of 65, out of 125 models evaluated.

What are the top AI models?

The top AI models by Intelligence Index are: 1. Claude Fable 5.2 (Max, Default Fallback) (65), 2. Claude Fable 5.2 (Xhigh, Default Fallback) (62), 3. GPT-6.2 Astra (Max) (62), 4. Claude Fable 5.2 (High, Default Fallback) (62), and 5. GPT-6.2 Astra (Xhigh) (62).

Which is the fastest AI model?

GPT-6 Luna (Low) is the fastest, with the lowest time per task at 0.6 minutes per Intelligence Index task, followed by GPT-6.1 Terra (Low) (1.3 min) and Gemini 4 Hydrogen (Low) (1.5 min). Lower is better.

Which is the cheapest AI model?

JoyAI-VL-Preview is the most affordable at $0.00 per 1M tokens (blended), followed by K2 Horizon 375B A23B ($0.00) and Command A+ ($0.00).

Which AI model has the lowest latency?

Tiny Aya Global has the lowest time to first answer token at 0.19s, followed by Phi-4 Multimodal Instruct (0.62s) and NVIDIA Nemotron Nano 9B V2 (Reasoning) (0.74s).

Which is the best open weights AI model?

Kimi K3.1 Pro is the highest-ranked open weights model with an Intelligence Index score of 46. There are 54 open weights models out of 125 total evaluated.

What are the top open weights AI models?

The top open weights AI models by Intelligence Index are: 1. Kimi K3.1 Pro (46), 2. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (Max) (33), and 3. GLM-5.3 (Max) (32).

Which is the best reasoning model?

Claude Fable 5.2 (Max, Default Fallback) leads among 120 reasoning models with an Intelligence Index score of 65. Reasoning models use extended thinking to work through complex problems before providing answers.

How are AI models compared on Artificial Analysis?

Models are compared across multiple dimensions including intelligence (quality), pricing, time per task (minutes per Intelligence Index task, from end-to-end output speed), latency (time to first answer token), and context window size. Performance metrics are measured directly using standardized prompts across 287 models.
