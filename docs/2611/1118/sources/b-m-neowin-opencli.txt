Google launches Gemini 4 Neon, claims it's three times faster than Carbon

Google's new Gemini 4 Neon model is here, promises faster performance and lower cost per task than Carbon, despite currently costing more per token.

Ivan Jenic @ivan_jenic · Nov 18, 2026 20:02 EST
0

Image: Google

After a few months of laying low while its competitors dominated the AI landscape throughout the summer, Google is now in counterattack mode. Less than a month ago, Google released its new flagship model Gemini 4 Carbon, followed by budget-friendly Gemini 4 Hydrogen. And now, Google is covering the middle ground with the release of its latest model, Gemini 4 Neon.

According to Google, Neon is primarily designed for complex coding tasks, analytical reasoning, and multi-step workflows. Interestingly, even though Carbon is still supposed to be Google's strongest model to date, the company has shared some benchmarks in which Neon exceeded Carbon's performance on certain tasks. Google also claims that Neon completes tasks three times faster at 60% of the cost of Carbon. These are, of course, Google's own benchmarks, and we have yet to see how the model performs outside internal tests.

Neon scores 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index and 65.4% on CursorBench 4.1, with Google reporting that it's "competitive with the leading models in its class." Neon is multimodal, as it supports text, images, audio, and video. It also has a context window of up to one million tokens.

Here are Google's official benchmarks of Gemini 4 Neon:

Image: Google

Neon pricing is where it gets interesting. Google charges $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens through the API. At the moment, that's actually more expensive than Carbon's current introductory pricing of $2 and $10, respectively. That's because Google is offering Carbon at half price until November 30. So, at least until the promotional period ends, it now costs less to run a nominally stronger model. However, raw numbers may not paint the full picture, especially with Google's claims about Neon being more cost-efficient than Carbon when it comes to completing tasks.

Gemini 4 Neon is available to Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in the Gemini app, while developers can access it through the paid API. It's also available through Google Antigravity and Google's business offerings.
