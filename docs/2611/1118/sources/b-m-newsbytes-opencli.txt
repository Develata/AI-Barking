Google launches Gemini 4 Neon: It's 3x faster, 60% cheaper
By
Akash Pandey
Nov 19, 2026
11:19 am
What's the story

Google has launched its latest AI model, Gemini 4 Neon.
The new model is designed to deliver advanced reasoning at a faster pace and lower cost.
It will be available across multiple platforms including the Gemini app, Google AI Studio, Gemini for Business, and Google Antigravity.
It will be accessible to paid subscribers of Google AI Pro and Google AI Ultra, as well as developers via the Gemini API in Google AI Studio and enterprise customers via Gemini for Business.
