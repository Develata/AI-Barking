 Google’s Gemini 4 Carbon is now available, and it’s built for your toughest tasks 

 Carbon can uncover security flaws and spot overlooked drug toxicity risks, tackling tasks that demand deeper reasoning. 

 By
 Rachit Agarwal [https://www.digitaltrends.com/users/rachit/] 

 Published November 10, 2026 9:41 AM 

 [https://www.google.com/preferences/source?q=https://www.digitaltrends.com]

Two weeks ago, Google [https://www.digitaltrends.com/topic/google/] gave Gemini 4 Carbon [https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/googles-new-ai-model-gemini-4-carbon-is-built-for-complex-coding-and-cybersecurity-tasks/] to a small group of cybersecurity defenders through its Fairwind Program. Now, Carbon is available [https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/] to developers, businesses, scientists, and Google AI Ultra subscribers.

Gemini Argon [https://www.digitaltrends.com/computing/gemini-4-argon-is-finally-available-for-developers-businesses-and-paid-users/] remains Google’s go-to pick for most coding and office work. Carbon is meant for jobs that push a model to its limits, like complicated debugging or open-ended scientific research. It uses more tokens than Argon, and it spends that extra effort exploring options and verifying its answers.
