Key findings

01
Medium already sets a new high

At medium, Gemini 4 Carbon passes 348 of 657 tasks (53.0%), above Gemini 4 Argon's best of 51.3%, for 3% less per task than Argon at high. High reaches 354, for 53.9%.

02
The jump is from low to medium

Moving from low to medium adds 21.5 points, from 31.5% to 53.0%. Moving from medium to high adds 0.9.

03
Its widest domain lead is Finance

Taking each domain at its best effort, Gemini 4 Carbon reaches 60.0% in Finance, 10.0 points above Claude Sonnet 5.5's 50.0%, the best other result. It ties Gemini 4 Argon in Operations at 68.0% and trails it in Sales (56.4% vs. 58.1%) and HR (35.0% vs. 39.2%).

04
Less thinking, more steps

At low effort, Gemini 4 Carbon averages 20.5 steps per task and 39 tasks hit the 50-step cap. At high, steps fall to 16.6 and 3 tasks hit the cap, while reasoning tokens grow 10.8×.

07
Methodology

AutomationBench tests AI agents on end-to-end workflow execution using 47 simulated apps across six business functions: Sales, Marketing, Operations, Support, Finance and HR. Each task boots a small simulated company, hands the agent one request a real operations person would get, then grades the world it leaves behind with deterministic assertions.

01
Boot a tiny company

CRM records, inbox threads, spreadsheets and support cases, including traps like stale rows and near-duplicate names.

02
The agent works alone

The agent gets one trigger message and tools to search, read, update and send. It can't ask clarifying questions, and there's no human in the loop.

03
Grade the end state

Deterministic assertions check what changed. The task passes only if every one holds.
