In true google fashion the situation is a total mess.
The gist of it is that there are many different "Pro" plans, and the model access/quota depends on things like whether or not you are a workspace "Pro" user, whether or not you are a discount/promo "Pro" user, whether or not you are a 3rd party "Pro" user, and then also beyond all that what country you are in. Common examples of these gimped "pro" plans are Pixel "pro" plans that came with pixel purchases, and Jio "Pro" plans which are discounted plans available through a telecom provider popular in India.
But if you have a normal Google account, and pay the full $20/mo, and live in a country that doesn't have tight regulations, you'll get the proper Pro account.
