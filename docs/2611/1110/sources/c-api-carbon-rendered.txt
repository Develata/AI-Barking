Gemini 4 Carbon

 content_copy check Copy page

Gemini 4 Carbon (gemini-4-carbon) is our most capable reasoning model, built for complex software engineering, mathematical and scientific problem solving, high factual accuracy, autonomous multi-step agents, and long decode up to 1M tokens.

Try in Google AI Studio [/prompts/new_chat?model=gemini-4-carbon]
