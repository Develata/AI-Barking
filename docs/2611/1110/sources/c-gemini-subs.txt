Frequently asked questions

What is Gemini Enterprise and what does it do?

Gemini Enterprise [https://cloud.google.com/gemini-enterprise/?utm_source=gemini.google&utm_medium=et&utm_campaign=gemini-app-website-subscriptions&utm_content=business-faq] refers to the editions of Gemini that are built for business use. These editions work across your business apps, email, docs, code, and data to generate content, produce insights, and automate workflows grounded in your business context.

What are the differences between Gemini Enterprise editions?

Gemini Enterprise offers multiple editions to fit the unique needs of your organization.
Business edition: Great for small businesses and teams up to 300—no IT setup needed.
Standard and Plus editions: Designed for organizations that need stricter security and compliance requirements. Includes higher quota, unlimited seats, and access to third-party agents.

Who owns the data and does Google use my data for training models?

Gemini Enterprise Business, Standard and Plus editions are built on Google’s secure-by-design infrastructure, giving you the peace of mind to deploy AI agents across your organization confidently.
