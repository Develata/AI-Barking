Introducing Claude Whiteboards, Claude Sheets, and Claude Data

Now you can brainstorm on whiteboards, build sheets, and share data directly in Claude.

CategoryProduct updates [/resources/product-updates]

ProductClaude apps

Getting started [#getting-started]

Today we’re launching Claude Whiteboards and Claude Sheets in beta, and Claude Dashboards (opens in new tab) [https://claude.com/resources/articles/dashboards-and-motion] becomes generally available as Claude Data. Whiteboards is a shared board where your team brainstorms with Claude, Sheets is a spreadsheet Claude builds and edits with you, and Claude Data adds data stories, certified queries, and live sharing. These are available on all paid plans.

Working with Claude on a project often meant moving the output over to another tool or program, where Claude didn’t have live access to follow progress and changes. Now, your documents, boards, sheets, and dashboards can stay in Claude, with access for your team to continue editing together. When ready, you can bring the outputs directly into tools from our partners and continue working there.

Claude Whiteboards

Many people use Claude as a thought partner, but brainstorming with it has typically meant copy-pasting the results elsewhere so you can then start brainstorming or collaborating with your coworkers. Now, you can talk through a messy plan out loud with Claude, and Claude can turn it into a board with a timeline with owners and dates, so you and your team can collaborate in real time. In a retro meeting, for example, everyone can add their notes, and Claude can group them by theme and draft the action items for each person. You can focus on any part of the board by dragging a box around it and asking Claude questions. When you’re done brainstorming, Claude can turn the board into a doc or deck, or bring it into tools like Lucid, Miro, or Vani by Zoho, where you can continue working on it with your team.

Claude Sheets

Claude can now generate spreadsheets with tabs, formulas, formatting, and drop-downs, often from just a prompt like asking for a campaign tracker or a hiring budget. It can edit in the cells, outlining each new edit so you can track changes. You can share sheets with your team, so you and Claude can work on them together.

For now, sheets have simple spreadsheet functionality, but we are working on adding more. When you need the full functionality of Excel, you can export your file to .xlsx or CSV, or use Claude for Excel (opens in new tab) [https://claude.com/claude-for-microsoft-365]. You can also continue working in partner tools including Airtable, Asana, monday.com, and Smartsheet.

Claude Data

Claude Dashboards (opens in new tab) [https://claude.com/resources/articles/dashboards-and-motion] launched in beta in October, enabling teams to ask quick questions of their company’s data in tools like Amazon Redshift, BigQuery, and ClickHouse. Starting today, it’s generally available as Claude Data. We changed the name because the way companies use their data is broader than dashboards, and the product now covers how they work more broadly. Certified queries and live sharing are in beta. Here are some ways teams can use Claude Data:

Create certified definitions of metrics. Your data team can certify the queries everyone should use, so you don’t end up building or working on dashboards with conflicting data. Charts built on these metrics carry a "certified" badge showing they’re certified.

Craft stories from data. You can work with Claude in Claude Data to draft data stories or narrated videos from your metrics, for you to review before sharing.

Share current numbers with everyone. With live sharing, your colleagues can see up-to-date numbers when they open a shared dashboard, even if they don’t have access to the connectors that keep that data current. Viewers see the results of the queries you’ve approved, and can’t run others with your access.

Match your look-and-feel. Design systems are now supported in Claude Data.

Get faster loading with caching. Previously, every dashboard would reload on every load. Now, dashboards support caching, and refresh cadences can be set on a per-dashboard basis.

Track changes over time. Claude Data now has tracked changes, so you can see who has made edits and roll back to a specific version if needed.

Our partners for Claude Data include Amplitude, Mixpanel, Omni, PostHog, and Sigma.

Work in your other tools, too

Starting today, interactive connectors (opens in new tab) [https://claude.com/resources/articles/interactive-connectors-full-tool] can open as full apps beside your conversation or in full screen, so you can work in tools like Adobe, Atlassian, Box, and Notion yourself, or ask Claude to make changes with you.

Getting started

Claude Whiteboards, Sheets, and Data work on web, desktop, and mobile. Claude Data needs a data connector, such as a warehouse or CRM.

For admins: On Team plans, all three are on by default. On Enterprise plans, Claude Whiteboards and Sheets are off by default, and Claude Data turns on by default on November 19, 2026. You can change these anytime in Organization settings > Artifacts.
