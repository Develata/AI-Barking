Finite-Time Singularity Formation for the Three-Dimensional Euler Equations
 [#finite-time-singularity-formation-for-the-three-dimensional-euler-equations]

Levent Alpöge, Tristan Buckmaster and Matei P. Coiculescu

This repository contains the manuscript, its auxiliary note on terminal smoothness, and a Lean 4
formalization of the main theorem.

Manuscript
 [#manuscript]

manuscript/paper.pdf [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/manuscript/paper.pdf] is the version submitted to arXiv.
manuscript/source/ [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/manuscript/source] is the arXiv source package: the TeX, the figures, and the
auxiliary note in anc/auxiliary/ [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/manuscript/source/anc/auxiliary] (TeX and PDF).

Lean formalization
 [#lean-formalization]

formalization/ [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/formalization] is a Lean 4 formalization by the authors of the arXiv version of
the manuscript, in one-to-one correspondence with its proof. Every numbered result, and every
display that the proof of a later result cites, is a Lean theorem TorusEuler.Paper.<result>
whose proof takes exactly the results cited by its TeX proof as hypotheses and uses no other
result's proof. The ledger spine.json [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/formalization/spine.json] records these citations, and
VerifySpine.lean [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/formalization/lean/VerifySpine.lean] checks the correspondence;
paper-coverage-latest.md [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/formalization/paper-coverage-latest.md] lists the results with their
Lean names. TorusEuler.Paper.Statement.theorem_1_1 states Theorem 1.1,
TorusEuler.Paper.theorem_1_1 proves it, and TorusEuler.theorem11 in
MainTheorem.lean [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/formalization/lean/TorusEuler/MainTheorem.lean] is its existence part. These
theorems depend only on the axioms propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound, and the
sources contain no sorry. The project uses Lean v4.34.0-rc2 and the mathlib revision fixed in
lake-manifest.json [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/formalization/lean/lake-manifest.json]; build and check instructions are in
formalization/README.md [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/formalization/README.md].

Exceptions to the correspondence:

The auxiliary note, unchanged since 18 November 2026 except for the title of the main paper,
is formalized by the theorems auxiliaryTerminalSmoothness, auxiliaryLemma12 and
auxiliaryLemma13, which are not part of the correspondence. Remark 1.2 is
proved from auxiliaryTerminalSmoothness.

Corollary 8.3 cites the periodic Beale–Kato–Majda criterion, which is not formalized. Its Lean
proof obtains the divergence of the curl integral from the curl lower bound in the proof of
Proposition 8.2.

Where the ledger departs from the literal citations of the TeX (implicit citations, displays used
by later proofs, generic facts treated as library results), the decision and its justification
are listed in ledger-decisions.md [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/formalization/ledger-decisions.md].

The formalization also contains an earlier direct proof of Theorem 1.1, TorusEuler.theorem11_library
in Theorem11.lean [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/formalization/lean/TorusEuler/Theorem11.lean], which follows the frozen version
of the manuscript of 18 November 2026 (below). VerifyPaper.lean checks its correspondence with the
numbered results of that version (paper-coverage.md [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/formalization/paper-coverage.md]) against a
copy of its TeX in formalization/frozen/ [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/formalization/frozen].

In this copy, the notes on the development workflow were removed from formalization/README.md,
and comments give the path of adapted files in sources/ccpoint-lean/ [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/sources/ccpoint-lean]. Some
declarations are adapted from an earlier Lean development distributed under the Apache License 2.0;
its license [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/sources/ccpoint-lean/LICENSE] and notice [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/sources/ccpoint-lean/NOTICE] are included.

Frozen manuscript
 [#frozen-manuscript]

frozen-manuscript/ [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/frozen-manuscript] is the manuscript and the auxiliary note as frozen on
18 November 2026, the version that TorusEuler.theorem11_library follows:
paper.pdf [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/frozen-manuscript/paper.pdf],
auxiliary/terminal_smoothness.pdf [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/frozen-manuscript/auxiliary/terminal_smoothness.pdf] and the
TeX source. For packaging, the figure paths in the TeX point to
frozen-manuscript/figures/ [/mcoiculescu/EulerBlowupPublic/blob/main/frozen-manuscript/figures], which holds the figures of the arXiv
package; the scripts that generate them are the same for both versions. The arXiv version differs
from the frozen one by a revision of Section 2 (scaled components, the reference angle and a
separate coupling lemma, which changes the numbering in Section 2) and by edits to the
introduction.
