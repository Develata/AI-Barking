 Hurried Announcement: Alessandro Masullo GRH 

 Posted by ravivakil under Uncategorized [https://math216.wordpress.com/category/uncategorized/] 

 Leave a Comment [https://math216.wordpress.com/2026/11/20/hurried-announcement-alessandro-masullo-grh/#respond] 

Hi everyone, 

I am at the American Mathematical Society Executive Committee and Board of Trustees meeting now, and about to chair an hours-long meeting, but this is urgent, so I will try to get this out quickly. Alessandro Maria Masullo has a proposed proof of GRH, and Brian Conrad has been looking closely at it for months and gave extensive feedback which is being incorporated, and it is so far looking good to him. It is all human, with no AI. The hope was to have Brian work with Alessandro for a year to polish the writing and then announce it after running it by enough people. Now it seems best to put out the current draft. I hope people quickly check it, formalize, fill any small gaps if they occur. I really hope it works. I have great faith in Brian’s judgement, and Brian has put a lot of thought into checking what Alessandro has done. There is a long story to tell, but right now I want to get the file out. Here it is.

Update about 8 hours later: I’m out of the meetings, and now catching up! Several people have contacted me to report problems in the proposed proof, identified with the assistance of different AI models. (Thanks to several mathematicians in particular who were first to let me know.) As is usual, all issues need to be resolved to the satisfaction of the community before this proof would be accepted as true. (I make no claim as to the correctness of the proof strategy.)
