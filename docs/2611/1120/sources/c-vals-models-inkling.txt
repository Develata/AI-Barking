 Nov 20, 2026 
 Thinking Machines' Inkling 2 Mini ties Claude Opus 5.5 (CVP) on CyberBench 

Thinking Machines’ new open-weight Inkling 2 Mini [/models/thinkingmachines_inkling-2-mini] ties Claude Opus 5.5 (CVP) [/models/anthropic_claude-opus-5-5-range] on CyberBench [/benchmarks/cyber] at 74.58% (#10 of 49), at $0.93 per test versus $4.17. It’s small enough to run on a Mac laptop or DGX Spark.

On the Vals Index [/benchmarks/vals_index], Mini scores 46.46%, up 17.5 points from the original Inkling [/models/thinkingmachines_inkling]. It improves on the original across 13 of 18 shared benchmarks.

On Vibe Code Bench [/benchmarks/vibe-code], it scores 77.92%, up from the original Inkling’s 19.21%. It scores 56.54% on Finance Agent v2.1 [/benchmarks/fabv2-1] at about 5 minutes per task, and 37.92% on SWE-bench Multimodal [/benchmarks/swebench-multimodal].

Mini reaches 96% of Inkling 2 Small [/models/thinkingmachines_inkling-2-small]‘s Vibe Code Bench score (77.92% versus 81.55%) and 97% of its SAGE [/benchmarks/sage] score (85.89% versus 88.57%).

Thinking Machines says Mini has 26B parameters, with 5.4B active on each token, and twelve of its 24 layers run three times on each token, using more computation per token instead of more tokens. It has a 1M-token context window and supports 1M output tokens. The weights are available on Hugging Face and Ollama under Apache 2.0.

Congrats to the Thinking Machines team on the release!
