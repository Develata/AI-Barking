Treasurer 

She/Her

Google

Meghan Day is a software engineer at Google based in the Pittsburgh office, where she has worked in the Shopping and Ads orgs since 2018. She joined the Alphabet Workers Union in 2020 and has been actively organizing with the Pennsylvania chapter since 2021. Outside of work, she plays roller derby, gardens, and enjoys spooky or nature-themed board games. 

Parul Koul

President

She/Her

Google

Parul is a software engineer at Google, based in Boston, MA. She has been involved in organizing at Alphabet since 2019 and is a founding member of the Alphabet Workers Union. She is passionate about tech and game worker organizing and has served as AWU’s President since 2021.

Emma Jackson

Communications Chair

Any Pronouns

Google

Emma Jackson joined Google’s UK office in 2004 and now works as a Senior Staff Program Manager in Accessibility at the company’s San Francisco office. She has dedicated much of her tenure to building worker solidarity within Google. Originally hailing from Liverpool, England, her early professional life was diverse - including work as a hotel maid, an au pair, and for a super yacht company - experiences that have instilled a deep appreciation for worker rights and advocacy. She currently lives in San Francisco with her dog.

Alexis Villalobos

Organizer

He/Him

alexis.villalobos@alphabetworkersunion.org [mailto:alexis.villalobos@alphabetworkersunion.org]

Before organizing, Alexis was a paralegal at various workers’ rights firms and worked on cases of discrimination, wrongful termination, contractor misclassification, and more. Since then, Alexis has organized SF janitors at SEIU Local 87, led grassroots electoral campaigns, and co-founded a community organization focused on building people power in his Bay Area home town. When he isn’t organizing, he’s singing karaoke and begging friends to try his cooking.

Carmen Estefani

Organizer

She/They

carmen.estefani@alphabetworkersunion.org [mailto:carmen.estefani@alphabetworkersunion.org]

Carmen started in the labor movement in 2013 when she got her first union job at the OAK airport. She went from a rank-and-file member to steward to Organizer. Since then, she has worked with Tech cafeteria workers at Intel, Google, Facebook and hotel workers from around the Bay. Her most recent work was helping organize and win recognition at the Moxy Hotel in Oakland, it was the first hotel to win NLRB certification in Oakland in over 20 years! When Carmen is not organizing, you can find her and her family at a picket line or march supporting workers across the Bay.
