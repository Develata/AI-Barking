About Us [/about-us]

What We Do

We are building worker power at Alphabet.

Protecting Our Jobs

We demand that Alphabet stop all layoffs and give workers a voice in re-orgs, that affect their lives and careers.

Fair pay for all

No one working at Alphabet should be struggling to make ends meet. Every worker is entitled to dignified wages, benefits and working conditions.

Flexible Work Conditions

Alphabet workers are professionals and should be treated as such. We demand a voice in where and how we get our work done with remote and hybrid options.

Protecting privacy rights

Alphabet must defend the privacy of users who are searching for ways to exercise their bodily autonomy.

Racial & economic justice

Alphabet should be transparent about their efforts to hire and retain diverse employees and commit to equitable pay and benefits for all.
