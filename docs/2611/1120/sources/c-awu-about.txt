About us

Google’s Motto used to be “Don’t Be Evil” – we are working to make sure they live up to that AND more.

Our Mission

We are reclaiming our power to make Alphabet a better workplace for all workers

We are a union of Alphabet workers in the U.S. and Canada, including full-time employees, temps, vendors, and contractors. We are deeply committed to our shared mission to organize the world’s information and make it universally accessible and useful. We believe that organizing our workplace to promote the job security and welfare of all Alphabet workers is essential to achieving this mission.

‍

Alphabet works best when we, the workers who make it run, are heard. We believe an organized workforce with a strong, democratic voice will ensure decisions are made in a way that benefits everyone.

Every Alphabet worker plays a crucial role in the success of our products and business. We believe that by coming together and reclaiming our collective power, we can win positive changes that benefit all Alphabet workers.

Our strength comes from our solidarity regardless of employment classification. Each of us contributes to Alphabet’s success, and together we can ensure that every voice is heard and every worker is valued.

The power to shape the future lies in the hands of those who build it. We have an unprecedented opportunity to make a difference—both for Alphabet and the broader world—and to create a future that works for everyone.

‍

To learn more about what we’re working on, see our current campaigns [https://www.alphabetworkersunion.org/our-campaigns] and then join us [https://www.alphabetworkersunion.org/join] to get involved.
