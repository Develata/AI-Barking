Finite-time blow-up for the Navier–Stokes equations
 [#finite-time-blow-up-for-the-navierstokes-equations]

This repository holds the paper Finite-time blow-up for the Navier–Stokes equations by L. Alpöge and
T. Buckmaster and the Lean 4 formalization that accompanies it.

Contents
 [#contents]

paper/finite-time-blow-up-navier-stokes.pdf: the paper (79 pages, version of 20 November 2026).

lean/: the Lean package (Lean v4.33.1 with Mathlib, at the revision pinned in lean/lakefile.toml).

lean/README.md explains how the formal statements relate to Theorem 1.1, Theorem 7.1, Corollary 7.2
and Appendix B of the paper, and where the formal proofs differ from the proofs of the paper.

lean/NOTICE says in words what each statement file contains and what is proved.

The Lean package
 [#the-lean-package]

The statement files at the top of lean/ give the definitions and state the theorems with sorry in
place of their proofs; Solution.lean proves them from the library lean/Breakdown/. Each
comparator*.json pairs a statement file with Solution.lean, names the theorems whose statements must agree,
and permits only the axioms propext, Quot.sound and Classical.choice.

To build, from lean/:

lake exe cache get

lake build

The library is large (about 2,270 files and 600,000 lines), and a full build needs a great deal of memory and
time.

What is and is not here
 [#what-is-and-is-not-here]

The formalization contains no data. The certificate whose hash the paper gives is not in this repository; the
paper says that it will be released. That the certificate satisfies the conditions of Appendix B is verified by
a rigorous computation, involving interval arithmetic (128 bits of precision, with the Arb library through
FLINT), which is not part of the formalization and is not in this repository either.

The source of the paper is not in this repository.

State of the Lean text
 [#state-of-the-lean-text]

The package is the one of the paper's working repository, with one patch of 20 November 2026 applied to the
cell-layer statements and to the library proofs that depend on them: the weight of one bound becomes
$(|m|+\tfrac12)^2$ in place of $m^2+\tfrac14$, one constant of a cell may be zero, and the set of the harmonics
of the model in a cell is restricted to those that do not vanish there. Appendix B of the paper states the
conditions in this patched form. The patched package was not rebuilt when this repository was assembled.
