I have big news: Conductor is joining Meta.

We launched Conductor a lifetime ago, in the distant past of July 2025. Writing most of your code with agents (let alone a team of coding agents) felt crazy back then. You trusted us to come along for the ride, and we cannot thank you enough!

We have some big, crazy plans for the next interface. If you liked Conductor, you’re gonna love what’s coming. We’ll have something new for you to use early next year.

Conductor will keep working as normal till then, except for Conductor Cloud, which will shut down over the next few weeks. We’ve built an easy way to transfer Conductor Cloud workspaces to local, and we’re around to help make the transition as easy as possible. Timeline

Wednesday, Nov 4

No new sign-ups, free for existing users

New users can no longer sign up for Conductor or download the app. Existing free users can’t upgrade to a pro plan, but pro users can keep using pro features for free.

Friday, Nov 13

Conductor Cloud goes read-only

Conductor Cloud workspaces go read-only. You can still read old chats and export data, but you can’t send new messages. Active subscribers will get a refund.

Friday, Nov 20

Conductor Cloud shuts down

We begin deleting Conductor Cloud user data. Please export your data before this date.

Local Conductor will continue to receive new models and critical bug fixes until early 2027.

We hope to have something new you can use by then!

FAQ

When will I have to stop using Conductor?+

Conductor Cloud will become read-only on Nov 13, and will fully shut down on Nov 20.

We’ve built an easy way to transfer your cloud workspaces to local. To move a cloud workspace and its chats to your Mac, right-click it and choose Transfer to local. To download all your data, go to Settings → Account → Export data.

Local Conductor will continue working as normal for now. It will start winding down in early 2027. We hope to have something new you can use by then!

Will Conductor still get updates?+

Yes, Conductor will still get new models and critical bug fixes until early 2027.

How can I export my data?+

You can transfer a cloud workspace to local by right-clicking the workspace and selecting Transfer to local.

To export an archive of all your Conductor data, go to Settings → Account → Export data.

What happens to my data?+

We have never sold or trained on Conductor user data, and we still won’t. We’ll delete all Conductor user data when the app shuts down in early 2027. Check our privacy policy here [/privacy].

I just subscribed to Conductor. Can I get a refund?+

Yes, active subscribers will get a refund.

What are you going to be building?+

We can’t share much yet. But if you liked Conductor, you’re gonna like what’s next.
