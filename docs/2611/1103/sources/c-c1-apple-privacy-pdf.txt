                                        Introduction
                                        Audio Intelligence brings a new class of Apple Intelligence capabilities to Apple
                                        Watch. It is designed to help you stay present and engaged, catch details in a
                                        conversation you may have missed, identify music playing nearby, and be aware
                                        of important sounds around you, all while protecting your privacy and the privacy
                                        of those around you.

                                        Audio Intelligence on Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 relies on
                                        the tight integration of hardware and software with the latest AI models to
                                        provide four new features.

                                                   Siri Recap
                                                   Siri Recap creates high-level Apple Intelligence-generated summaries of
                                                   your conversations throughout your day so you can stay present and
                                                   engaged in the moment.

                                                   Live Rewind
                                                   Live Rewind helps you recall what was just said in conversation. A double
                                                   press of the Digital Crown shows you the previous 15 seconds of a
                                                   conversation as text, so you can catch something you may have missed
                                                   or are less familiar with.

                                                   Music Recognition with Shazam
                                                   Shazam instantly detects the music playing around you and automatically
                                                   displays the song title and artist s name on the Music Recognition widget
                                                   in the Smart Stack, all without a tap.

                                                   Sound Recognition on Apple Watch

                                        All four of these features are enabled by the new S11 chip on Apple Watch and its
                                        Secure Exclave, a dedicated secure buffer that processes audio privately in a
                                        protected environment that no one can access, not even Apple.

                                        Audio Intelligence features do not create audio recordings that can be accessed
                                        by the operating system, apps, the user, or Apple. There is no recording to share,
                                        forward, or produce if requested by any party, because no recording exists.

                                        This paper details how Audio Intelligence works, the privacy and security
                                        architecture that protects you and those around you, and the controls you have
                                        over the entire experience.

                                       Privacy and security architecture
                                       Secure Exclave
                                       On Apple Watch
                                       The S11 chip in Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 includes a
                                       Secure Exclave: a hardware-isolated compartment built into the silicon to
                                       process sensor data separately from the rest of the system. Data that is
                                       processed and analyzed in the Secure Exclave cannot be accessed by watchOS,
                                       apps, the user, or Apple.

                                       Audio from the microphone enters the Secure Exclave of Apple Watch, where it is
                                       initially processed for speech, sounds, or music, without transcribing or storing
                                       the raw audio. This buffer is a continuously overwritten stream that exists only
                                       within the protected hardware and never creates an audio recording.

                                       On iPhone
                                       iPhone 16, iPhone 16 Pro, iPhone 17e, iPhone Air, and later models contain a
                                       Secure Exclave that processes raw audio in a contained, secure environment that
                                       cannot be accessed by iOS, apps, the user, or Apple.

                                       Secure pairing between Apple Watch and iPhone
                                       Apple Watch pairs with iPhone using a new audio-verified pairing mechanism
                                       that exists in addition to Bluetooth pairing. This creates an even more secure
                                       connection between the Secure Exclaves on Apple Watch and iPhone. Secure
                                       pairing ensures that ambient data from the Secure Exclave on Apple Watch can
                                       only be sent to the Secure Exclave of the paired iPhone, and no other device.

End-to-end encrypted syncing with iCloud
Once a Siri Recap or Live Rewind text is generated, you decide what to keep. No
raw audio is ever saved or synced. If you use iCloud with two-factor
authentication and have a device passcode, any Audio Intelligence text you
choose to save syncs across your devices end-to-end encrypted. Only you can
access this information, and only on devices where you are signed in to iCloud.
Apple does not hold the encryption keys. No one else, not even Apple, can
access the end-to-end encrypted data you saved in the Siri app. If you delete a
saved Siri Recap or Live Rewind text, it is permanently removed from all your
devices and from iCloud.

                                         Siri Recap
                                         Siri Recap summarizes conversations throughout your day and produces high-
                                         level Apple Intelligence-generated notes so you can stay present in the moment
                                         and catch up later.

                                         Detecting that a conversation is happening
                                         The S11 chip uses a lightweight AI model to determine whether speech is
                                         occurring nearby. This model doesn t transcribe or record anything. It simply
                                         detects whether a conversation has started. If a conversation is detected, audio
                                         flows into a protected buffer inside the Secure Exclave on the S11 chip. It is
                                         isolated in hardware so that it is inaccessible to the user, the operating system,
                                         apps, and even Apple.

                                         Audio securely transfers to the Secure Exclave on iPhone
                                         The Secure Exclave on Apple Watch encrypts the audio and transmits it to the
                                         Secure Exclave on the paired iPhone. Once the transfer completes, audio is
                                         immediately deleted from the Secure Exclave on Apple Watch, and only the
                                         Secure Exclave of the paired iPhone can decrypt it. If the transfer fails, for
                                         example if iPhone is not within wireless range of Apple Watch, the Secure
                                         Exclave on Apple Watch will attempt the transfer again when the connection is
                                         re-established. If a connection cannot be re-established in time, the encryption
                                         keys expire and the encrypted audio is permanently deleted.

                                         Secure on-device transcription turns audio into text
                                         The encrypted audio is decrypted inside the Secure Exclave of the paired iPhone.
                                         On-device speech recognition transcribes the audio to text, and then an on-
                                         device language model generates a condensed version that is less than half the
                                         length of the original transcript. This is designed to distill the conversation to its

                                         Private Cloud Compute summarizes the text
                                         The condensed text is encrypted and sent to Private Cloud Compute. Contextual
                                         information is also sent to improve summary quality. For example, Now Playing
                                         data helps the model understand that speech may have originated from music or
                                         a podcast. Calendar data can help generate accurate titles of summaries. High-
                                         level location labels such as home, work, and school, along with locality
                                         information such as city, state, and country, add context to the summarization.
                                         Point-of-interest categories like “grocery store” or “park” may also be included.
                                         Precise location and specific points-of-interest are not included.

                                         Apple Foundation Models running on Private Cloud Compute generate a title and
                                         a summary with key points. Siri Recap is designed so that sensitive information is
                                         not included in summaries, such as financial data, government-assigned
                                         identifiers, authentication data, and certain personal identifiers.

                                        Data in Private Cloud Compute is never stored or accessible by Apple or anyone
                                        else. It is used only to fulfill your request. Independent security experts can verify
                                        this promise at any time. For a complete description of Private Cloud Compute s
                                        architecture and guarantees, see Apple s security documentation at
                                        security.apple.com/blog/private-cloud-compute/.

                                        Your summary in the Siri app
                                        The finished, text-based summary is encrypted and sent from Private Cloud
                                        Compute back to iPhone and Apple Watch, where it is available to view in the Siri
                                        app in the Recaps tab.

                                        Siri Recaps are automatically deleted after 7 days, unless you explicitly choose to
                                        save them. If you use iCloud with two-factor authentication and have a device
                                        passcode, Siri Recaps sync across your devices end-to-end encrypted. You can
                                        edit or delete any summary directly in the Siri app.

                                        How Siri Recap respects those around you
                                        By design, Siri Recap does not create a recording, does not produce a verbatim
                                        transcript, and does not identify and attribute speakers. The output is a brief,
                                        high-level summary, comparable to notes a person might write after a
                                        conversation. There is no audible signal because no raw audio is retained, and
                                        there is no way to reconstruct the original audio from a Siri Recap or share raw
                                        audio with anyone.

                                        Control for Siri Recap
                                        To use Siri Recap, you must opt in when setting up Apple Watch, and you can
                                        enable or disable Siri Recap at any time through Siri Settings on Apple Watch,
                                        and Siri Settings in the Apple Watch app on iPhone. You can control when it s
                                        active (for example, only during work hours, or never at night) and where it s
                                        active (for example, only at your workplace). You can also turn it on and off
                                        manually with Control Center.

                                                Live Rewind
                                                Live Rewind lets you instantly see the last 15 seconds of a conversation as text.
                                                This is great for moments like when someone quickly shares a book
                                                recommendation, cooking instructions, or even an unfamiliar term you want to
                                                revisit later.

                                                A continuous rolling buffer
                                                Audio from the microphone flows into the audio buffer inside the Secure Exclave
                                                on the S11 chip. Old audio is continuously replaced by new audio, so audio
                                                doesn t accumulate, and raw audio isn t accessible outside this protected buffer.

                                                Secure on-device processing turns audio into text
                                                Live Rewind processes audio on-device. Live Rewind requires a double-press of
                                                the Digital Crown to activate it every time you use it. After activating, the
                                                immediately preceding 15 seconds of buffered audio is sent from the Secure
                                                Exclave of the Apple Watch to iPhone. If iPhone is not within wireless range of
                                                Apple Watch, the transfer will fail with an error, and the audio will be immediately
                                                discarded. If the transfer to iPhone is successful, on-device speech recognition
                                                converts the audio to text, and is designed so that the raw audio cannot be
                                                accessed by iOS, apps, the user, or Apple. Once converted, the raw audio is then
                                                discarded immediately.

                                                Review and optionally save
                                                The 15-second Live Rewind text is then sent back to Apple Watch, where it
                                                appears on the display. 30 seconds after your Apple Watch display dims, the text
                                                disappears. You can choose to save it to the Siri app to revisit later, where it

                                                How Live Rewind respects those around you
                                                When activated, an audible chime plays from the speaker on your Apple Watch,
                                                even if your Apple Watch is on silent or you have headphones connected. A full-
                                                display animation and microphone indicator appear on the Apple Watch display,

                                                   Shazam and Sound Recognition
                                                   The Secure Exclave in the S11 chip also enables Shazam and Sound Recognition
                                                   on Apple Watch. Audio is buffered into small segments within the Secure Exclave
                                                   and analyzed for specific sounds, then immediately deleted if nothing is detected
                                                   in that buffer.

                                                   When Shazam is enabled, the Secure Exclave on Apple Watch is designed to
                                                   detect music playing around you. If you open the Smart Stack on Apple Watch
                                                   and music has been detected, the Secure Exclave will process the audio in the
                                                   secure buffer and create an acoustic signature of that song (a compact
                                                   representation that cannot be reversed into the original audio). That signature,
                                                   and not the raw audio, is then sent to Shazam servers to identify the song. The
                                                   audio buffer on the Secure Exclave of Apple Watch is then immediately deleted. If
                                                   there s a match to the signature, the song title and artist s name are sent to Apple
                                                   Watch, which then displays this information in the Music Recognition widget in
                                                   the Smart Stack, the Shazam app, or as a response from Siri.

                                                   For Sound Recognition, when the audio processing within the Secure Exclave
                                                   recognizes a specific sound, such as a smoke alarm, doorbell, or baby crying, it
                                                   sends you a notification. You can enable or disable individual sound categories in
                                                   Settings. No audio is accessible outside of the Secure Exclave on Apple Watch,
                                                   and no audio is transcribed or transmitted. Sound Recognition takes place on
                                                   Apple Watch.

    Conclusion
    Audio Intelligence features on Apple Watch help you stay present and engaged,
    catch details in a conversation you may have missed, and be aware of important
    sounds around you. These are enabled by a privacy and security architecture in
    which raw audio is handled within dedicated hardware, is not saved as a file, and
    is not accessible to the operating system, apps, or Apple. All of this is possible
    due to the Secure Exclave architecture on the new S11 chip and on supported
    iPhone models. This architecture creates a hardware-enforced boundary around
    audio processing. You control whether and when these features are active.

    Audio Intelligence features on Apple Watch respect you by having privacy
    designed into the product, from the silicon up, with hardware and software
    working seamlessly together.
