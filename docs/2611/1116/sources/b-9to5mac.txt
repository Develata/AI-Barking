Apple reveals how iCloud+ increases Siri AI and Apple Intelligence usage limits

 [https://9to5mac.com/author/chmiller44/]
 Chance Miller [https://9to5mac.com/author/chmiller44/] | Nov 16 2026 - 10:12 am PT 

 3 Comments [#comments]

iOS 27.3 beta 2 is now available, and Apple is adding the ability for users to unlock higher usage limits for Apple Intelligence and Siri AI via iCloud+. Here’s everything you need to know. 

From the start, Apple has said [https://9to5mac.com/2026/09/09/apple-shares-more-details-about-apple-intelligence-usage-limits-and-terms/] that some Siri AI and Apple Intelligence features have usage limits because “they rely on powerful server models.” The company previously outlined that increased access would be available across most iCloud+ subscription plans, and now we have the full details on how this will work.

Apple says the new system applies to several categories of Apple Intelligence features, including:

Siri AI

Photo tools: Clean Up, Extend, Spatial Reframing, and Image Playground

AFM Cloud and Cloud Pro models in Shortcuts

Third-party apps using Apple Foundation Models in Private Cloud Compute

The usage limits are per device, not per account. This means your iPhone, iPad, and Mac each have separate allowances. Apple isn’t disclosing specific limit numbers. Instead, it says that limits can vary based on the feature, request complexity, system demand, and more.

As you near your limit, you’ll see an alert within whichever feature you’re using. For example, if you are using Image Playground, a message will appear telling you that you’ve almost hit your usage limit for the day. Once you hit the limit, you’ll be prompted to wait for it to reset or upgrade your iCloud+ plan.

If you hit your limit, basic Siri requests will still work. This includes things like setting timers and alarms. Requests that require Siri to understand personal context and perform more complex, multi-step actions, however, are subject to the limits.

For example, asking Siri to find the details of an upcoming dinner reservation in your email and add them to your calendar would count toward your daily limit. 

Additionally, each device uses its own standard daily usage limit first. After that, it can tap into the additional capacity included with an eligible iCloud+ subscription:

iCloud+ planMonthly price (US)Increased daily limit

50 GB$0.99Standard limits

200 GB$2.99Up to 2x

2 TB$9.99Up to 5x

6 TB$29.99Up to 10x

12 TB$59.99Up to 20x

The usage unlocked by iCloud+ is shared across all devices signed in to your Apple Account, as well as family members sharing your iCloud+ plan. The shared usage is available on a first-come, first-served basis

All limits will reset daily at the same time, and limits don’t roll over. In most cases, limits reset around midnight in your current time zone.

Apple has published a support document with more details [https://support.apple.com/en-us/127901].
