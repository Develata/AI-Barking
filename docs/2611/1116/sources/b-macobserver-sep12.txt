Apple Has Given Two Different Answers on Paying for More Apple Intelligence
Apple Has Given Two Different Answers on Paying for More Apple Intelligence
Mike Peterson

 3 minute read
Sep 12th, 2026 7:43 AM EEST | News
Image: Apple

Apple has published two statements about paying for more Apple Intelligence, four months apart, and they do not line up. In June it said increased access comes with most iCloud+ subscription plans. In September it said expanded access will be available for a fee in the future.

The daily usage limits are published in footnotes on Apple's iOS 27 and Apple Intelligence pages. Image: Apple

Both statements are Apple’s own, both concern the same daily usage limits on server-side features, and neither carries a number, a price or a date. That combination is the story, because the limits take effect for everyone on Monday, September 14.

Key facts
Item	What Apple publishes
Features Apple names as limited	Siri AI, Intelligent Photo Editing Tools, Image Playground, AFM 3 Cloud models in Shortcuts
Is that the full list	No, Apple writes including but not limited to
Size of any daily limit	Not published for any feature
What the limits depend on	Feature, request complexity, system demand, system policies and other factors
June wording	Increased access is available with most iCloud+ subscription plans
September wording	Expanded access to such features will be available for a fee in the future
Price of the future tier	Not published
iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max ship with iOS 27 and the same server-side limits as every other supported iPhone. Image: Apple
The four features Apple names, and the phrase that matters

Apple’s footnote reads that certain Apple Intelligence features relying on server-side models are subject to daily usage limits, including but not limited to Siri AI, Intelligent Photo Editing Tools, Image Playground, and AFM 3 Cloud models in Shortcuts.

Those four cover a lot of ground. Siri AI is the assistant itself. Intelligent Photo Editing Tools is the group that includes Clean Up, the Extend tool and Spatial Reframing in Photos. Image Playground is image generation. Shortcuts can call Apple’s cloud models directly. The phrase including but not limited to means the list is a sample rather than a boundary, so a feature not named here can still be limited.

Write with Siri is one of the features that leans on Apple's server models. Image: Apple
June’s answer and September’s answer, side by side
When	Where	What Apple says about getting more
June 8, 2026	Apple Intelligence press release	Increased access is available with most iCloud+ subscription plans, which also include Apple Intelligence support for compatible Home cameras
September 2026	Apple Intelligence page and iOS 27 page footnotes	Expanded access to such features will be available for a fee in the future

One is present tense and points at a subscription people already buy. The other is future tense and points at a fee Apple has not described. Apple has not said whether the fee is iCloud+, whether it sits on top of iCloud+, or whether the June arrangement continues once the fee exists.

What can be said plainly is that the September footnote is the newer of the two, it shipped alongside the iPhone 18 Pro announcement, and it is the first on-the-record confirmation that Apple intends to charge for something inside Apple Intelligence.

No page gives you a number

There is no published figure for any daily limit. Not a count of Image Playground generations, not a number of Siri AI requests, not a cap on Clean Up edits. Apple says the limits may vary by feature, request complexity, system demand, system policies and other factors, which rules out a fixed number even in principle.

That matters for anyone weighing the $1,199 iPhone 18 Pro partly on its AI features, and equally for anyone keeping an older iPhone. The limits are the same on every supported device, because they are set by Apple’s servers rather than by the chip in your hand. A more powerful phone does not buy you a higher cloud allowance, and that includes the iPhone Duo when it arrives in October.

What the limits do not touch

Apple scopes the limits to features that rely on server-side models, which run through Private Cloud Compute. Work that happens entirely on the device is not described as limited anywhere on Apple’s pages, and Apple does not publish a per-feature breakdown of which path each feature takes.

The whole system is also subject to Apple Intelligence Terms and Conditions, which Apple references in the same footnote. Anything sitting inside those terms rather than on the product pages is outside what we can check today, and nothing about a paid tier was said on stage at the Surprise and shine event.

What Apple has not said

Apple has not priced the future tier, named it, dated it, or said which features it will cover. It has not published any daily limit as a number, has not said what happens when you reach one, and has not reconciled the iCloud+ line from June with the fee line from September. It has not said whether the limits differ by country or by language.

iOS 27 ships free on Monday, September 14, and the first real test is whether iOS itself tells you when a limit has been reached. We will report what the interface says rather than what the footnotes imply.
