Apple Confirms iCloud+ Can Boost Siri AI Usage Limits by Up to 20x
Apple Confirms iCloud+ Can Boost Siri AI Usage Limits by Up to 20x
Shane Watson

 1 minute read
Nov 17th, 2026 9:06 AM EET | News
Image: Siri AI

Apple has revealed how iCloud+ subscriptions will increase daily usage limits for Siri AI and Apple Intelligence, allowing users to access cloud-based AI features more frequently. The changes are being tested in iOS 27.3 beta 2, alongside corresponding iPadOS and macOS betas, with higher limits available through existing iCloud+ plans at no additional cost.

Apple previously confirmed that Siri AI would have daily usage restrictions, although it had not explained how much additional access subscribers would receive.

How Much More Siri AI Usage Does iCloud+ Offer?

According to the latest details, Apple will provide up to 20 times the standard daily allowance, depending on the subscription tier. The 50GB plan offers no increase, while higher tiers receive progressively larger allowances.

Apple has not disclosed the exact number of requests allowed each day, as limits depend on the feature, request complexity, and server demand.

Which Apple Intelligence Features Have Usage Limits?

According to Apple’s support documentation, the restrictions apply to features that require server-side AI processing, including:

Siri AI requests involving personal context and multiple actions.
Image Playground and Photos tools such as Clean Up, Extend, and Spatial Reframing.
Shortcuts using Apple’s cloud-based foundation models.
Third-party apps accessing Apple Foundation Models through Private Cloud Compute.

Basic Siri commands, including setting alarms and timers, will remain unlimited.

How Daily Limits and Family Sharing Work

Each iPhone, iPad, and Mac receives its own free daily allowance. Once exhausted, the device draws from the additional usage included with an eligible iCloud+ subscription.

This extra capacity is shared across devices and Family Sharing members on a first-come, first-served basis. Limits generally reset around midnight in the user’s local time zone, and unused allowances do not carry over.

Apple will also notify users when they approach or reach their limits, offering the option to wait for a reset or upgrade their subscription.

The changes provide clearer details about Apple Intelligence’s paid access model, although Apple still has not published the exact daily request allowances for individual features.
