Everything New in iOS 27.3 Beta 2

Monday November 16, 2026 4:15 pm PST by Juli Clover [https://www.macrumors.com/author/juli-clover/]

Apple released the second beta of iOS 27.3 today, and like the first beta [https://www.macrumors.com/guide/ios-27-3-beta-features/], it has several major new features. There are improvements to the iPhone Duo [https://www.macrumors.com/roundup/iphone-duo/], tweaks to apps, new Siri [https://www.macrumors.com/guide/siri/] features, and more.

iPhone Duo Face Unlock

Apple added a new option to quickly unlock the iPhone Duo [https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/16/iphone-duo-face-unlock/] using the front-facing camera. The option is only available when the &zwnj;iPhone Duo&zwnj; is closed, and it can only be used on the Lock Screen.

Authenticating with Touch ID [https://www.macrumors.com/guide/touch-id/] is required for Apple Pay [https://www.macrumors.com/roundup/apple-pay/] purchases and for accessing passwords and logins. Unlocking with the camera requires recent &zwnj;Touch ID&zwnj; or passcode authentication, and &zwnj;Touch ID&zwnj; is required again after about 45 minutes of inactivity.

Unlocking with the front-facing camera does not work when the &zwnj;iPhone Duo&zwnj; is open, presumably because the under-display camera is not high enough quality. The face unlock feature can be turned on in the Settings app under &zwnj;Touch ID&zwnj; and Passcode.

iPhone Duo Lock When Closed

Apple added an option to lock the &zwnj;iPhone Duo&zwnj; when it's closed instead of transferring content to the front display.

Close to Lock can be turned on in the Display and Brightness section of the Settings app.

Siri AI

There's a new "Ask About Page" feature for &zwnj;Siri&zwnj; AI that lets &zwnj;Siri&zwnj; see the entire content of a webpage, even if it's not currently in view on the screen.

&zwnj;Siri&zwnj; AI can also now access Health data when given permission, and control over smart home products has improved.

Apple Intelligence and Siri AI Usage Limits

iOS 27.3 introduces the Apple Intelligence [https://www.macrumors.com/guide/apple-intelligence/] usage limits cxdApple has been warning about for months. &zwnj;Siri&zwnj; AI and &zwnj;Apple Intelligence&zwnj; features that use server-side processing will now have daily usage limits on a per-device basis.

Extra access is tied to 200GB and higher iCloud [https://www.macrumors.com/guide/icloud/]+ plans. The 200GB plan gives up to 2x usage, the 2TB plan is up to 5x, the 6TB plan is up to 10x, and the 12TB plan is up to 20x.

Limits apply to complex &zwnj;Siri&zwnj; AI requests that involve multiple steps, image generation and editing tools, AI workflows and automations in Shortcuts, and third-party apps that use Apple Foundation Models in Private Cloud Compute. More information can be found in our article on the new limits [https://www.macrumors.com/2026/11/16/apple-icloud-paid-siri-ai-upgrade/].

Calendar

The Calendar app has an updated layout with the options for searching and adding an event moved to the bottom of the app. The "Today" button that takes you to the current date has also been updated to show the date.

Apple TV App Podcasts

Video podcast episodes are now available in the Apple TV [https://www.macrumors.com/roundup/apple-tv/] app. Only video podcasts that are free will show up in the &zwnj;Apple TV&zwnj; app.

Apple News+ Puzzles

Apple News [https://www.macrumors.com/guide/apple-news/]+ Puzzles are expanding to Australian, UK, and French-Canadian &zwnj;Apple News&zwnj;+ subscribers.

Subscribers in Australia get access to a daily quick crossword, crossword mini, Sudoku, Quartiles, and Emoji Game. UK subscribers get the daily quick crossword, crossword mini, and Sudoku, while French-Canadian subscribers can play Quartiles and Emoji Game.

Weather App

There are 10 new weather condition symbols in the Weather app, but Apple doesn't appear to be using them yet.

The Weather app gets 10 new weather-condition symbols in iOS 27.3 beta 2. https://t.co/1c5W4n5gKn
— pdfu (@itspdfu) November 16, 2026 [https://x.com/itspdfu/status/2122410081052750128?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw]

Home App

The Home app has an "Unrecognized People" section that limits how guests can use Safari and &zwnj;Siri&zwnj; on Apple home devices.

Apple Music

There's code in Apple Music [https://www.macrumors.com/guide/apple-music/] for an album release countdown [https://x.com/itspdfu/status/2122455331842261469], but it doesn't appear to be active yet.
