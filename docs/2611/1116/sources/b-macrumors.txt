iOS 27.3 Beta Adds Daily Siri AI Limits and iCloud+ Upgrades

Monday November 16, 2026 10:14 am PST by Juli Clover [https://www.macrumors.com/author/juli-clover/]

With the latest iOS 27.3, iPadOS 27.3, and macOS 27.3 betas, Apple is starting to test limits and paid upgrades for Siri AI [https://support.apple.com/en-us/127901] and Apple Intelligence [https://www.macrumors.com/guide/apple-intelligence/] features.

Every Apple device gets a free daily access allowance for Siri [https://www.macrumors.com/guide/siri/] AI and &zwnj;Apple Intelligence&zwnj; features that rely on server-side AI processing, such as Image Playground [https://www.macrumors.com/guide/image-playground/] and Clean Up in Photos [https://www.macrumors.com/guide/photos/]. When that limit is hit, there will be an option to extend usage with an iCloud [https://www.macrumors.com/guide/icloud/]+ plan. Here's what will be limited after the daily allowance has been reached:

&zwnj;Siri&zwnj; AI for multi-step, personal context requests that rely on server-side processing, such as asking &zwnj;Siri&zwnj; what time you need to leave from home to make it to a location on time.

Image generation and editing tools, including &zwnj;Image Playground&zwnj; and the AI options in &zwnj;Photos&zwnj; like Clean Up, Extend, and Spatial Reframing.

Workflows and automations in Shortcuts that rely on AFM Cloud and AFM Cloud Pro.

Third-party apps that use Apple Foundation Models in Private Cloud Compute for AI features.

While more complex, multi-step requests will be restricted, basic &zwnj;Siri&zwnj; commands will continue to be available even after a device limit is reached. Examples of basic &zwnj;Siri&zwnj; commands include those with a single ask, such as "set a timer for 30 minutes" or "text Eric I'm on my way." 

Apple's existing &zwnj;iCloud&zwnj;+ plans will include higher daily usage for &zwnj;Siri&zwnj; AI and &zwnj;Apple Intelligence&zwnj;, with no change in pricing. If you already subscribe to an eligible &zwnj;iCloud&zwnj;+ plan, you will have higher usage limits. Free limits are per device, not per account, and do not roll over. 

More access starts with the 200GB &zwnj;iCloud&zwnj;+ plan, which is priced at $2.99 per month. Users who subscribe to this plan have up to a 2x usage limit. Users who subscribe to the $9.99/month 2TB plan get up to 5x usage. The $29.99/month 6TB plan comes with up to 10x usage, and the $59.99/month 12TB plan offers up to 20x usage. Family members who share a plan will have a shared usage pool.

50GB - No limit increase

200GB - Up to 2x limit

2TB - Up to 5x limit

6TB - Up to 10x limit

12TB - Up to 20x limit

Every Apple device will use the free daily limit first, and when that's reached, &zwnj;Siri&zwnj; AI and &zwnj;Apple Intelligence&zwnj; will use the &zwnj;iCloud&zwnj;+ paid access. In a family group, a device that hits the free limit will start drawing from the shared pool on a first-come, first-served basis. Limits will reset at around 12:00 a.m. in the user's current time zone, and in families, the reset is based on the plan purchaser's time zone.

Each of the four categories has a different limit, and Apple will likely use the beta testing period to refine those limits and make sure people aren't hitting their caps too quickly. Limits depend on feature category, request complexity, and system demand, and they could change over time. 

Apple plans to give warnings when people are approaching their limits, and a notification when the limit has been hit. From there, users will be able to purchase additional access through an &zwnj;iCloud&zwnj;+ plan, or wait for their limit to reset.

Apple is not planning to offer additional usage beyond what's available with the 12TB &zwnj;iCloud&zwnj;+ plan at this time.
