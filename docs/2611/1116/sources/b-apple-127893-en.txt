How to get Siri AI

Siri AI (Beta) is an entirely new version of Siri powered by the next generation of Apple Intelligence, and deeply integrated across your Apple devices. A more capable and conversational assistant, Siri AI can answer questions about almost anything and help you find what you need in the moment.

Siri AI understands personal context across your messages, emails, photos, and more, so you can find what you need just by asking. It can answer questions about what's on your screen, or go out to the web for up-to-date information to generate a helpful answer. And with even more systemwide app actions, you can get more done across your apps.

A dedicated Siri app uses iCloud to privately sync your conversation history across your devices, so you can start a conversation on iPhone and continue it on Mac, iPad, Apple Watch, or Apple Vision Pro.

Siri AI is available in beta for iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27 Golden Gate, visionOS 27, tvOS 27.2.1, HomeView software 27.2, and watchOS 27 when your device language [https://support.apple.com/en-us/109358] and Siri language [https://support.apple.com/en-us/120499] are set to the same supported language and your Apple Account is in an eligible region.

Turn on Siri AI

After you update your device, go to Settings, tap or click Siri, then tap or click Try Siri AI (Beta). If Siri is turned off, tap or click Turn On Siri first.

Follow the onscreen instructions to pick a voice, complete voice enrollment, and review your privacy settings. Siri AI will begin to download automatically. During times of high demand, you might be added to a waitlist first, and wait times can vary.

Siri AI requirements

To use Siri AI, you need an Apple Intelligence–capable iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple HomeView, Apple TV, or Apple Vision Pro with device language [https://support.apple.com/en-us/109358] and Siri language [https://support.apple.com/en-us/120499] set to a supported language:

iOS 27 on iPhone 15 Pro models and later, iPhone 16 models or later, iPhone Duo, and iPhone Air

iPadOS 27 on iPad mini (A17 Pro and A20 Pro), and iPad models with M1 or later

macOS 27 Golden Gate on MacBook Neo (A18 Pro) and Mac with M1 or later

visionOS 27 on any Apple Vision Pro

HomeView software 27.2 on Apple HomeView*

tvOS 27.2.1 on Apple TV 4K (2nd generation and later), and a Siri AI-enabled, paired device nearby

watchOS 27 on Apple Watch Series 9 or later, Apple Watch Ultra 2 or later, and Apple Watch SE 3, with a Siri AI-enabled, paired iPhone nearby

*For personal context understanding on Apple HomeView, you need a Siri AI-enabled, paired device nearby.

Supported languages and regions

Siri AI is currently available in English, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese, and Spanish. Availability varies by region and platform. After you turn on Siri AI, it will continue to work when you travel to other regions. Siri AI is available only in English on Apple HomeView.

For European Union residents

Siri AI is not available for iOS, iPadOS, or watchOS in the EU. It is available for macOS 27 Golden Gate and visionOS 27 if your Apple Account region [https://support.apple.com/en-us/118283] is in the EU and your device language and Siri language are set to the same supported language.

For China mainland residents

Siri AI will not currently work if your Apple Account region [https://support.apple.com/en-us/118283] is in mainland China.

Siri AI features

Siri AI features vary by region, language, hardware, and software version.

Broad world knowledge: ask about virtually any topic and get a detailed answer.

Personal context understanding: find what you're looking for across apps like Messages, Mail, Calendar, Notes, Reminders, and more.

Onscreen awareness: ask about and take action on what's on your screen.

App actions: use Siri to get things done across your apps, like sending messages, creating calendar events, editing notes, and more.

Siri app: pick up where you left off with the Siri app, which keeps your conversation history synced with iCloud across your Apple devices. Not available on Apple HomeView.

Write with Siri: describe what you need, and Siri can generate a draft from scratch, edit what you've written, or give you feedback—virtually anywhere you type.

Visual Intelligence: learn more about what's in front of you with Visual Intelligence. Use Siri mode in the Camera app (iPhone only), the screenshot gesture on iPhone and iPad, or keyboard shortcuts on Mac. On Apple Vision Pro, you can search, ask questions, and take action by looking at your surroundings and asking Siri. Not available on Apple HomeView.

Expressive voices: customize pace and expressiveness (English only). Requires a device with the AFM Core Advanced on-device model. AFM Core Advanced is available on iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max, iPhone Duo, iPhone 17 Pro, iPhone 17 Pro Max, iPhone Air, iPad mini (A20 Pro), iPad models with M4 and later and at least 12GB of unified memory, Mac models with M3 and later and at least 12 GB of unified memory, and Apple Vision Pro (M5). For Apple HomeView, the paired device must meet this requirement.

Siri AI is not available for users under 13. Certain Apple Intelligence features that rely on server-side models are subject to daily usage limits, including but not limited to Siri AI, Intelligent Photo Editing Tools, Image Playground, and AFM 3 Cloud models in Shortcuts. Daily limits may vary by feature, request complexity, system demand, system policies, and other factors. Expanded access to such features will be available for a fee in the future. Use of these features is subject to the Apple Intelligence Terms and Conditions [https://support.apple.com/en-us/148587].
