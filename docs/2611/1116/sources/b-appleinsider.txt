Siri AI daily usage limits and fees will launch with iOS 27.3
William Gallagher
4

Mon Nov 16 2026, 01:16 PM EST · 2 minute read

Siri AI costs have been revealed

Apple has unveiled its charges for users who utilize Siri AI more than a quite vague amount, and confirmed how the fees are tied to iCloud+ subscriptions.

Video Muted

As Apple has been saying since July 2026, Apple Intelligence compute costs are tied to iCloud+ subscriptions. The company has now revealed more of what those costs are, and how users will be kept informed of their usage.

Apple Intelligence and Siri AI does not depend on users paying for an iCloud+ subscription. All users will always be able to make the most simple requests such as sending a text message or setting an alarm.

Then all users will have the ability to use an unspecified number of more complex Siri AI requests. While Apple hasn't defined any particular number, these more complex requests are ones that require server-side handling rather than being done entirely on-device.

From iOS 27.3, Apple is imposing daily usage limits on such requests as Siri AI and image editing tools like Extend. There are also limits on Shortcuts and third-party apps that use Apple Foundation Models.

Using iCloud+

Apple's iCloud+ offers greater cloud storage and other benefits, which now include further AI requests in all but the lowest tier. Users on the lowest $1/month 50GB iCloud+ tier do not get any further usage allowance.

Beyond that, all tiers get increased AI usage:

200GB tier ($2.99/month) has up to twice the regular limit
2TB tier ($9.99/month) has up to five times
6TB tier ($29.99/month) has up to ten times
12TB tier ($59.99/month) has up to 20 times

What Apple is not making clear is precisely what these figures actually mean. The company is not saying how many Siri AI requests fall within the limits, for example, nor how many times Image Playground can be used.

Apple describes the regular daily usage limit as being per device. So presumably if you exceed the limit on your iPhone, you can try it on your iPad.

Previously finding out how much AI usage you've done has taken a couple of steps, but now from iOS 27.3 there will be notifications. Users will be notified when they hit around 80% of their limit, and the limit resets every 24 hours.

If a user hits their limit and needs to continue without waiting for the reset, the notification will include the option to pay to get more usage. However, this is not a one-time extra, it is not a way to increase the limit for the current day alone.

Instead, it is an upsell to the next tier of iCloud+. This means that users will then be on that next tier until they actively choose to change their plan back down to their previous tier.

There are no changes to the iCloud+ pricing tiers. And iCloud+ storage can be shared with your family, in which case the extra AI usage can be shared with them too.
