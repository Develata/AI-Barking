Artificial Analysis Word Error Rate (AA-WER) Index

Measures transcription accuracy across 3 datasets to evaluate models in real-world speech with diverse accents, domain-specific language, and challenging channel & acoustic conditions.

AA-WER is calculated as an audio-duration-weighted average of WER across ~8 hours from three datasets: AA-AgentTalk (50%), VoxPopuli-Cleaned-AA (25%), and Earnings22-Cleaned-AA (25%). See methodology [/methodology/speech-to-text] for more detail.

Artificial Analysis Diarization Index

Measures Word Diarization Error Rate (WDER): the share of scored reference words not given to the person who said them, across English conversations of 2 to 8 people. The index is the equal mean of the 2, 3–4 and 5–8 speaker bands. Lower is better. Further detail in the methodology [/methodology/speech-to-text#diarization-evaluation].

Diarization by Dataset

AA-Diarization is our own held-out English conversations in the Diarization Index set, real and synthetic. NOTSOFAR-1 is only the NOTSOFAR-1 meetings inside the Diarization Index set, not the separate shared-room meetings. Each is pooled WDER: wrong speaker plus missed words ÷ scored reference words, with no speaker-band weighting. Lower is better. Further detail in the methodology [/methodology/speech-to-text#diarization-evaluation].

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the most accurate speech to text model?

Fun-Realtime-ASR-preview leads with the lowest AA-WER (Artificial Analysis Word Error Rate) of 1.7% across 65 models evaluated.

What are the top speech to text models?

The top speech to text models by accuracy (AA-WER) are: 1. Fun-Realtime-ASR-preview (1.7%), 2. Scribe v3, ElevenLabs (1.9%), 3. MAI-Transcribe-2 (2.0%), 4. Zoom Scribe V2.1 Pro (2.1%), and 5. Voxtral Small Transcribe 3 (2.1%). Lower AA-WER indicates better transcription accuracy.

Which is the fastest speech to text model?

Parakeet TDT 0.6B V3, Togetherai is the fastest with a speed factor of 664.5x real-time, followed by Nova-3 (502.7x) and Resonant-1 (355.9x). Higher speed factors mean faster transcription.

Which is the cheapest speech to text model?

StepAudio 2.5 ASR is the most affordable at $0.37 per 1,000 minutes, followed by Modulate STT Batch English VFast ($0.42) and Wizper (L, v3), fal.ai ($0.50).

Which is the best open weights speech to text model?

SOV-Transcribe-1, Salesforce is the most accurate open weights model with an AA-WER of 2.3%. There are 16 open weights models out of 65 total evaluated.

What are the top open weights speech to text models?

The top open weights speech to text models by accuracy are: 1. SOV-Transcribe-1, Salesforce (AA-WER 2.3%), 2. Voxtral Small, Mistral (AA-WER 2.8%), and 3. Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe (AA-WER 2.8%).

How do I choose the best speech to text model?

The best model depends on your priorities. Use the scatter plots to visualize trade-offs between accuracy (AA-WER), speed, and price. For applications requiring high accuracy, prioritize models with lower AA-WER scores. For real-time applications, focus on speed factor. For cost-sensitive workloads, compare the price charts.
