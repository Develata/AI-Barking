About the Anthropic Safeguards Blog

We are Anthropic's Safeguards team [https://www.anthropic.com/news/building-safeguards-for-claude]. We work to keep AI systems like Claude both helpful and safe. Our work spans safety systems, monitoring, robustness, cybersecurity, and privacy. We'll use this blog to share research notes and findings from our work. We hope they're useful to others working on similar problems.
