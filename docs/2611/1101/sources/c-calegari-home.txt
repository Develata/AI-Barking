 I am a Professor at the University of Chicago [http://www.math.uchicago.edu]. 

 My research is in the area of algebraic number theory.
I am particularly interested in the Langlands programme, especially, the notion of reciprocity linking Galois representations
and motives to automorphic forms.
For reprints and preprints, please visit my "Research" [research.html] page.
