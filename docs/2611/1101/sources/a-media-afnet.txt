 SemiAnalysis Launches GDPx AI Benchmark as Claude Opus 5.5 Leads with 12% Success Rate

 Wisevoter
 Published Nov 2, 2026 · 2:54 AM 

 On November 2, 2026, research firm SemiAnalysis unveiled GDPx, an AI benchmark evaluating frontier models across agentic tasks in 11 economically valuable domains. Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 topped the initial leaderboard with a 12.0% success rate, followed by Google's Gemini 4 Carbon at 10.9% and OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra at 9.6%, highlighting that frontier models still require extensive human oversight.

 Source: Wisevoter · image source [https://wisevoter.com/world/2026/11/02/semianalysis-gdpx-ai-benchmark-agentic-capabilities] 

SemiAnalysis unveiled the GDPx artificial intelligence benchmark on November 2, 2026, evaluating frontier models across agentic tasks in 11 economically valuable domains, including chip design, biology, and finance. Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 topped the initial v1.0 leaderboard with an overall success rate of 12.0%.

On the v1.0 leaderboard, Google's Gemini 4 Carbon placed second with 10.9%, leading specifically in biology and software engineering. OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra ranked third with 9.6%, securing the highest marks in recursive self-improvement and materials science. All evaluated models scored well below full task completion, indicating that no current model comprehensively solves tasks across the tested sectors.

SemiAnalysis selected domains based on where it projects frontier models will yield the highest return on investment, operating on the view that the first trillion dollars of AI revenue will cluster in high-ROI areas, rather than weighting domains by gross domestic product share or occupational taxonomy. The benchmark builds on the structure of task-specific evaluations like SWE-bench, which assess models against defined practical labor rather than general academic knowledge.

The benchmark tasks were built in collaboration with 14 partner startups. However, the report notes that the benchmark excludes the government and insurance sectors, and the underlying source does not explain how task scores were validated beyond the 14 partner startups.

SemiAnalysis plans to update the GDPx benchmark every 3 to 6 months. Future versions are expected to incorporate industry feedback and add new partner organizations to expand the testing framework.

Why it matters

The single-digit and low double-digit completion rates recorded on GDPx demonstrate that frontier AI systems remain far from providing autonomous professional labor in high-value industries. For organizations seeking to deploy generative models in specialized workflows such as chip design or finance, the low success rates necessitate structured human-in-the-loop validation frameworks to catch failures. As benchmark cadences shift toward practical commercial workflows rather than academic metrics, enterprise adoption will likely prioritize narrow task assistance and supervisory software rather than full end-to-end task replacement.

GDPx基准所反映的极低任务完成率表明，前沿AI模型短期内仍难以在高价值行业中实现完全自主的专业工作。对于计划在芯片设计、金融等专业工作流中部署前沿模型的企业而言，普遍偏低的任务成功率意味着必须建立严格的人工复核机制以防范错误。随着评估标准从传统学术测试转向商业任务完成度，各行业的落地策略预计将更倾向于特定环节的辅助工具与人机协作模式，而非完全端到端的自主化替代。

Les taux de réussite à un chiffre et dans le bas des chiffres doubles enregistrés sur GDPx démontrent que les systèmes d'IA de pointe sont encore loin de pouvoir fournir un travail professionnel autonome dans les secteurs à haute valeur ajoutée. Pour les organisations cherchant à déployer des modèles génératifs dans des flux de travail spécialisés tels que la conception de puces ou la finance, ces faibles taux de réussite imposent des cadres de validation structurés intégrant l'humain dans la boucle (« human-in-the-loop ») pour déceler les défaillances. À mesure que les cycles d'évaluation s'orientent vers des flux de travail commerciaux pratiques plutôt que vers des métriques académiques, l'adoption par les entreprises privilégiera probablement l'assistance sur des tâches ciblées et les logiciels de supervision plutôt que le remplacement intégral des tâches de bout en bout.

تثبت معدلات الإنجاز المسجلة في اختبار GDPx، والتي تراوحت بين خانة واحدة وخانات عشرية منخفضة، أن أنظمة الذكاء الاصطناعي الرائدة لا تزال بعيدة كل البعد عن تقديم عمل مهني مستقل في الصناعات عالية القيمة. وبالنسبة للمؤسسات التي تسعى إلى نشر النماذج التوليدية في مسارات عمل متخصصة مثل تصميم الرقائق أو التمويل، فإن معدلات النجاح المنخفضة تفرض وجود أطر عمل منظمة للمصادقة تعتمد على إبقاء العنصر البشري في حلقة العمليات لرصد الإخفاقات. ومع تحول وتيرة الاختبارات المعيارية نحو مسارات العمل التجارية العملية بدلاً من المقاييس الأكاديمية، فإن تبني المؤسسات لهذه التقنيات سيركز على الأرجح على المساعدة في المهام المحددة وتطوير برمجيات إشرافية، بدلاً من الاستبدال الشامل للمهام من البداية إلى النهاية.
