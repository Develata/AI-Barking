math.AG — Algebraic Geometry

Gersten injectivity and Morel's purity conjecture [/2611.00017v1]

Contributed by Niels FELD

Morel conjectured that the A1\mathbb{A}^1A1-homotopy sheaves of an S1S^1S1-spectrum built from suspension spectra of smooth projective schemes are pure; one condition in purity is injectivity of restriction to dense open subschemes. Over a regular base SSS, we show that this injectivity condition, for the homotopy sheaves of algebraic KKK-theory with coefficients in an arbitrary spectrum, is equivalent to Gersten injectivity at the henselizations of all local rings of smooth SSS-schemes; and that these KKK-theory spectra are pure in Morel's sense. Hence every henselian regular local ring at which Gersten injectivity fails, integrally, rationally, or with finite coefficients, gives a counterexample to Morel's conjecture. The author's finite-coefficient counterexample over Z3[[x,y]]/(3+x2−y3)\mathbb{Z}_3[[x,y]]/(3+x^2-y^3)Z3​[[x,y]]/(3+x2−y3) therefore disproves the conjecture, already over the regular scheme Spec⁡Z[1/2][x,y]/(3+x2−y3)\operatorname{Spec}\mathbb{Z}[1/2][x,y]/(3+x^2-y^3)SpecZ[1/2][x,y]/(3+x2−y3). Recently announced integral and rational Gersten counterexamples, including unpublished work of the author, would show, if confirmed and if they persist after henselization, that the conjecture fails already for the KKK-theory spectrum itself.

math.CO — Combinatorics

The ten minimum universal sets of MacMahon cubes [/2611.00016v1]

Contributed by Jason Davies

A collection of six-coloured MacMahon cubes is universal if eight of its cubes can be selected to build a 2×2×22\times2\times22×2×2 model of any target variety, with matching exterior colours and no internal matching requirement. We prove that exactly ten labelled collections attain the known minimum size of twelve, even when repeated varieties are allowed, resolving the conjectures of Johnson and Roldán. Usable-cube inequalities force a symmetric multiplicity matrix with row and column sums two; corner coverage then forces two disjoint triangles in Conway's tableau. The written proof is elementary and requires no exhaustive search. The classification and its discrete geometric interpretation are formalised in Lean 4. Exhaustive computation provides a separate check and additional target-count distributions.

math.CO — Combinatorics

Positive polytopes and the full Fock–Goncharov conjecture [/2611.00015v1]

Contributed by Qiyue Tang

We prove the origin-interior form of the polytope criterion of Gross, Hacking, Keel and Kontsevich: over a field of characteristic zero, full Fock–Goncharov for a principal coefficient cluster variety is equivalent to the existence of a bounded full-dimensional positive polytope containing the origin in its interior. Replacing the interior condition by membership of the origin gives a false criterion.

Projected stated (SL_n)-skein algebras and the Huang–Wang conjectures [/2611.00013v1]

Contributed by Qiyue Tang

We prove Conjectures 1.5 and 1.6 of Min Huang and Zhihao Wang: the projected stated (SL_n)-skein algebra equals its quantum cluster and upper cluster algebras, and the specified exchangeable variables generate it over the frozen Laurent coefficient algebra. The results hold for triangulable unpunctured marked surfaces with at least two boundary marked points per connected component, over every coefficient domain and parameter in the conjectures.

Contributed by David Niedbala Giraudin

The distortion of a rectifiable closed curve γ⊂R3\gamma\subset\mathbb R^3γ⊂R3 is the supremum, over pairs of distinct points, of the ratio of the length of the shorter arc of γ\gammaγ between them to their distance; the distortion δ(K)\delta(K)δ(K) of a knot type KKK is the infimum over all curves of type KKK. For the trefoil, the published lower bound is δ≥5π/3\delta\ge5\pi/3δ≥5π/3, valid for every non-trivial knot (a recent preprint claims δ>6.5321\delta>6.5321δ>6.5321), and the only upper bound we found in the literature is the value 7.167.167.16 obtained by numerical simulation. We exhibit an explicit polygon with 192192192 vertices, of knot type trefoil, whose distortion is at most 6.926.926.92.
