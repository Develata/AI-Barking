 Nov 15, 2026 
 Alibaba's Qwen 4 Max is the top Chinese model on the Vals Index 

We evaluated Alibaba’s new Qwen 4 Max [/models/alibaba_qwen4-max] on Vals Index v2.2 [/benchmarks/vals_index], its components and the Vals Finance Index [/benchmarks/finance_index].

#11 of 38 on the Vals Index [/benchmarks/vals_index] (63.20%), the highest-scoring Chinese model, ahead of Kimi K3.1 Pro [/models/kimi_kimi-k3.1-pro] (62.52%) and up from 59.05% for Qwen 3.8 Max 0902 [/models/alibaba_qwen3.8-max-0902].

Strong on finance: #8 of 41 on Finance Agent v2.1 [/benchmarks/fabv2-1] (66.68%) and #8 of 41 on the Vals Finance Index [/benchmarks/finance_index] (61.99%). It also ranks #13 of 86 on Tax Agent Bench [/benchmarks/tax_agent_bench] (40.35%).

On coding, it scores 86.66% on Vibe Code Bench [/benchmarks/vibe-code] (#15 of 129) and 48.49% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 [/benchmarks/terminal-bench-4] (#15 of 65).

#7 of 39 on HLAB-Vals-1.1 [/benchmarks/hlab] (21.25%), but weaker on EMB v1.1 [/benchmarks/emb-v1-1] (70.83%, #18 of 43) and Legal Research Bench [/benchmarks/legal_research] (41.35%, tied for #32 of 95).

Qwen 4 Max is a reasoning model with a 1M-token context window and 262k max output tokens. We evaluated it at xhigh reasoning effort, temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95 and top_k 20.

Congrats to the Qwen team on the release!
