Introducing Hy4 flash:
Ultimate cost-efficiency for everyday productivity
Tencent Cloud
OpenRouter
WorkBuddy
Yuanbao

We’re introducing Hy4 flash, our most cost-effective Hy model to date—featuring 224B total parameters (including 64B Engram parameters) with just 12B active parameters per token. Like Hy4, it is natively multimodal with a 1M-token context window, while offering significantly lower API pricing.

API pricing (per million tokens)	Cached input	Input	Output
Hy4 flash	$0.003	$0.070	$0.281
Hy4	$0.042	$0.834	$2.501

Across training, evaluation, and inference, we aggressively optimized for cost efficiency while preserving intelligence:

Pre-training: Hy4 flash integrates hybrid attention, LatentMoE, and Engram into its architecture—reducing KV cache size to just 1/24 of Hy3 and 1/6 of Hy4, with extensive scaling ladder experiments ensuring performance meets expectations.


Post-training: Self-distillation from Hy4 establishes strong general intelligence, while reinforcement learning cuts average end-to-end output tokens by 50%.


Evaluation: We built WorkIndex, an internal benchmark grounded in typical WorkBuddy tasks, and paired it with human evaluation—focusing on deliverable quality, single-turn output length, and task turnaround time rather than public benchmark scores detached from everyday work.


Inference: Combining megakernels, 4-bit quantization, and DFly pushes decoding speed past 150 tokens/s, enabling low-cost serving at scale.

Together, these gains bring Hy4 flash slightly above Hy4 preview in terms of overall task performance. On WorkIndex, its average cost per task is just 1/13 of Hy4 preview, 1/20 of Hy4, and 1/8 of DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 (Max tier, peak-hour pricing).

WorkIndex (internal)

Figure 1: Performance and cost across internal and external tasks. WorkIndex comprises 8 evaluation sets covering slide creation, slide editing, human-AI slide co-writing, Markdown human-AI co-writing, document editing, spreadsheet repair, audio/video processing, and visual understanding. Open Benchmarks includes 7 public agent benchmarks: DeepSWE, ProgramBench, Terminal-Bench 4.0, APEX-Agents, AutomationBench, HLE, and OSWorld 2.1.

 


Figure 2: Average cost per task on WorkIndex.

 

In a blind evaluation with 85 internal WorkBuddy users across 259 tasks spanning 27 categories, Hy4 flash achieved user satisfaction close to Claude Haiku 5.5, while recording the shortest end-to-end completion time and significantly lower cost per task than any other evaluated model.

Hy4 flash is now available across Tencent Cloud TokenHub, WorkBuddy, Yuanbao, WeCom, QQ, ima, Marvis, and more, and will launch on OpenRouter soon. Through November 30, users can access it for free in WorkBuddy and get 50% off API pricing via Tencent Cloud TokenHub.

We recommend Hy4 flash for most everyday productivity tasks—such as deep search, document processing, slide creation, simple frontend demos, and computer use—while continuing to recommend Hy4 for complex workloads like long-horizon software engineering for its higher intelligence ceiling. We also plan to release Hy4 flash as an open-weight model and ship frequent updates to continuously improve its utility and efficiency across real-world workflows.

 

In everyday office work, users care just as much about turnaround time and cost as they do about execution quality. In WorkBuddy’s evaluations, Hy4 flash cuts average task time by 70% at roughly 1/15 the cost of Hy4—matching Hy4’s quality on everyday tasks with only a slight dip on long-horizon coding workloads. Combined with its native multimodal capabilities, Hy4 flash is our go-to choice for balancing quality, speed, and cost.

In our static code scanning evaluations, Hy4 flash reduced scan completion time by 37% and token consumption by 46% compared to DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017, while matching its reasoning performance. Its outputs are also more concise and better at grounding decisions in contextual evidence.
