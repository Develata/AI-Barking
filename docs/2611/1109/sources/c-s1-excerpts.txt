Revenue
Revenue increased sequentially in each of the quarters presented, from $376 million for the three months 
ended March 31, 2025 to $11.6 billion for the three months ended June 30, 2026. The increases in each quarter 
were driven by successive model and product releases that drove higher usage, expansion, and broader 
adoption across our offerings, with both consumption-based and subscription-based revenue contributing. 
Growth in customers generating meaningful revenue contributed across the periods: customers with $100,000 
or more of revenue on a TTM basis grew from approximately 400 as of March 31, 2025 to more than 6,000 as 
of June 30, 2026.
Operating Expenses
Compute and Infrastructure
Compute and infrastructure expenses increased sequentially in each of the quarters presented, from $944 
million for the three months ended March 31, 2025 to $5.6 billion for the three months ended June 30, 2026, 
driven by the delivery of committed compute under our multi-year CSP arrangements. Because we typically 
procure compute 12 to 18 months in advance of availability, these expenses are generally fixed in the near term 
and increased as committed compute became available to us rather than in response to in-period demand.
