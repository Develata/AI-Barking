 Nov 9, 2026 
 Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 is #1 on the Vals Index 

We evaluated Anthropic’s new Claude Fable 5.2 [/models/anthropic_claude-fable-5-2] on Vals Index v2.2 [/benchmarks/vals_index], its components and the Vals Science Index [/benchmarks/science_index].

#1 of 36 on the Vals Index [/benchmarks/vals_index] (71.31%) at $17.94 per test, ahead of Claude Sonnet 5.5 [/models/anthropic_claude-sonnet-5-5] (70.85%) and Gemini 4 Carbon [/models/google_gemini-4-carbon] (70.45%).

#1 on four Vals Index components: Finance Agent v2.1 [/benchmarks/fabv2-1] (69.47%), Vibe Code Bench [/benchmarks/vibe-code] (93.77%), EMB v1.1 [/benchmarks/emb-v1-1] (77.38%) and, tied with Claude Opus 5.5 [/models/anthropic_claude-opus-5-5], Terminal-Bench 4.0 [/benchmarks/terminal-bench-4] (65.15%).

Also #1 of 66 on ProgramBench [/benchmarks/programbench] (19.00%, ahead of Opus 5.5 at 18.50%), and #2 of 22 on SWE-bench Multimodal [/benchmarks/swebench-multimodal] (62.29%), behind Opus 5.5 (70.21%).

Strong on science: #1 of 31 on BioMysteryBench [/benchmarks/biomysterybench] (83.70%), and #2 of 24 on the Vals Science Index [/benchmarks/science_index] (63.74%), behind GPT-6 Astra [/models/openai_gpt-6-astra] (65.09%).

Weaker on HLAB-Vals-1.1 [/benchmarks/hlab] (5.00%, tied #29 of 37) and Tax Agent Bench [/benchmarks/tax_agent_bench] (32.09%, #27 of 84).

Claude Fable 5.2 is a reasoning model with a 1M-token context window, priced at $8.00 per million input tokens and $40.00 per million output tokens. We evaluated it at High effort.

Congrats to the Anthropic team on the release!
