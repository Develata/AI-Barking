Updates

 Sep 22, 2026 
 Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 evaluated across our benchmark suite 

We evaluated Anthropic’s new Claude Opus 5.5 [/models/anthropic_claude-opus-5-5] across our benchmark suite.

Opus 5.5 places #1 of 63 on the Vals Index [/benchmarks/vals_index] (69.69%) at $32.77 per test, ahead of Claude Fable 5.1 [/models/anthropic_claude-fable-5-1] (68.83%), Claude Opus 5 [/models/anthropic_claude-opus-5] (67.21%) and GPT-6 Astra [/models/openai_gpt-6-astra] (66.61%).

It takes #1 on six leaderboards: Terminal-Bench 2.1 [/benchmarks/terminal-bench-2-1] (87.64%, ahead of GPT-6 Astra at 87.27%), Terminal-Bench 4.0 [/benchmarks/terminal-bench-4] (61.62%), ProgramBench [/benchmarks/programbench] (18.50% fully resolved), MedScribe [/benchmarks/medscribe] (91.43%), the Vals RSI Index [/benchmarks/rsi_index] (37.13%), and a perfect 100.00% on ProofBench v1.1 [/benchmarks/proof_bench], matching Fable 5.1 and the specialized prover AlephProver [https://logicalintelligence.com/].

It sits at #2 on Tax Agent Bench [/benchmarks/tax_agent_bench] (45.57%, out of 23 models, behind Fable 5.1 at 49.19%), Vibe Code Bench [/benchmarks/vibe-code] (90.29%, within noise of Claude Fable 5 [/models/anthropic_claude-fable-5]‘s leading 90.35%), IOI [/benchmarks/ioi] (95.06%), EMB [/benchmarks/emb] (75.94%, behind Fable 5.1 at 76.67%), MysteryMechanism [/benchmarks/mysterymechanism] (49.55%), Terminal-Bench-Science [/benchmarks/terminal-bench-science] (48.57%) and SRE Bench [/benchmarks/srebench] (33.59%), and matches GPT-6 Astra’s leading 79.26% on BioMysteryBench [/benchmarks/biomysterybench].

Mid-board on Public Benefits Bench [/benchmarks/public-benefits-bench] (70.64%, #3 of 38), Legal Research Bench [/benchmarks/legal_research] (50.48%, #4 of 63) and Finance Agent v2 [/benchmarks/fabv2] (58.59%, #8 of 63).

Agentic legal work remains the weak spot: 3.75% on Harvey’s Legal Agent Benchmark [/benchmarks/hlab] (#31 of 64). It also lands below Opus 5 on MedCode [/benchmarks/medcode] (49.80%, #15 of 95) and SAGE [/benchmarks/sage] (45.83%, #33 of 83).

We ran Opus 5.5 with Claude Opus 5 [/models/anthropic_claude-opus-5] and Claude Opus 4.8 [/models/anthropic_claude-opus-4-8] as server-side fallbacks for refusals. Counting fallback-assisted tasks as failures changes Terminal-Bench 2.1 from 87.64% to 79.77% (26 of 267 tasks), Terminal-Bench 4.0 from 61.62% to 53.54% (30 of 198), Vibe Code Bench from 90.29% to 83.34% (4 of 50), Terminal-Bench-Science from 48.57% to 47.14%, and MysteryMechanism from 49.55% to 49.10%. The effect is largest on SRE Bench, where 217 of 262 tasks (82.82%) were fallback-assisted and the score falls from 33.59% to 5.34%. Legal Research Bench saw 11 refusals with no fallback, which did not change its score.

Opus 5.5 is priced at $4.00 per million input tokens and $20.00 per million output tokens. The model has a 1M-token context window and 128k max output tokens. Evaluations were run with compute effort set to “max” except Terminal-Bench 2.1, which used “high” effort, and temperature set to 1.0.

Congrats to the Anthropic team on the release!
