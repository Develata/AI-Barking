Updates

 Sep 28, 2026 
 Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 5.5 evaluated across our benchmark suite 

We evaluated Anthropic’s new Claude Sonnet 5.5 [/models/anthropic_claude-sonnet-5-5] across our benchmark suite.

Sonnet 5.5 places #2 of 66 on the Vals Index [/benchmarks/vals_index] (69.22%) at $20.80 per test, 0.47 points behind Claude Opus 5.5 [/models/anthropic_claude-opus-5-5] (69.69%) at under two-thirds of its $32.77 per test, and ahead of Claude Fable 5.1 [/models/anthropic_claude-fable-5-1] (68.83%) and Claude Opus 5 [/models/anthropic_claude-opus-5] (67.21%).

It takes #1 on three leaderboards: Vibe Code Bench [/benchmarks/vibe-code] (92.39%, ahead of Claude Fable 5 [/models/anthropic_claude-fable-5] at 90.35%), Code Migration [/benchmarks/code-migration] (69.83%, ahead of GPT-6 Astra [/models/openai_gpt-6-astra] at 67.74%) and BioMysteryBench [/benchmarks/biomysterybench] (81.11%, ahead of a three-way tie at 79.26%), and scores a perfect 100.00% on ProofBench v1.1 [/benchmarks/proof_bench], matching Opus 5.5, Fable 5.1 and AlephProver [https://logicalintelligence.com/].

It sits at #3 on EMB [/benchmarks/emb] (75.71% of 66), MedScribe [/benchmarks/medscribe] (91.10% of 104), Terminal-Bench 4.0 [/benchmarks/terminal-bench-4] (53.03% of 37), Terminal-Bench-Science [/benchmarks/terminal-bench-science] (38.57% of 33) and MysteryMechanism [/benchmarks/mysterymechanism] (49.10% of 19), and #4 of 14 on SRE Bench [/benchmarks/srebench] (30.15%).

Further top-ten finishes on Tax Agent Bench [/benchmarks/tax_agent_bench] (41.96%, #5 of 61), Terminal-Bench 2.1 [/benchmarks/terminal-bench-2-1] (83.15%, tied #6 of 75), IOI [/benchmarks/ioi] (83.06%, #7 of 36), Legal Research Bench [/benchmarks/legal_research] (48.08%, tied #7 of 69), Finance Agent v2 [/benchmarks/fabv2] (58.10%, #9 of 70) and SAGE [/benchmarks/sage] (51.77%, #10 of 88).

Mid-board on MedCode [/benchmarks/medcode] (52.92%, #11 of 102) and Public Benefits Bench [/benchmarks/public-benefits-bench] (67.19%, #12 of 43). Weaker on CyberBench [/benchmarks/cyber] (59.58%, #31 of 41) and Harvey’s Legal Agent Benchmark [/benchmarks/hlab] (2.92%, tied #35 of 70).

We ran Sonnet 5.5 with Claude Sonnet 5 [/models/anthropic_claude-sonnet-5] as a server-side fallback for refusals. Counting fallback-assisted tasks as failures changes Terminal-Bench 2.1 from 83.15% to 80.52% (10 of 267 tasks), Terminal-Bench 4.0 from 53.03% to 50.51% (7 of 198) and the Vals Index from 69.22% to 68.89%. The effect is largest on security work: CyberBench falls from 59.58% to 41.97% (54 of 116 tasks), and SRE Bench from 30.15% to 19.08% (126 of 262). Refusals that the fallback also refused are scored as failures, including 41 SRE Bench tasks and nine BioMysteryBench attempts.

Sonnet 5.5 is priced at $2.00 per million input tokens and $10.00 per million output tokens. The model has a 1M-token context window and 128k max output tokens. Evaluations were run with compute effort set to “max” except Terminal-Bench 2.1, which used “high” effort, and temperature set to 1.0.

Congrats to the Anthropic team on the release!
