Hexagon is a repository for the preservation, dissemination, study, and evaluation of research-level works in the mathematical sciences and theoretical computer science, including both original and expository texts. Hexagon does not perform peer review. Every deposit is screened automatically and, during the beta, also reviewed by a human moderator before it appears; acceptance is not a judgment of correctness, quality, or novelty.
