The Public Standard for
 Real-World AI Benchmarks

Generic benchmarks only go so far.
 Vals AI evaluates models on the
 real tasks each industry relies on.

Vals AI runs 40 independent AI benchmarks across legal,
 finance, healthcare, coding, math, and more. Each leaderboard compares
 large language models (LLMs) on accuracy, cost, and latency, and each methodology
 page explains how the benchmark is run.

 As of November 9, 2026, Claude Fable 5.2 [/models/anthropic_claude-fable-5-2] ranks first on the Vals Index with 71.31%, followed by Claude Sonnet 5.5 [/models/anthropic_claude-sonnet-5-5] (70.85%) and Gemini 4 Carbon [/models/google_gemini-4-carbon] (70.45%). See the full Vals Index leaderboard [/benchmarks/vals_index].
