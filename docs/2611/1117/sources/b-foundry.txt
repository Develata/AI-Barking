Prices are estimates only and are not intended as actual price quotes. Actual pricing may vary depending on the type of agreement entered with Microsoft, date of purchase, and the currency exchange rate. Prices are calculated based on US dollars and converted using London closing spot rates that are captured in the two business days prior to the last business day of the previous month end. If the two business days prior to the end of the month fall on a bank holiday in major markets, the rate setting day is generally the day immediately preceding the two business days. This rate applies to all transactions during the upcoming month. Sign in to the Azure pricing calculator [/en-us/pricing/calculator/] to see pricing based on your current program/offer with Microsoft. Frequently asked questions

 Frequently asked questions about Azure pricing [/en-us/pricing/]

 Is Foundry Free?

The Foundry platform is free to use and explore. Individual features customers access and consume are billed at their normal rates.

 Do I need an Azure account to use Foundry?

You do not need an Azure account to explore Foundry. However, before you can start building agents, you will need an Azure subscription.

 How is Foundry billed?

Each service and product available in Foundry has its own billing model and price.

 What is the Microsoft Agent pre‑purchase plan and how does it work?

The Microsoft Agent pre‑purchase plan allows you to save on Microsoft Foundry and Copilot Credit costs by purchasing Agent Commit Units (ACUs) up front in a 1‑year metered plan at discounted tiers and using them flexibly across the included Microsoft services.

 What is AI Flex Plan1, and can I use it for Foundry?

Yes. AI Flex Plan is a new, simpler way to use services across the Microsoft AI portfolio. It's one commitment you can use across eligible Microsoft AI services, including Microsoft Foundry, Microsoft Copilot , GitHub Copilot, Microsoft Security Copilot and Microsoft IQ. You can shift your usage between services as your priorities change. Buying is simpler and more predictable, and FinOps for AI helps you plan, manage and optimize your AI spend with confidence.

For Foundry, eligible usage includes Foundry Models, Foundry Agent Service, Foundry Tools, Azure Machine Learning, Foundry Control Plane, Foundry Local and Foundry IQ, at the rates shown on this page. Choose a 1- or 3-year term and pay monthly or up front. You don't need a MACC or Microsoft 365 E7 to get started.

To learn more contact sales [https://azure.microsoft.com/contact/pricing/#contact-sales]

 Does AI Flex Plan1 change the prices on this page?

No. Each service keeps its own prices and meters. AI Flex Plan only changes how you commit to and pay for that usage.

To learn more contact sales [https://azure.microsoft.com/contact/pricing/#contact-sales]

 How is AI Flex Plan1 different from a pre-purchase plan (P3)?

With a pre-purchase plan, you buy units ahead of time for a set list of services. AI Flex Plan is one commitment across the broader Microsoft AI portfolio. You can pay monthly or up front, and you can shift your usage between services as your priorities change.

To learn more contact sales [https://azure.microsoft.com/contact/pricing/#contact-sales]
