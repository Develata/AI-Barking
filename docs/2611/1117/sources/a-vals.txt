 OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra is #1 on the Vals Science Index 

We evaluated OpenAI’s new GPT-6.2 Astra [/models/openai_gpt-6.2-astra] on Vals Index v2.2 [/benchmarks/vals_index], its components and the Vals Science Index [/benchmarks/science_index].

#7 of 40 on the Vals Index [/benchmarks/vals_index] (67.66%) at $10.68 per test.

#1 of 27 on the Vals Science Index [/benchmarks/science_index] (68.44%), including #1 of 47 on Terminal-Bench-Science [/benchmarks/terminal-bench-science] (71.43%).

On science components, it scores 80.74% on BioMysteryBench [/benchmarks/biomysterybench] and 53.15% on MysteryMechanism [/benchmarks/mysterymechanism].

On coding and finance, it scores 63.13% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 [/benchmarks/terminal-bench-4] and 76.03% on EMB v1.1 [/benchmarks/emb-v1-1].

GPT-6.2 Astra has a 1M-token context window and 128k max output tokens. We evaluated it at max reasoning effort.

Congrats to the OpenAI team on the release!
