Good at coding, or good at DeepSWE?

DeepSWE uses original engineering tasks to test how well models can build software. But once those tasks and their solutions are public, they can become tuning material, and developers can optimize against the same problems again and again. A rising score should mean a model is better prepared for your next project—even when the requirements look a little different.

MaxxBench changes the instructions or starting code of 89 DeepSWE tasks while keeping the main engineering problem. That connection lets us examine how models handle the change: whether they revise their approach, overlook a new requirement, or fall back on a solution that no longer fits. The feature still needs to work. Can the model make the adjustment?
