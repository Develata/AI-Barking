Goldman, Morgan Stanley Take Top Banking Roles in Anthropic IPO

Oct. 26, 2026, 6:40 PM UTC

 Anthony Hughes 

 [/author/anthony-hughes-24511219]

Goldman Sachs Group Inc. and Morgan Stanley scored the top roles on Anthropic PBC’s upcoming initial public offering, taking the same positions they had for SpaceX’s record-shattering $86.2 billion listing in June.

As with the SpaceX IPO, Goldman was named in the coveted lead left position, while Morgan Stanley was named second and will act as the stabilization agent, according to Anthropic’s S-1 filing.
