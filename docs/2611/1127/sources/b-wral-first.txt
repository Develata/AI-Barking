Raleigh woman says company's AI agent confused her over rent. Now she's evicted.

A leasing office told a Raleigh woman she could pay her rent at the end of November, but when she received an eviction notice on Monday and called the leasing office, the leasing agent blamed it on a mistake by the company's AI agent and said the woman would be evicted on Thursday.

 By Tolly Taylor, WRAL investigative reporter

An AI-driven nightmare unfolded for a Raleigh woman who thought she had until the end of the month to pay her back rent.

Laura Mack received emails from an AI assistant at the leasing office informing her about her association. Still, an eviction notice showed up.

When an email from Mack's leasing office said she could pay her rent at the end of the month, she was relieved. 

Days later, that relief turned to confusion, followed by anger and, eventually, desperation.

Mack was told she had until the end of November to pay her overdue rent, and she has the email from her leasing office to prove it. 

Now, she's at the courthouse fighting to avoid eviction. 

"I'm baffled," Mack said. "I need a place to stay."

Here's what WRAL Investigates has learned about Mack's situation.

On Nov. 8, she received an email asking, "What date works for you to pay the full $3,934.17?"

The email asking about her rent is signed by "Colony Townhome Apartments."

"I responded, 'I'd like to pay on the last day of the month.'"

Mack thought the leasing agents were being understanding because it took them almost two weeks to fix a severe leak in her bathroom ceiling earlier this month.

The email response she got back that same day said "Paying on November 30, the last day of the month, works."

"I thought it was someone from the office because all my information was in there and it mentioned my bathroom," Mack said.

Mack said she did not receive any communication between Nov. 8 and Nov. 16.

On Monday, she received an eviction notice. When Mack called the leasing office, she said the leasing agent told her that Olivia, the company's AI agent, had emailed her both times on Nov. 8.

It was Olivia who mistakenly gave her permission to delay her rent payment until Nov. 30.

Mack said she was reeling at learning her new fate.

"I'm sad," she said. "Like, I didn't even have time -- I'm still ordering things to pack."

Back in December 2025, the leasing office first notified all residents that Olivia, their new do-it-all AI agent, could now handle their rent and maintenance questions 24/7.

But as Mack's email account shows, emails from Olivia are usually clearly marked.

The emails Mack received on Nov. 8 never identified that they were from Olivia, included Mack's personal details and were signed "Colony Townhome Apartments".

"If you told me till the 30th, I'll get out by the 30th," Mack said. "But give me that. You at least honor that. You have to have somewhere where you're taking responsibility."

Mack went to the Wake County courthouse Thursday morning, where she attempted to reverse that eviction notice. WRAL News called and emailed Colony Townhome Apartments for comment but did not hear back immediately.
