 Lawmaker Blasts Eviction Tied to AI Bot Error

 Bot told NC woman she had until the end of the month to pay, which wasn't true

 By
 Arden Dier [/bio/83590798/arden-dier.html]
 withNewser.AI [/policyai.aspx]

 Posted Nov 24, 2026 11:00 AM CST

 [sms:?body=I saw this Newser story and thought of you. Read it!%0d%0a%0d%0aLawmaker Blasts Eviction Tied to AI Bot Error%0d%0a%0d%0ahttps://www.newser.com/story/400555/lawmaker-blasts-eviction-tied-to-ai-bot-error.html]

 Copied

 (Getty Images/No-Mad)

 A Raleigh woman says she received an email from her landlord indicating she had time to pay her overdue rent. Instead, she was evicted from her home Thursday, with the landlord blaming the misunderstanding on an AI chatbot. As WRAL [https://www.wral.com/news/investigates/raleigh-eviction-after-ai-bots-promise-push-for-protection-nov-2026/] reports, the Nov. 8 email Laura Mack had received, stating she had until the end of the month to cover nearly $4,000 in unpaid rent, had been sent by "Olivia," an AI-powered property management service used by the Colony Townhome Apartments' leasing office.

 Though the apartment complex informed residents that it would be using an AI agent beginning in December 2025, there was nothing in the email to indicate it was written by "Olivia." Indeed, Mack says sympathetic language made her think she was conversing with a human. State Sen. Jim Burgin says "ridiculous" cases like these are behind his push to revive his stalled "Bot Bill," which would require companies to disclose when consumers are interacting with AI. Mack says she takes responsibility for the missed payments and simply wants the leasing office to take responsibility for its contradictory messaging.
