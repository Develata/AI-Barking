 VP != VNP: “I do not complain, the antelope falls with the arrow” 

It was just announced a proof of the most famous open problem in algebraic complexity: VP is not equal to VNP: https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/blob/main/vp-vnp/paper.pdf [https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/blob/main/vp-vnp/paper.pdf]

The human-readable part of the paper is very short and mostly points to the Lean formalization. Looking in the future, you can see that some papers will be an even shorter human-readable fragment, accompanied by lots of computer code; I also see that we will get to non-asymptotic results, proving complexity bound for precise systems in use, which will be amazing.

So now we have seen both new upper and lower bounds. I still believe we will see more surprises in terms of upper bounds. What other conjectures will be solved this week? I asked AI it gave me some very boring answers.
