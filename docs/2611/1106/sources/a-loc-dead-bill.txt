What happens to a bill that has not become law at the end of a Congress? 

If a bill from any Congress [https://www.congress.gov/help/legislative-glossary#glossary_congress] does not become law during the Congress in which it is introduced, it is considered “dead.” For a “dead” bill to be enacted in a new Congress, it would have to be reintroduced with a new number and begin anew its journey through the legislative process [https://www.congress.gov/legislative-process?loclr=askfaq].
