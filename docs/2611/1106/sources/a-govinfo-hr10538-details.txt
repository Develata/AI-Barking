 Action

Mr. Casar (for himself, Mr. Khanna, Ms. Mejia, Ms. Ansari, Ms. Hoyle of Oregon, Mr. Lynch, Mr. García of Illinois, Mr. Deluzio, Mrs. Grijalva, and Ms. Velázquez) introduced the following bill; which was referred to the Committee on Oversight and Government Reform

H.R. 10538

 Bill Version

Introduced in House
 (IH)

 Short Title

Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act of 2026

 Full Title

To establish the Department of Artificial Intelligence and to provide for the regulation of advanced artificial intelligence systems, and for other purposes.
