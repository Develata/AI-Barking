Scaling expertise

doubleAI is a deep frontier lab, focused on the hardest problems in science and engineering. Our goal is expertise that scales without limits.

Search in the space of ideas

The hardest problems demand the deepest search. Our AI searches the space of ideas with scientific rigor and intuition, backtracking when a path fails and building on past results. Our system is designed to compound experience into expertise.

Verification

Verification is the missing piece. Where it exists, as in mathematics, our AI already solves problems that have stood for decades. We are bringing formal verification to code, opening new product horizons by allowing AI to run code intelligently on hardware. Our goal is to bring reliable verification to all scientific domains, so expertise can scale without limits.
