Using ChatGPT for accounting work

Learn how to set up ChatGPT for accounting work and review the results.

Updated: 10 hours ago

Overview

ChatGPT can help you with common accounting tasks, such as closing the books for your business or your clients. It can bring together records from your spreadsheets and connected accounting apps to prepare drafts for your review. Use the Accounting plugin to give ChatGPT guidance for recurring accounting work - for example, you can ask it to draft a month-end report in your usual format and show the records behind the figures.

Workspace setup

ChatGPT Business members can install Accounting without prior admin approval. In an Enterprise workspace, an admin must approve Accounting before members can install it. In the admin console [https://admin.openai.com], open Plugins, find Accounting, review its included apps, and approve the plugin for your workspace.

Set up your first task

Open the ChatGPT desktop app [https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/app] in your workspace.

Open the plugin directory [https://chatgpt.com/plugins], search for Accounting, and select Install plugin if it’s available to you. The Accounting plugin is free to install. Access to the model, tools, and usage included with your plan can vary.

Choose your sources. Upload an Excel template and the relevant files, or connect an accounting app. If prompted, sign in to an account with access to the company’s records.

Follow the computer use setup instructions [https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/computer-use].

Tell ChatGPT what you need, which company and period to use, and which rules to follow.

Available partner plugins include BILL, Campfire, FreshBooks, Oracle NetSuite, Oracle QuickStart, QuickBooks Online, Rillet, and Xero.

Open the specific listing to check its supported actions and account requirements. A connection that lets you read transactions may have different permissions for entering records. For installation and authorization details, see Plugins in ChatGPT [/en/articles/20001256].

Use cases

Reconcile transactions and payments

Provide a bank statement or payment export and the corresponding ledger for the same period. Ask ChatGPT to list proposed matches separately from possible duplicates, missing transactions, or differences in fees or currency. Ask for the source row and amount on each side so you can investigate the exceptions.

Prepare invoice and receipt entries

Provide invoices or receipts from your files or connected email, along with the chart of accounts or coding rules. Ask for the vendor, invoice number, amount, suggested account, and a reference to the original document. Ask ChatGPT to flag missing information or uncertain coding.

Compare the proposed entries with the original invoices. If you use an app that supports entry, review the company, destination, and amounts before approving. If an entry’s status is unclear, check the accounting app before trying again to avoid creating a duplicate.

Build a month-end or client report

Share your existing Excel report template and the records for the current and comparison periods. Ask ChatGPT to keep your layout, explain material changes, and point to the workpapers behind each explanation. Check the calculations, assumptions, and commentary before sharing the report.

Gather audit support

Provide the request list and the supporting schedules or documents you’re authorized to use. Ask ChatGPT to match each item to its evidence and list anything missing. Review the links and follow up on gaps before handing the package to your auditor.

Repeat a reviewed workflow

If your workspace has access to skills [/en/articles/20001066], save the report format, coding rules, and review checks that worked for you. For the next run, provide the new period and source records. Check the company and account again when switching clients.

Troubleshoot a missing app or unexpected result

If you can’t find Accounting, check the selected workspace and plugin listing. Ask a workspace administrator whether the required app is enabled.

If records are missing, check which provider account and company you connected and whether you can open the item there. You can also provide an authorized file export for a preparation task.

If figures differ, ask for the records and assumptions behind the result. Check dates, currency, fees, and possible duplicates before approving an entry or report.

For connection errors or an action that still fails, follow Troubleshooting plugins and apps in ChatGPT [/en/articles/20001497].
