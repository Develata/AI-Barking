More time for the work that needs you

Cut down on manual entry

Prepare draft entries from invoices and receipts, with suggested account coding and unclear details flagged for review.

Spend less time gathering records

Bring together invoices, statements, and payment records from your email, files, and accounting apps for reconciliation and reporting.

Check your work for errors

Compare workpapers with supporting records and draft review notes on possible duplicates, missing information, and unexplained differences for you to investigate.

Reuse your process each month

Save your templates, instructions, and review checks as reusable skills, then apply them to each month’s new records.

Put ChatGPT to work on everyday accounting

Start with a task you know, use the records you already have, and review the results.

Your business data stays yours

Data from ChatGPT Business and Enterprise isn’t used to train OpenAI’s models by default.

Control access to connected apps

Workspace admins choose which apps are enabled. Users connect their own accounts, and ChatGPT respects their existing app permissions.

You make the final call
