How the ranking works

 Methodology [methodology]

 OBJ_01 HUMAN_REF 0.98
 OBJ_02 JUDGED 0.94
 OBJ_03 RUBRIC 0.94

Real jobs

Each job is a real client brief from Contra’s marketplace with its files, the client renamed. Every model on the round builds a working website for it, and its golden, a design a person picked, is set beside the outputs.

Blind review

Reviewers see two outputs for the same brief side by side and pick the better one, without seeing which model made which. The sides are shuffled for every pair.

Rubric

We curated 35 design rubrics from widely acknowledged design principles. They catch common mistakes, such as too many fonts or inconsistent spacing, so a score says what to fix. Across the models, rubric scores track the designers’ ranking (r = 0.94).
