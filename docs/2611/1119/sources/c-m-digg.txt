Contra Labs' design leaderboard gives stealth model Gestalt the gold over human references

On Contra Labs' leaderboard, Gestalt scores 1,931 Elo against 1,739 for the human reference designs.

TLDR

Gestalt, labeled “stealth,” tops Contra Labs' Frontier Design Benchmark, with human reference designs second and Claude Fable 5.2 third. Contra says its evaluation draws on 33 real client briefs, 68 professional designers and 4,311 blind votes. That's a leaderboard win, not a blanket verdict on human creativity.
