Usually I do it when one of them is stumped on their own, or seems to be thrashing. I guess it's more like... adjacent to the main loop.
I'll spin up both TUI's in herdr, tell them both to run "hc --skill" and then talk to each other until they figure out a solution. Before herdr I had them use tmux and files to talk to each other. That works too, but with a little more fumbling.
