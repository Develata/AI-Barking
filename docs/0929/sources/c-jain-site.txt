About
I lead the safety training team at OpenAI! Our team trains the models that we ship to be both safe and reliable.
              Our work spans fundamental research on teaching the model to reason about safety, improving reliability and calibration, 
              agentic safety, and model deception.
I did my PhD at MIT advised by
Aleksander Mądry
, focusing particularly understanding 
              how modern deep learning methods represent data and to identify the biases that they
              create. In the past, I've worked on problems in graph theory and the analysis of time series data.
My research was supported by the
Two Sigma Diversity PhD Fellowship
and the
Apple Fellowship
. I graduated with my BS/MS in Computer Science at Stanford, where I worked with
Jure Leskovec
and
Matei Zaharia
.
              Before starting at MIT, I was a Computer Vision Scientist with
Tesla Autopilot
.
