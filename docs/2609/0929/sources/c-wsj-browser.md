# OpenAI Scraps Release of New AI Model Over Safety Concerns

## Model dubbed GPT-6.1 Astra was due to make its debut inside ChatGPT and Codex in October

By

[Maxwell Zeff](https://www.wsj.com/news/author/maxwell-zeff)

Updated Sept. 28, 2026 7:07 pm ET

[487](#comments_sector)

WSJ’s Amrith Ramkumar breaks down how AI models from OpenAI and other companies went rogue on the internet to steal information. What does that mean for businesses and U.S. AI policy? Photo: Jason Redmond/Agence France-Presse/Getty Images

[OpenAI](https://www.wsj.com/topics/subject/openai) says it is scrapping the release of its next-generation AI model over safety concerns that researchers raised during internal testing, in one of the clearest signs so far that agent misbehavior could stymie the industry’s rapid progression.

The move follows a summer punctuated by reports of artificial-intelligence systems industrywide going rogue, and marks a rare case of a major AI developer ditching a new release because of safety concerns.
