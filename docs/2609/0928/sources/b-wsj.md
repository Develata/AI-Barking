# Top AI Researchers Call for Urgent Oversight of Self-Improving Systems

## Leaders at OpenAI, Anthropic, Microsoft and Meta say AI could soon self-improve faster than humans can keep up with

By

[Maxwell Zeff](https://www.wsj.com/news/author/maxwell-zeff)

[](#comments_sector)

![Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark](https://images.wsj.net/im-32767293?width=700&height=466)

Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark is among the co-authors of a paper warning about risks of automating AI research. brendan smialowski/AFP/Getty Images

Research leaders at [OpenAI](https://www.wsj.com/topics/subject/openai), Anthropic, [Meta Platforms](/market-data/quotes/META) [META \-3.96%decrease; down pointing triangle](/market-data/quotes/META) and [Microsoft](/market-data/quotes/MSFT) [MSFT \-0.86%decrease; down pointing triangle](/market-data/quotes/MSFT) are asking policymakers to probe the degree to which their companies have automated artificial-intelligence research, joining industrywide calls for greater oversight of the fast-evolving technology.

In [a paper published Monday](https://casp.ac/reports/intelligence-explosion), researchers including OpenAI Chief Scientist Jakub Pachocki, Anthropic co-founder Jack Clark, Microsoft Chief Scientific Officer Eric Horvitz and Meta’s Vice President of AI Research Dawn Song wrote that automating AI research could set off an “intelligence explosion.” Such a development could compress years of progress into months or less and outpace humans’ ability to understand it, the authors said.
