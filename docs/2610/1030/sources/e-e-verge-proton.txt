Proton now won’t “re-interview” staff, actually.

This week, 404 Media reported that [https://www.404media.co/proton-to-re-interview-staff-about-ai-to-see-who-gets-to-keep-their-job/] Proton would be “re-interviewing” staff to ask about how they use AI at work and published an investigation [https://www.404media.co/manifestos-fear-and-loser-thinking-inside-protons-ai-reckoning/] into the company’s push into AI, driven in part by CEO Andy Yen.

But today, 404 Media reports that [https://www.404media.co/manifestos-fear-and-loser-thinking-inside-protons-ai-reckoning/] Yen is canceling the interviews. There will be a reorganization, but Yen said that “there will be no loss of jobs from AI.”
