The GPT-6.1 Terra release offers 6 models, each with different intelligence, performance, and pricing characteristics. Below is a comparison of the key metrics across the 6 models.

For intelligence, the top model of GPT-6.1 Terra is GPT-6.1 Terra (Max) at 48.

For output speed, the fastest model is GPT-6.1 Terra (Non-reasoning) at 91 t/s.

For latency, GPT-6.1 Terra (Non-reasoning) at 1.07s offers the lowest time to first answer token.

For pricing, GPT-6.1 Terra (Low) at $0.05 offers the lowest cost per task. Prices vary up to 7.4x across models.
