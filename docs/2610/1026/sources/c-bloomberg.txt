Takeaways by Bloomberg AI

Microsoft Corp. [https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/MSFT:US] will offer buyouts to about 60,000 workers, representing roughly a quarter of its total staff, as part of the company’s efforts to trim costs and retool its workforce for the artificial intelligence era.

Some personnel in the US, Canada, India and the UK at a vice president level and below will be eligible, said a Microsoft spokesperson.
