Gemini Apps limits & upgrades for Google AI subscribers
Gemini Apps have compute-based usage limits that determine how much you can interact with Gemini tools and features. These limits factor in the complexity of your prompt, the models and features you use, and the length of your chat. Your limit refreshes every 5 hours until you reach your weekly limit. 

You can upgrade to a Google AI plan for expanded access to features and models in Gemini Apps. Gemini Apps upgrades are part of select Google One paid plans for personal accounts. Learn more about availalble subscriptions.

Important: This article is for users managing Gemini Apps for a personal Google Account. If you’re using a work or school Google Account, learn more about using Gemini Apps with a work or school account.

What you need
To use Gemini Apps with a Google AI plan in most countries, you must be 13 (or the applicable age in your country) or over.
To use Gemini Apps with a Google AI plan in the European Economic Area, Switzerland, or the United Kingdom, you must be 18 years of age or older.
Some features may not be available for users under the age of 18 or 13 (or the applicable age in your country). In the help center, you will find additional information about age requirements.

Gemini app access and limits

Important:

Gemini Apps limits may change. Access is subject to change or may be limited based on testing, experimentation, or availability. 
Gemini Apps usage limits are designed to ensure an optimal experience for everyone. This means we may at times have to cap the number of prompts and conversations you can have, or how much you can use some features, within a specific timeframe.

About Gemini 3 models

Important: Model names, versions, and availability may change.

Gemini has the following available models:

Gemini Flash-Lite is an efficient workhorse model designed for speed. It's ideal for everyday tasks like summarization and brainstorming. 
Gemini Flash is a more powerful model that balances speed and reasoning to solve a large variety of problems, from simple to complex.
Gemini Pro is our most advanced model that excels at complex math and coding prompts with high performance and reasoning. Pro has a deeper understanding across text, files, images and videos, plus major upgrades for coding and studying. Generally, response time for Gemini 3 Pro prompts will take longer than if using other models.
Thinking level

Where available, you can adjust the thinking level for Gemini models. 

Standard thinking is the default and best for most questions. With Standard thinking, responses will be generally faster.
Extended thinking is best for complex problem solving. With Extended thinking, the model will reason over your prompt longer before responding.
Deep think (AI Ultra only) provides maximum parallel reasoning. Deep Think queries generally can take a few minutes before seeing a response. Deep think requires the Pro model.

Tip: More advanced models and higher thinking levels consume more of your usage.

Context windows

When you upload files to Gemini Apps, it needs to "read" the content to provide useful insights or answers. The context window is like the reading capacity. It determines how much content can be understood together at one time. A larger context window allows Gemini Apps to work with longer prompts and chats.

Plan	Context window
Without an AI plan	32k tokens
AI Plus	128k tokens
AI Pro & AI Ultra	1 million tokens
