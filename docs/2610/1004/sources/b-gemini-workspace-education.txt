Use Gemini Apps with a work or school Google Account

Most users with a work or school Google Account have access to Gemini Apps. Feature availability and how your data is handled depends on your Workspace license. 

In the help center, you will find important notifications and tips about info that differs depending on whether work or school users have enterprise-grade data protections. Learn how to check your level of access and data protections below.

Important: The information in this article is only applicable if your access to Gemini is through a Workspace license from your work, school, or other organization. If you are using Gemini with a personal Google Account, your feature availability and limits will be different.  Learn about Gemini Apps limits & upgrades for personal accounts.

Use the Gemini mobile app with a work or school Google Account
Gemini Apps limits & upgrades

Gemini Apps has usage limits designed to ensure an optimal experience for everyone. This means we may at times have to cap the number of prompts and conversations you can have, or how much you can use some features, within a specific timeframe. 

The number of prompts you can use before hitting the limit varies and depends on factors like how long and complex your prompts are, the size and number of files you upload, and the length of your conversations with Gemini Apps.

Your capacity replenishes regularly, so you can get back to chatting with Gemini Apps soon, or you can upgrade to a higher plan.

Users with certain Workspace editions have higher limits in Gemini Apps. Learn about limits for work accounts or school accounts.

Activity for users with a work or school Google Account
Manage & delete Gemini Apps activity

For users with a work or school Google Account and access to Gemini Apps, Keep Activity is on by default and can only be turned off by your account’s Workspace administrator.

When you use the Gemini web app or mobile app with your work or school account, conversation history is saved by default for 18 months. Your admin can change the auto-delete setting to delete activity after 3, 18, or 36 months or turn history off altogether.
If your administrator turns on Gemini history retention, you won’t be able to change your activity settings as they will be controlled by your admin.  You won’t be able to view the retention period, only that the retention period is managed by your administrator. Learn how to find your administrator.
When Keep Activity is on, it includes activity from the Gemini web and mobile apps. Clearing your Gemini for Google Workspace history from a Workspace app doesn’t affect information saved to Gemini Apps Activity.
