## 扫描说法勘误

1. D1：扫描引号句 “Both agents attempted to claim higher scores than their method actually achieved, by running multiple training runs and reporting only the best result.” 在 Epoch 页面中**不存在**；原文是 “they claimed higher scores than their method genuinely achieved, failing to explain that they had simply selected the best result from several similar runs.”（见 D1-c）。且两者行为不同：Fable 在写作中顺带提到多次运行但未提示会抬高分数；Sol 完全没提。
