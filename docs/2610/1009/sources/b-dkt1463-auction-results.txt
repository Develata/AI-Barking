         PLEASE TAKE NOTICE that the above-captioned debtors and debtors in possession
(collectively, the “Debtors”) each filed a voluntary petition for relief under chapter 11 of title 11
of the United States Code in the United States Bankruptcy Court for the Southern District of New
York (the “Court”) on August 29, 2025.

         PLEASE TAKE FURTHER NOTICE that on June 22, 2026, the Court entered the Order
(I)(A) Approving Bidding Procedures for Sale of the Debtors’ Assets, (B) Authorizing the Potential
Selection of Stalking Horse Bidder(s), (C) Approving Bid Protections, (D) Scheduling Auction(s)
for, and Hearing(s) to Approve, the Sale of the Debtors’ Assets, (E) Approving the Form and
Manner of Notices of Sale, Auction(s), and Sale Hearing(s), and (F) Approving the Assumption
and Assignment Procedures [ECF No. 1213] (the “Bidding Procedures Order”)2 by which the
Court, among other things, approved procedures (the “Bidding Procedures”) to be used in
connection with one or more transfers of the Debtors’ assets free and clear of all liens, claims,
encumbrances, and other interests.

         PLEASE TAKE FURTHER NOTICE that, in accordance with the Bidding Procedures
and the Bidding Procedures Order, the Debtors conducted a virtual Auction with respect to the
Deidentified Data on Friday, August 14, 2026.

         PLEASE TAKE FURTHER NOTICE that, at the Auction, the Debtors (after consulting
with the Consultation Parties) selected the following bid from Google LLC (the “Successful
Bidder”) as the highest and best bid (the “Successful Bid”) and the following bid from Mercor.io
Corporation (the “Alternate Bidder”) as the Alternate Bid, subject to entry of an order by the
Court (the “Order”) approving the transaction with the Successful Bidder (or, if the Successful
Bidder fails to consummate the transactions in accordance with the Bidding Procedures, the
Alternate Bidder).

              Section 1. Asset Transfer.

              (a) The Seller hereby irrevocably sells, assigns, transfers and conveys to Buyer,
     and Buyer hereby purchases, acquires and takes assignment from the Seller of, all of the
     Seller’s right, title and interest in, to and under all of the assets set forth on Exhibit A
     hereto (the “Assets”) for an aggregate purchase price equal to Ten Million U.S. Dollars
     ($10,000,000.00) (the “Purchase Price”), with delivery of the Assets to be made in
     deidentified form in accordance with Section 1(a)(ii) following, and in no event prior to,
     the satisfaction (or waiver) of the conditions set forth in Section 3; provided that:

                         (i) it is the express intention of the Parties that the Assets shall not
              include information that relates to, describes, or is reasonably capable of being
              associated with a consumer or is otherwise considered “personal data”, “personal
              information”, “nonpublic personal information” or other similar term under
              applicable data protection laws (such information, “Personal Data,” provided that,
              for the avoidance of doubt, the definition of Personal Data does not include
              Deidentified Data (as defined below));

                        (ii) prior to the transfer of the Assets to Buyer, the Seller shall first
              deliver, at Buyer’s direction, all or a portion of the data set forth in Exhibit A to
              one or more third parties acceptable to or designated by Buyer (such a third party,
              a “Deidentification Agent”) in order to: (A) take reasonable measures as set forth
              in Section 3(c) below to remove or transform certain data elements such that the
              data cannot be associated with, reasonably used to infer information about, or
              otherwise linked to, a particular consumer (such transformed data, “Deidentified
              Data,” and the process of such transformation, “Deidentification” or
              “Deidentifying”); and (B) migrate such data in a format that is reasonably
              acceptable to Buyer for further delivery to Buyer;

                       (iii) Buyer shall be solely responsible for any and all costs and expenses
              incurred in connection with Deidentifying the Assets in accordance with Section
              1(a)(ii); provided that, for the avoidance of doubt, any such costs and expenses shall
              not reduce the amount of the Purchase Price; and

                       (iv) delivery of the Assets by the Seller to Buyer, or by a Deidentification
              Agent to Buyer, as applicable, in deidentified form consistent with Section
              3(c)Section 3, shall be deemed delivery by the Seller to Buyer for all purposes of
              this Agreement.

              that, following the transfer of the Assets, the Seller determines that the transferred
              Assets contain any Privileged Materials inadvertently transferred or not removed
              by the Seller prior to the transfer of the Assets to Buyer: (A) this Agreement or
