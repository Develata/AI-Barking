# Composer predictions in Codex

Get suggestions for your next message.

Updated: 12 hours ago

Composer predictions **is in beta** for users aged 18 and older on personal ChatGPT Pro plans in all supported regions. It’s available in local and SSH threads in the Codex desktop app with GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol.

# Overview

Composer predictions is a beta feature that suggests what you might want to say next, based on the conversation in your current Codex thread. After Codex finishes a response, a suggestion may appear in the message box. The composer may choose to skip a prediction.

You can accept a suggestion, edit it, or write your own message. Predictions may not always match your intent, so review them before sending.

## Availability

Composer predictions is **currently in beta** and is available in all supported regions for adults aged 18 and older using a personal ChatGPT Pro account in the Codex desktop app. It is available in local and SSH threads with supported models.

Predictions are not available in Chat on web or Desktop. In Work, predictions may appear if you use local Work threads and conversations.

## Supported models

Predictions are supported  when using GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6.1 Sol in local and SSH threads. Select one of these models in your local or SSH thread to use the feature.

# Use Composer predictions

1. Update the Codex desktop app and sign in to your personal ChatGPT Pro account.
2. Open a local or SSH thread with a supported model and send a message.
3. When Codex finishes responding, look for a suggestion in the message box.
4. Press Tab to accept a suggestion. Review or edit the text, then send it when you’re ready.

To write something else, start typing your own message. You can keep typing without waiting for a prediction. Accepting a prediction adds text to the message box - it does not send the message for you.

# Turn predictions off

Composer predictions are on by default for eligible users. To turn them off:

1. Open **Settings** in the Codex desktop app.
2. Go to **General**, then **Composer**.
3. Turn off **Show predictions**.

You can turn **Show predictions** back on in the same place.

# How predictions use context

Predictions use the conversation in your current thread. If context from past work or connected apps is already in that thread, it can inform a prediction. Predictions don’t look up memories or retrieve information from connected apps on their own. You can use predictions with memory off.

# Troubleshooting

A prediction won’t appear after every response. This alone does not mean something is wrong. You can continue by typing your own message.

# FAQ

## Do composer predictions cost extra?

During the beta, composer predictions are included at no additional cost for eligible Pro users.

## Do predictions count toward my usage limits?

During the beta, generating predictions doesn’t count toward your Codex usage limits or consume credits. When you send a message, including an accepted prediction, normal Codex usage limits and billing apply.

## Can I use predictions on Work or other clients?

During the beta, predictions are available only on personal ChatGPT Pro plans in the Codex desktop app. They aren’t available on other plans on the web, or in Chat.

## Can composer predictions continue a task automatically?

No. Composer predictions don’t automatically accept or send follow-up messages. You should review each prediction before choosing to send it.
