Brice Ménard works with Anthropic’s Claude Science to produce first complete UV light map of the sky

 October 9, 2026October 9, 2026 

Professor Brice Ménard [https://physics-astronomy.jhu.edu/directory/brice-menard/] explains first-hand in a new Anthropic article about how his research utilizing agents working in Claude Science [https://www.anthropic.com/research/the-missing-map-of-the-sky] led to the production of the first complete map of the sky in UV light. Ménard used measurements from GALEX [https://archive.stsci.edu/missions-and-data/galex] and Swift [https://swift.gsfc.nasa.gov/archive/] (NASA), FIMS/SPEAR [https://archive.stsci.edu/missions-and-data/fims-spear] (Korea), TD-1 [https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/td1-stellar-ultraviolet-fluxes-catalog] (Europe), and Planck [https://pla.esac.esa.int/#home] and Gaia [https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/] (ESA) to create the map with the Claude Science agents.
