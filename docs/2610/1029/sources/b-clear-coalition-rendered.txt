CORE PHILOSOPHY
"Language communities are the primary stakeholders and decision-makers regarding the creation, curation, and application of language data and models pertaining to their linguistic heritage."

Linguistic sovereignty is CLEAR's foundational principle. It counteracts historical patterns of marginalization and ensures AI development is unbiased, equitable, and sustainable — grounded in the rights of the communities it is meant to serve.
