THE IMPERATIVE
The AI divide is a language divide.
~20

Languages well-served by today's most powerful AI systems, out of more than 7,000+ spoken worldwide. Beyond this small set, performance degrades sharply, leaving billions without meaningful AI access.

Billions

People whose languages of choice are inadequately represented in powerful emerging technologies. This is not a marginal problem — it is the default experience for the majority of humanity.

Language is a human right, a vessel for cultural heritage, identity, and knowledge, and a means of economic enfranchisement. When AI fails a language, it limits that community's ability to participate in the next generation of the internet.

The technical barriers are compounding: scarce training data, absent evaluation standards, and chronic underinvestment keep low-resource languages locked out of the AI era.

01
Scarce training data
Languages without easily accessible digital resources lack the relevant, high-quality corpora needed to train capable AI systems.
02
No evaluation standards
Without authoritative benchmarks, there is no way to measure, or hold anyone accountable for, AI quality across languages.
03
Cultural erasure
When model builders ignore languages, it can accelerate the erosion of cultural heritage, identity, and community knowledge.
04
Economic exclusion
Billions in low-resource language communities are locked out of the productivity gains AI is creating everywhere else.
