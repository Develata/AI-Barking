 Mistral Large 4 by @MistralAI just landed in the Code Arena: WebDev at #45. With 1534 pts, this is a +304 pt improvement from Mistral Large 3 at #130!
 This release also marks a 271-point improvement over its next-best-performing variant, Mistral Medium 3.5, at #122.
 Mistral Large 4 delivers performance within just 2 points of Claude Opus 4.8 (High) at a nearly 6× lower blended price: $3.47/M vs. $20/M tokens. Nearby but higher-performing models are available for less, keeping it just off the Code Arena: WebDev Pareto frontier.
 @MistralAI announced its open weights will be released at the end of October. Stay tuned for its score on Arena within open models, and congrats to the team on this release!
