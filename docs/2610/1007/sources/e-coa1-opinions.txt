To obtain a signed copy of the original document, contact the Court of Appeals, Division One Clerk’s Office. Please note that if a certified copy of the document is required, state law requires a certification fee to be collected.

Opinions issued by Division One of the Arizona Court of Appeals are usually posted to this web site on the date they are filed with the Clerk of Division One and distributed to the parties. Later proceedings in other courts may affect the legal status of any Opinion. For example, an Opinion could be vacated or reversed in whole or in part by the Arizona Supreme Court or the United States Supreme Court. In addition, a Opinion might later be changed, in whole or in part, or might be vacated by Division One pursuant to a motion for reconsideration filed in the case. An Opinion might also be ordered not to be published as a precedential opinion by the Arizona Supreme Court. Division One does not undertake to note any such later actions or to amend or adjust its listing of posted Opinions in light of such actions. The reader is cautioned to confirm independently the current legal status of any Opinion posted on this web site.
