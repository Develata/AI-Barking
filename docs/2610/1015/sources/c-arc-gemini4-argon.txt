Gemini 4 Argon

Google·Oct 13, 2026·6 harness configurations·$2 input / $10 output per 1M tokens

Blog post ↗ [https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-argon/]Tweet ↗ [https://x.com/OfficialLoganK/status/2110038940841545827]

Gemini 4 Argon's best Semi-Private scores are 98.5% on v1 and 91.2% on v2, both at high reasoning. On v3, it reaches 49.8% with the Standard harnessStandard harness enables a model to carry forward notes it chooses to keep with it throughout the environment. at low reasoning and 97.5% with the Provider Adapter harnessThe Provider Adapter harness preserves opaque reasoning state between requests and uses compaction for longer conversations, allowing the model to reuse prior work. at low reasoning. API issues prevented complete v3 evaluation with the standard harness. Runs completed on 42 of 55 tasks at low reasoning, 46 of 55 at medium reasoning, and 19 of 55 at high reasoning. Reported scores include all 55 tasks, retaining partial credit earned on incomplete runs. We excluded the high-reasoning standard-harness point from the chart because of this incomplete coverage. Reported costs use Google's introductory pricing of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens.
