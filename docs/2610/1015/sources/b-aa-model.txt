Comparison Summary

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) is amongst the leading models in intelligence, but particularly expensive when comparing to other open weight models of similar size. It's also notably fast and fairly concise. The model supports text, image, speech, and video input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) scores 39 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 19). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 110M tokens, which is fairly concise in comparison to the median of 140M.

Pricing for Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) is $1.25 per 1M input tokens (expensive, median: $0.43) and $4.25 per 1M output tokens (expensive, median: $1.59). On average, it costs $0.84 per task to evaluate Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) on the Intelligence Index.

At 102 tokens per second, Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) is notably fast (68).

Total Parameters

The total number of trainable weights and biases in the model, expressed in billions. These parameters are learned during training and determine the model's ability to process and generate responses.

Active Parameters at Inference Time

The number of parameters actually executed during each inference forward pass, expressed in billions. For Mixture of Experts (MoE) models, a routing mechanism selects a subset of experts per token, resulting in fewer active than total parameters. Dense models use all parameters, so active equals total.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Muse Spark Open (Xhigh)

When was Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) released?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) was released on October 15, 2026.

Who created Muse Spark Open (Xhigh)?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) was created by Meta.

How intelligent is Muse Spark Open (Xhigh)?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) scores 39 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among other open weight models of similar size (median: 19).

How fast is Muse Spark Open (Xhigh)?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) generates output at 102.0 tokens per second (based on the median across providers serving the model), which is well above average compared to other open weight models of similar size (median: 67.7 t/s).

What is the latency of Muse Spark Open (Xhigh)?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) has a time to first token (TTFT) of 0.67s (based on the median across providers serving the model), which is very competitive compared to other open weight models of similar size (median: 1.76s).

How much does Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) cost?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) costs $1.25 per 1M input tokens (at the higher end, median: $0.43) and $4.25 per 1M output tokens (at the higher end, median: $1.59), based on the median across providers serving the model.

What is Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) API pricing?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) costs $1.25 per 1M input tokens and $4.25 per 1M output tokens (based on the median across providers serving the model). For a blended rate (7:2:1 cache hit/input/output ratio), this is $0.78 per 1M tokens. Pricing may vary by provider. Compare provider pricing [/models/muse-spark-open/providers]

How verbose is Muse Spark Open (Xhigh)?

When evaluated on the Intelligence Index, Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) generated 110M output tokens, which is better than average compared to other open weight models of similar size (median: 140M).

Is Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) a reasoning model?

Yes, Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) is a reasoning model. It uses extended thinking or chain-of-thought reasoning to work through complex problems before providing an answer.

What input modalities does Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) support?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) supports text, image, speech, and video input.

What output modalities does Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) support?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) supports text output.

Can Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) process images?

Yes, Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) supports image input and can analyze, describe, and answer questions about images.

Is Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) multimodal?

Yes, Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) is multimodal. It can process text, image, speech, and video input and generate text output.

What is the context window of Muse Spark Open (Xhigh)?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) has a context window of 1.0M tokens. This determines how much text and conversation history the model can process in a single request.

Is Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) open source?

Yes, Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) is open weights. The model weights are publicly available and can be downloaded for self-hosting.

How many parameters does Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) have?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) has 816 billion parameters (42 billion active).

What are the active parameters of Muse Spark Open (Xhigh)?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) is a Mixture of Experts (MoE) model with 816 billion total parameters, but only 42 billion active parameters are used during inference.

What is the license for Muse Spark Open (Xhigh)?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) is released under the Muse Model License license. Commercial use is allowed with restrictions. View license [https://huggingface.co/meta-models/Muse-Spark-Open-816B-A42B/blob/main/LICENSE.md]

How does Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) perform on benchmarks?

Muse Spark Open (Xhigh) achieves a score of 39 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. This composite benchmark evaluates models across reasoning, knowledge, mathematics, and coding.
