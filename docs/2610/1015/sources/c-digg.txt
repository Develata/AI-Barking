Gemini 4 Argon scored 97.5% on ARC-AGI-3 using a provider-adapter harness, while its standard-harness score was 49.8%, according to verified results posted by ARC Prize [https://x.com/arcprize/status/2110768288523251762] on Oct. 15, 2026. The two figures describe different evaluation setups, not interchangeable scores for the same configuration.

Featured Source

AP [https://x.com/arcprize]ARC Prize@arcprize [https://x.com/arcprize]9:22 AM · Oct 15, 2026 [https://x.com/arcprize/status/2110768288523251762]

Gemini 4 Argon from @Google [https://x.com/Google] on ARC-AGI (Verified):

- ARC-AGI-3: 97.5%, $4.8K (provider adapter harness), 49.8%, $5.8K (standard harness)
- ARC-AGI-2: 91.3%, $0.32/task
- ARC-AGI-1: 98.5%, $0.16/task

Google DeepMind is the second frontier AI lab to score above 95% on ARC-AGI-3.

ARC Prize described Google DeepMind as the second frontier AI lab to score above 95% on ARC-AGI-3. The result above that threshold is the provider-adapter score; it is not a perfect 100% result or the standard-harness result.

The same post lists Argon at 91.3% on ARC-AGI-2, with a cost of $0.32 per task, and 98.5% on ARC-AGI-1, at $0.16 per task.

In a quote-post of the results [https://x.com/mikeknoop/status/2110769895332040761], Mike Knoop characterized Argon as the second model family to effectively max out ARC-AGI-3 using a provider adapter. He praised Google DeepMind's results and highlighted what he called a “backwards bend” in action efficiency.
