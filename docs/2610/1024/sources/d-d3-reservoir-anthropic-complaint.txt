 9                  A. Reservoir and Its Copyrights

10            21.      Reservoir is a leading independent music company that owns or exclusively controls

11 copyrights in tens of thousands of musical compositions and master recordings spanning virtually

12 every genre and era of popular music. Reservoir’s catalogs include Works by some of the most

13 celebrated songwriters and recording artists in history, from rock, pop, country, blues, R&B, Hip

14 Hop, and alternative.

15            22.      Reservoir knows that great music starts with great songwriting and masterful lyricists

16 and is committed to supporting and protecting the creative works its songwriters have entrusted to it.

17 As a publisher, Reservoir regularly enters into licenses and other agreements for the Works, through

18 which Reservoir compensates its songwriters for their share of earnings. Reservoir’s songwriters rely

19 on the income received from these transactions to support their livelihood and their art. This system
20 of licensing, permissions, and authorized use is the backbone of the music publishing industry and

21 ensures that the songwriters and publishers who have invested their blood, sweat, tears, heartache,

22 elation, and experience into their creative works receive compensation for the use of those works.
23            23.      U.S. copyright law simply does not let technology platforms like Anthropic ride

24 roughshod over these Constitutionally mandated protections of creative expression. Indeed, copyright

25 owners necessarily have the right to police the use of their copyrighted works. “The owner of the
26 copyright, if he pleases, may refrain from vending or licensing and content himself with simply

27 exercising the right to exclude others from using his property.” Fox Film Corp. v. Doyal,

28 286 U.S. 123, 127 (1932); see also Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer Studios, Inc. v. Grokster, Ltd.,

 3          27.      Anthropic is a corporation that develops, operates, sells, and licenses AI programs.

 8          29.      Anthropic’s primary product is Claude, a family of LLMs offered through a consumer-

 9 facing chatbot (claude.ai), a subscription Application Programming Interface (“API”), and enterprise

24 case involving Anthropic, that there is no AI carve-out from the Copyright Act. Id. at 1022.
