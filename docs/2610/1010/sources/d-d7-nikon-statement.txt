Nikon Small World in Motion Video Statement

Posted on October 09, 2026

An update from Nikon Small World regarding the 2026 Small World in Motion first-place winning video: After thoroughly re-evaluating the video and supporting materials and consulting with members of our judging panel, Nikon has determined that the winning video did not comply with the competition rules regarding generative AI. Nikon has adjusted the competition rankings to reflect this decision.

This decision is based solely on the submitted video's eligibility under the rules outlined for this competition. It should not be interpreted as a judgment of the entrant's professional reputation, scientific contributions, or intent.

Advances in imaging and artificial intelligence continue to evolve rapidly, creating new challenges for organizations across many fields. This event has shown the need for Nikon Small World and Nikon Small World in Motion to revisit rules and evaluation procedures for future competition entries.

The Nikon Small World and Nikon Small World in Motion competitions celebrate excellence in imaging, both scientific and artistic in nature. We value the integrity of each image and video selected to appear in our winning galleries, and remain committed to upholding the standards of excellence that have define this competition.
