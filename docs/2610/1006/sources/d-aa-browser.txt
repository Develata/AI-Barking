Comparison Summary

Mistral Large 4 Preview is above average in intelligence and well priced when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also faster than average, however very verbose. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 524k tokens context window.

Mistral Large 4 Preview scores 38 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it above average among comparable models (median: 26). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 200M tokens, which is very verbose in comparison to the median of 81M.

Pricing for Mistral Large 4 Preview is $1.36 per 1M input tokens (moderately priced, median: $2.00) and $4.18 per 1M output tokens (competitively priced, median: $10.00). On average, it costs $1.13 per task to evaluate Mistral Large 4 Preview on the Intelligence Index.

At 116 tokens per second, Mistral Large 4 Preview is faster than average (87).
