 What counts as a Claude reset? 
 A reset means Anthropic reset the counters — 5-hour
 and/or weekly usage came back for the announced plans. A
 banked reset is one you apply yourself, when you choose;
 it counts as a reset too. A limit change 
 only moves the ceiling; counters are not reset, so those posts
 appear in the archive but never add to the day counter or
 scoreboard.

 Predictions, plan labels, limit changes and alerts.
 When is the next Anthropic reset?
 This tracker records announced resets; it does not predict the next one. The average gap and longest drought describe past announcements. They are not a schedule for future resets or for your account.
 Does a Claude reset apply to every plan?
 It depends on the announcement. Some resets cover everyone; others cover particular plans, or only the users hit by a specific issue. Each entry in the Claude reset archive shows the plans it covered.
 Is a limit increase the same as a reset?
 No. A reset restores usage by clearing the usage counters for the plans it covers. A limit increase raises the allowance without necessarily clearing them. The archive lists both, but only resets count toward the statistics here. Banked resets, which you apply yourself later, count as resets too.
 How can I get Claude Code reset alerts?
 Subscribe to the reset-only RSS feed , or turn on browser alerts with Enable reset alerts at the top of the page, where your browser supports them. Alerts go out as soon as a reset is detected. A detected reset is marked “Awaiting review” and joins the archive unless it is withdrawn.
 To follow resets in a calendar, subscribe to the reset calendar in Apple Calendar or Outlook, or add it to Google Calendar . Each reset becomes an event, and so does the last day to apply a banked reset.
 About the data Sources, coverage and machine-readable feeds 
 Where the data comes from. Each entry links to its original
 post, and its date is decoded from that post's X status ID rather than typed
 in, so it cannot drift from the source. The exception is a reset recorded
 from an announcement page: it links that page and keeps the time it was
 given. Times are UTC.
