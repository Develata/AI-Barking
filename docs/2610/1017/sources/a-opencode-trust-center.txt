Our Mission

Anomaly Innovations, Inc positions its mission around building open-source developer tools and an on-device AI coding agent, with an emphasis on open-source product development and empowering end users through local-first execution. It envisions expanding its suite of open-source projects, including OpenCode, SST, and related tools, to advance developer productivity and deliver AI coding capabilities that run entirely on user devices.
