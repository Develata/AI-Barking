NVIDIA RTX Spark [http://nvidia.com/en-us/products/rtx-spark] laptops are now available, bringing NVIDIA Blackwell RTX graphics, up to 128GB of unified memory and the full NVIDIA AI platform to slim, premium Windows PCs. 

Starting at $2,399, laptops from ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Microsoft and MSI bring powerful local AI, content creation and gaming capabilities to a portable device, with additional systems on the way.

NVIDIA celebrated the launch with events around the world, including one in Berlin, where NVIDIA founder and CEO Jensen Huang made a surprise appearance.

A New Generation of Windows PCs Has Arrived

RTX Spark brings decades of NVIDIA accelerated computing innovation into a single superchip, combining an NVIDIA Blackwell RTX GPU and NVIDIA Grace CPU with up to 1 petaflop of AI compute and up to 128GB of unified memory. Read more in the press release [https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-rtx-spark-pcs-available-today-a-new-beginning-for-windows-pcs].

RTX Spark Launch Celebrations Light Up Berlin

The arrival of RTX Spark brought fans, creators and PC enthusiasts together in Berlin during the Festival of Lights, an annual autumn art festival that transforms the German capital’s most famous landmarks, streets and squares into glowing canvases. This year, the festival featured RTX Spark-themed lighting on the Berlin TV Tower.

 [https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/berlin-tv-tower-rtx-spark-event.png]
Berlin sparked something special, indeed.

Fans gathered hours ahead in anticipation for the launch celebration at XPERION, the gaming venue inside Saturn at Alexanderplatz, where attendees got hands-on with RTX Spark laptops from leading PC makers, exploring the latest gaming experiences, content-creation workflows and local AI capabilities.

 [https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Geralt-rtx-spark-event.png]
Even this Geralt cosplayer waited to get his hands on RTX Spark.

The celebration included a special appearance by Huang, who greeted an enthusiastic crowd, passed out popcorn and posed for selfies. He also gave out five personally signed RTX Spark laptops. 

 [https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jensen-rtx-spark-event.png]
You get a signed RTX Spark. You get a signed RTX Spark.

Berlin held one of six RTX Spark retail launch celebrations worldwide.

From San Francisco to Seoul: How the World Welcomed RTX Spark

A massive crowd gathered at the JD MALL Shuangjing Store in Beijing to celebrate the highly anticipated launch, enjoying a night of innovation and community connection.

 [https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/beijing-rtx-spark-event.png]
Crowds gathered to demo RTX Spark at the Beijing RTX Spark launch party.

Attendees at Vatan Bilgisayar Topkapı in Istanbul experienced a lively event filled with interactive demonstrations and specialized programming led by local creators.

 [https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/istanbul-rtx-spark-event.png]
Attendees witnessed local celebrities speak at the RTX Spark launch party in Istanbul.

Seoul’s COEX East Lobby transformed into a bustling hub of creativity, hosting an eager crowd for engaging hands-on demos and captivating evening sessions dedicated to gaming and AI.

 [https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/seoul-rtx-spark-event.png]
Hands-on demos at the RTX Spark launch party in Seoul.

Tech enthusiasts in San Francisco visited the local Best Buy store for an unforgettable morning of hands-on RTX Spark demos, fireside chats with local founders and a special appearance by tech influencer iJustine.

 [https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/sf-rtx-spark-event.png]
Lines were long but lively at the San Francisco RTX Spark launch party.

The energy at Muse Garden in Taipei was electric as the local AI community came together for a vibrant evening of music, engaging discussions and incredible product showcases.

 [https://blogs.nvidia.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/taipei-rtx-spark-event.png]
Incredible atmosphere at the Taipei RTX Spark launch party.
