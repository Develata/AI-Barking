Intro

The OpenCode Console [https://opencode.ai/console] is an optional service that provides additional benefits to
using OpenCode, on your own or as a team.

Workspaces [/v2/docs/console/workspaces] for sharing access with your team [/v2/docs/console/members]

Inference [/v2/docs/console/models] for both proprietary and open source models

Providers [/v2/docs/console/providers] for connecting your own provider accounts through the Console gateway

Usage tracking and budget [/v2/docs/console/budgets] controls

Deploy team wide policies to control OpenCode behavior

Web search [/v2/docs/console/websearch]

OpenCode Go and Go Plus [/v2/docs/console/go], $10 and $40 monthly subscriptions for open source model access

Get started

You don’t need a team to use the Console. Every account starts with a Personal workspace where you are the only
member.

Sign in to the Console [https://opencode.ai/console].

Subscribe to Go [/v2/docs/console/go], add credits [/v2/docs/console/billing#add-credits], or connect a provider [/v2/docs/console/providers].

Run /connect in OpenCode and choose the workspace.

Optionally, invite [/v2/docs/console/members#invite-members] your team.

/connect

Policies

Providers and Tools rules from the Policies page apply to every OpenCode V2 client
connected to the workspace. They are delivered as policy [/v2/docs/policies#console]
statements with final authority over the member’s own configuration and take effect
within about a minute.
