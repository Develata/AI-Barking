Reflection’s first model, Beam, scores 30 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it #2 among US open weights models
See model page [/models/reflection-beam]

See model page [/models/reflection-beam]

Beam is an open weights model with 501B total and 23B active parameters

Key takeaways:

➤ Second among US open weights models: Beam scores 30, behind only Meta's Muse Spark Open (39) and ahead of yesterday’s NVIDIA Nemotron 3.5 Super VL (26) release. Outside China, only Muse Spark Open and the UAE's K2 Horizon (31) score higher

➤ Token efficient for its intelligence: Beam uses ~26k output tokens per Intelligence Index task compared to K2 Horizon, one point ahead, which uses twice as many (~52k). Proprietary models still set the frontier, with GPT-6 Astra (max) scoring 53 on the same budget (~27k)

➤ Strong on agentic workflows, weaker in the terminal: Beam scores 48.5% on AutomationBench-AA, 11 percentage points ahead of K2 Horizon (37.2%) and level with Qwen3.8 27B (48.2%). Terminal-Bench 4.0 is a weak point, where Beam scores 3.0%, behind Qwen3.8 27B (5.6%) and Muse Spark Open (8.1%)

➤ Knows more, guesses more: On AA-Omniscience it answers 33% of questions correctly, well ahead of K2 Horizon (18%) and Qwen3.8 27B (16%). But its hallucination rate is 47%, against 26% for K2 Horizon and 30% for Qwen3.8 27B, giving Beam a net index score of 1.5, ahead of both peers (−3.0 and −10.0)

Other model details

Context window: 1M tokens

Modalities: Text

Parameters: 501B / 23B

Compared to open weights peers

Beam scores highly against US open weights models, trailing Meta's Muse Spark Open (39) and ahead of NVIDIA's Nemotron 3.5 Super VL (26), all released this week. Among all open weights models it ranks tenth, one point behind K2 Horizon (31)

Cyber capabilities

Beam completes 34% of tasks on the Artificial Analysis Cyber Index, making it the leading US open weights model that we’ve tested

Driven by its token efficiency, it also sits on the Cyber Index vs. Output Tokens per task frontier, alongside GPT-6 Sol (Daybreak Blue)

Highly token efficient

Beam uses ~26k output tokens per Intelligence Index task: ~19k for reasoning and ~7k for answers. Only 26% of its output goes to final answers, the 28th-lowest share of 219 models tested on Intelligence Index v4.3

Coding skills

Running in Mirror, Reflection's open-source CLI harness, Beam scores 28 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index. It sits 15 points behind the next setup, Claude Code with Qwen3.8 Max (43), and 40 behind the leaders (68). It is, however, frugal: only Codex with GPT-6 Astra and Codex with GPT-6.1 Sol use fewer tokens

Intelligence Index Components

Across the ten evaluations in the Intelligence Index, Beam stands out on AutomationBench-AA (48.5%) and CritPt (13.1%), where it more than doubles K2 Horizon (4.6%) and Qwen3.8 27B (5.4%). Its weakest results are on Terminal-Bench 4.0 (3.0%) and AA-Briefcase (976 Elo)
