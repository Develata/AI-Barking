2026

Public policy has long favored transparent and accountable forms of money, yet the rapid growth of stablecoins—particularly those held and transacted through self-custody wallets—raises new challenges for regulators seeking to curb illicit activity, safeguard monetary sovereignty, and maintain financial integrity. As digital forms of money become increasingly global, strengthening international cooperation and regulatory frameworks will be essential to ensuring transparency and trust in the financial system. Hosted by the Bank for International Settlements (BIS), this year's Per Jacobsson Foundation Lecture was given by Gita Gopinath , Gregory and Ania Coffey Professor of Economics at Harvard University and Professor of Public Policy at Harvard Kennedy School, on "Stablecoins and Anonymous Money [https://www.bis.org/events/agm2026/sp260628_lecture.htm]," on June 28, 2026, in Basel, Switzerland.
