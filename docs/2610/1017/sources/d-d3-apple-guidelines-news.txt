Updated App Review Guidelines are now available

October 16, 2026

The App Review Guidelines [https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/] have been revised to support new features, updated policies, and to provide clarification.

App Review Guidelines

Before You Submit: Added links to additional documentation. 

1.2: Clarified app content and app types that are subject to this guideline, and potential Developer Code of Conduct violation for failing to implement appropriate safeguards or remediating violations after notice.

2.3.14: New guideline covering creative assets.

2.5.19: New guideline clarifies long-standing policy on hidden, dormant, or undocumented features, formerly covered in Guideline 2.3.1.

3.1.1(a): Removed bullet point on Music Streaming Services Entitlements, which is now incorporated in the bullet point on StoreKit External Purchase Link Entitlements; clarified that links may be provided to a permitted destination.

5: Clarified that apps found to facilitate human trafficking and/or the exploitation of children will be removed from the App Store and blocked from alternative distribution.

After You Submit: Added links to additional documentation. 

Translations of the updated guidelines will be available on the Apple Developer website [https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/] within one month.
