 2026

Sunday
June 28, 2026

Public policy has long favored transparent and accountable forms of money, yet the rapid growth of stablecoins—particularly those held and transacted through self-custody wallets—raises new challenges for regulators seeking to curb illicit activity, safeguard monetary sovereignty, and maintain financial integrity. As digital forms of money become increasingly global, strengthening international cooperation and regulatory frameworks will be essential to ensuring transparency and trust in the financial system. Hosted by the Bank for International Settlements (BIS), this year's Per Jacobsson Foundation Lecture was given by Gita Gopinath , Gregory and Ania Coffey Professor of Economics at Harvard University and Professor of Public Policy at Harvard Kennedy School, on "Stablecoins and Anonymous Money [https://www.bis.org/events/agm2026/sp260628_lecture.htm]," on June 28, 2026, in Basel, Switzerland.

Read the Lecture [https://www.bis.org/events/agm2026/sp260628_lecture.pdf]

 2025

Wednesday
October 15, 2025

As economic nationalism rises, the path to shared prosperity lies not in retreat but in renewal—modernizing global institutions, forging open coalitions for trade, technology, and digital governance, and unlocking greater development finance for emerging regions. Sustaining an open world economy will depend on cooperation among major powers and coherent domestic strategies that ensure all citizens share in global progress. This year's Per Jacobsson Lecture will be given by Tharman Shanmugaratnam, President of the Republic of Singapore on "An Era of Possibility: Renewing Economic Order and Shared Purpose [https://meetings.imf.org/en/2025/annual/schedule/2025/10/15/imf-per-jacobsson-foundation-lecture]", followed by a panel discussion moderated by Raghuram Rajan, with distinguished panelists Moritz Schularick and Lael Brainard, October 15, 2025.

Read the Lecture [https://meetings.imf.org/-/media/AMSM/Files/AM2025/2025-per-jacobsson-foundation-lecture-by-president-tharman-shanmugaratnam.ashx]

May 31, 2025

Ms. Aban Haq, Head of the Project Management Office and Member Engagement at the Alliance for Financial Inclusion (AFI), visited CDE/Williams College for a special two-day event as part of the CDE/Per Jacobsson Foundation Speaker Series.

On Saturday, May 31, 2025 Ms. Haq delivered a public talk titled “A Public Purpose Across Institutions,” in which she reflected on the pivotal choices, risks, and lessons that have shaped her career in international development.

On Sunday, June 1, 2025 she joined Professor Jerry Caprio for a conversation on the future of financial inclusion policy, with a focus on emerging trends in digital finance, regulatory innovation, and the role of inclusive financial systems in building climate resilience.
