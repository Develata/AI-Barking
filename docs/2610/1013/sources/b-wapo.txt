OpenAI employee arrested by FBI after listening device found at its office

The government framed the arrest of the Saudi national employee as important for national security.

October 13, 2026 at 9:20 p.m. EDT
4 minutes ago
4 min
loading
Attendees at the OpenAI DevDay 2026 conference last month. (Jeff Chiu/AP)
By Gerrit De Vynck

SAN FRANCISCO — The FBI arrested an employee of ChatGPT-maker OpenAI last week after the company discovered a “sophisticated” recording device in an executive conference room at its offices.

[当事人], a 35-year-old citizen of Saudi Arabia who has been working at OpenAI since July 2025, was arrested Oct. 9 and appeared in federal court on Tuesday. He was charged with using a listening device to “intercept oral communications,” the Justice Department said in a statement.
