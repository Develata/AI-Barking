Block unveils Dessa, a speech-only AI model built for real-time laptop use

Led by former Dessa co-founder Ragavan Thurairatnam, the Block research project is opening early access to a small group of AI safety researchers.

 By Ryan Merket [/author/ryan-merket]
 · Published Oct 23rd, 2026, 6:46pm CT
 · Updated Oct 23rd, 2026, 6:47pm CT

Primary source: X [https://x.com/jack/status/2113697902916751661]

Why it matters

Dessa tests whether a model trained only on conversation audio can deliver natural, real-time voice interaction on a laptop. Block's demos show the intended behavior.

Jack Dorsey [https://x.com/jack/status/2113697902916751661] introduced Dessa on October 23rd as a full-duplex speech model from Block, pitching voice as a more natural way to interact with AI. The project is led by Ragavan Thurairatnam [https://www.ivey.uwo.ca/scotiabank-digital-banking-lab/canada-fintech/infrastructure-services/dessa/], an AI researcher who co-founded the earlier Toronto startup named Dessa. Block says the new model was trained from scratch on human conversation audio alone, with no text training or pretrained model.…
