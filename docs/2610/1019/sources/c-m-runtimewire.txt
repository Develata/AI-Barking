Inception launches Mercury 3 at 1,068 tokens per second

Stefano Ermon's diffusion model claims a 1-million-token context window and benchmark gains over rival models, with launch pricing half off.

 By Ryan Merket [/author/ryan-merket]
 · Published Oct 19th, 2026, 12:06pm CT

Primary source: X [https://x.com/StefanoErmon/status/2112222911218291051]

Why it matters

Mercury 3 tests whether diffusion can scale into general agent and business workloads while keeping its speed advantage. Its published results and partner examples are company-reported, so adoption will depend on how the model performs in developers' own tasks.

Inception launches Mercury 3 at 1,068 tokens per second [https://runtimewire.com/api/storage/uploads/tweet-videos/inception-mercury-3-diffusion-llm-launch-a29e5c9f.mp4?generation=1792429487310475]

Inception [https://www.inceptionlabs.ai/blog/introducing-mercury-3] launched Mercury 3 on October 19th, pitching its diffusion-based language model as a faster alternative to conventional systems that generate text one token at a time. CEO and co-founder Stefano Ermon (@StefanoErmon) [https://x.com/StefanoErmon] says it delivers more than 1,000 tokens per second while improving on the company's earlier models.
