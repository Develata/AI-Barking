DeepSeek Open-Sources DeepInfer Inference Framework

DeepSeek says DeepInfer includes optimized kernels, distributed inference, and speculative decoding to help lower compute costs and energy use.

TLDR

DeepSeek says DeepInfer powers DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash at scale with high throughput and low latency, and plans to add inference code for future models. It says sharing its production stack is meant to lower compute costs and energy use for the community. The company also open-sourced deepseek-recipe, a token encoding and decoding component meant to bring official DeepSeek API behavior to community deployments.
