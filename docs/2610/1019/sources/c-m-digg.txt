Inception introduced Mercury 3 [https://www.inceptionlabs.ai/blog/introducing-mercury-3] on Oct. 19, claiming its diffusion-based language model delivers 1,068 tokens per second on NVIDIA GPUs. The company says it outperforms GPT-6 Luna and Claude Haiku 5.5 across a range of benchmarks.

Featured Source

The launch also expands context capacity to 1 million tokens, up from 260,000 for Mercury 2.5, according to Inception. The company says Mercury 3 has more than 40 times Mercury 1’s total parameters while remaining just as fast.

Inception announced availability through its API platform, OpenRouter, Baseten and Vercel. Its Oct. 19 launch offer advertised a 50% discount: $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.35 per million output tokens, against listed prices of $0.20 and $0.70, respectively.

The benchmark comparisons come with a qualification. Artificial Analysis cautioned [https://x.com/ArtificialAnlys/status/2112258558599512078] that the announcement’s AA-Omniscience and GDPval-AA v2 scores did not follow its methodology and were not comparable to scores on its platform. It said AA-Omniscience uses a private dataset, with 10% released publicly, while comparable GDPval-AA v2 results require its grading methodology and model comparison set.
