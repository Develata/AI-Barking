DeepSeek opens its inference engine, leaving service plumbing to developers

DeepInfer targets large-scale serving and supports DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash, but its README says a complete inference service requires components the project does not include.

 By RuntimeWire Staff [/author/runtimewire-staff]
 · Published Oct 20th, 2026, 4:52am CT

Primary source: DeepSeek [https://x.com/deepseek_ai/status/2112476498598957056]

Why it matters

DeepInfer extends DeepSeek's open-model strategy into serving infrastructure, but operators still need missing service components and independently reproducible benchmarks to judge its practical advantage.

DeepSeek described DeepInfer, its inference framework for running DeepSeek models, in an October 20th post on DeepSeek's official X account [https://x.com/deepseek_ai/status/2112476498598957056?ref=runtimewire]. The project puts model-serving infrastructure alongside the open models DeepSeek [https://deepseek.com/?ref=runtimewire] has released, offering a view into how DeepSeek says it runs inference at scale. The available materials do not identify founder Liang Wenfeng as a DeepInfer builder or maintainer.…
