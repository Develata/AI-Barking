Comparison Summary

Gemini 4 Argon (High) is amongst the leading models in intelligence, but somewhat expensive when comparing to other models of similar price. It's also slower than average and somewhat verbose. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

Gemini 4 Argon (High) scores 50 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 42). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 110M tokens, which is somewhat verbose in comparison to the median of 64M.

Pricing for Gemini 4 Argon (High) is $4.00 per 1M input tokens (somewhat expensive, median: $2.00) and $20.00 per 1M output tokens (expensive, median: $10.00). On average, it costs $7.68 per task to evaluate Gemini 4 Argon (High) on the Intelligence Index.

At 82 tokens per second, Gemini 4 Argon (High) is slower than average (82).

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Gemini 4 Argon (High)

When was Gemini 4 Argon (High) released?

Gemini 4 Argon (High) was released on September 30, 2026.

Who created Gemini 4 Argon (High)?

Gemini 4 Argon (High) was created by Google.

How intelligent is Gemini 4 Argon (High)?

Gemini 4 Argon (High) scores 50 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among other models in a similar price tier (median: 42).

How fast is Gemini 4 Argon (High)?

Gemini 4 Argon (High) generates output at 82.4 tokens per second (based on Google's API), which is above average compared to other models in a similar price tier (median: 82.4 t/s).

What is the latency of Gemini 4 Argon (High)?

Gemini 4 Argon (High) has a time to first token (TTFT) of 46.64s (based on Google's API), which is at the higher end compared to other models in a similar price tier (median: 3.56s).

How much does Gemini 4 Argon (High) cost?

Gemini 4 Argon (High) costs $4.00 per 1M input tokens (somewhat higher than average, median: $2.00) and $20.00 per 1M output tokens (at the higher end, median: $10.00), based on Google's API.

What is Gemini 4 Argon (High) API pricing?

Gemini 4 Argon (High) costs $4.00 per 1M input tokens and $20.00 per 1M output tokens (based on Google's API). For a blended rate (7:2:1 cache hit/input/output ratio), this is $2.94 per 1M tokens. Pricing may vary by provider. Compare provider pricing [/models/gemini-4-argon/providers]

How verbose is Gemini 4 Argon (High)?

When evaluated on the Intelligence Index, Gemini 4 Argon (High) generated 110M output tokens, which is somewhat higher than average compared to other models in a similar price tier (median: 64M).

Is Gemini 4 Argon (High) a reasoning model?

Yes, Gemini 4 Argon (High) is a reasoning model. It uses extended thinking or chain-of-thought reasoning to work through complex problems before providing an answer.

What input modalities does Gemini 4 Argon (High) support?

Gemini 4 Argon (High) supports text and image input.

What output modalities does Gemini 4 Argon (High) support?

Gemini 4 Argon (High) supports text output.

Can Gemini 4 Argon (High) process images?

Yes, Gemini 4 Argon (High) supports image input and can analyze, describe, and answer questions about images.

Is Gemini 4 Argon (High) multimodal?

Yes, Gemini 4 Argon (High) is multimodal. It can process text and image input and generate text output.

What is the context window of Gemini 4 Argon (High)?

Gemini 4 Argon (High) has a context window of 1.0M tokens. This determines how much text and conversation history the model can process in a single request.

Is Gemini 4 Argon (High) open source?

No, Gemini 4 Argon (High) is proprietary. The model weights are not publicly available.

How many parameters does Gemini 4 Argon (High) have?

Gemini 4 Argon (High) is a proprietary model and Google has not disclosed the model size or parameter count.

How does Gemini 4 Argon (High) perform on benchmarks?

Gemini 4 Argon (High) achieves a score of 50 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. This composite benchmark evaluates models across knowledge work, coding, science, and general knowledge.
