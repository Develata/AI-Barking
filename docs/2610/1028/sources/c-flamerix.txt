Why control

Your agents got fast.Your accountability did not move.

Whoever writes the code, the company still answers for delivery, quality, risk and cost. Agents change the executor. They do not change who signs off. Four questions your agents cannot answer about themselves:

01Is autonomous work staying inside the rules we actually set?

02Is more agent activity producing more delivery, or just more activity?

03Are cost and human intervention moving the right way?

04Can we compare what happens across teams, agents and vendors?

DRIM answers all four. On your data, not on impressions.
