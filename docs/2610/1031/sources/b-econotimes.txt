Exin Compares Three AI-Guided Gene-Therapy Procedures in Mice

Each model directed an injection in a separate mouse. Ocana-Santero said behavior and brain activity checks are planned over the next several weeks, while the post provided no measure of seizure effects.

By Elias Mercer [/contributors/61212]Oct 31, 2026, 10:31 AM UTC

Updated Oct 31, 2026, 10:31 AM UTC1 min read
 Share article

A mouse in a recovery cage beside stereotaxic equipment at a neuroscience lab in Taguig. (AI-generated image / EconoTimes)

Exin Therapeutics is comparing three AI-directed gene-therapy procedures in mice in an epilepsy study aimed at reducing seizures, co-founder and CEO Gabriel Ocana-Santero said in an Oct. 30 post.

Ocana-Santero said all three models received an identical set of 19 candidate vectors. The targets were the entorhinal cortex for Claude Sonnet 5, the hippocampus for GPT-6.1 Sol and the somatosensory cortex for Grok 4.7.

Ocana-Santero said the models proposed distinct treatments and explanations for their effects. Each selected an injection volume and controlled the rig’s movements and pauses, operating the stereotaxic equipment while looking at camera images.

He said the mice were recovering, with behavior and brain activity checks planned over the next several weeks. Asked whether one system performed better, he said it was “too early to say.” His post included no measurements showing whether the injections affected seizures.
