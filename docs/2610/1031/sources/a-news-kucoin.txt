Summary

OpenAI announced in a recent update that, starting in December, ChatGPT Plus, Pro, and Business users will share usage quotas between ChatGPT and Codex. Work’s agent features will also be integrated into ChatGPT’s main interface, while instant chat remains exempt from the quota. Usage is measured in tokens, with more complex tasks consuming a larger portion of the monthly allowance. Most users are expected to retain their current reset cycle. As crypto news continues to evolve, this change could influence how developers and users manage AI resources.

OpenAI announced that, starting in December, the usage quotas for ChatGPT Plus, Pro, and Business plans will be shared between ChatGPT and Codex. OpenAI is integrating Work’s agent capabilities into the main ChatGPT interface. Usage is measured in tokens, with consumption depending on the model and task complexity—more complex tasks consume more tokens; Instant Chat does not count against this shared quota. OpenAI expects most users will not need to wait for quota resets more frequently than they do now. (Jin10 Data APP)
