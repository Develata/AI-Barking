## 可能的吠点（线索，进正文前须回一手核实）

1. 疗效没有：W “waiting for the therapy to take effect before assessing its impact on seizures”；X2 “we don't yet know which will actually reduce seizures”。IT之家标题“成功……治疗癫痫”超出一手来源，一手来源只有“注射完成、小鼠恢复”。
2. N=1：X2 “N=1 per model, on three different animals… not as a proper benchmark”；无对照、无重复。
3. 开颅、缝合、把关、急停都是人：X2 “A licensed veterinary surgeon still performed the incision, craniotomy and suturing”；W “requiring the AI to propose an injection plan for human approval”。智能体负责的是选药、定靶点、进针与注射。
4. 安全限制反过来卡住了手术：W “it refused to modify the code… because its instructions explicitly prohibited doing so”；X2 “we had to fix it ourselves mid-surgery”。
5. 谁修了代码，两个一手版本不同：W 称另一个 Opus 5 智能体改代码且没告诉它是活体手术；CEO 两个帖串都写人工修（X1 “Had to manually fix the code…”，X2 “we had to fix it ourselves mid-surgery”）。
6. 拒绝的理由与归属两版不同（B2）；原文没说“被截断”的机制，Anthropic 未回应置评；CEO 自己写 “WEIRD, never came accross this before”。

## 扫描说法勘误

1. CEO 主帖时间：扫描写“约北京 10-29”；实际 2026-10-28 21:58 北京（13:58 UTC）。HN 首个提交在北京 10-29 17:03。
