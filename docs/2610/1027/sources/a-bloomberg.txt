Takeaways by Bloomberg AI

Alphabet Inc.’s [https://www.bloomberg.com/quote/GOOGL:US] Google announced a new version of its artificial intelligence world model that generates more realistic, real-time virtual environments, advancing a technology that has rattled investors in video game companies.

Genie 4, unveiled Tuesday by the company’s Google DeepMind AI lab, lets users create explorable worlds from text prompts and navigate them with keyboard controls. People can also alter a scene as it unfolds by typing instructions, such as changing the weather or adding a character.
