AA-Omniscience Index

AA-Omniscience v1.1 Index (higher is better) measures knowledge reliability and hallucination. It rewards correct answers, penalizes hallucinations, and has no penalty for refusing to answer. End-to-End Response Time

Seconds to receive a 500 token response. Key components:

Input time: Time to receive the first response token

Thinking time (only for reasoning models): Time reasoning models spend outputting tokens to reason prior to providing an answer. Amount of tokens based on the average reasoning tokens across a diverse set of 60 prompts (methodology details [/methodology/performance-benchmarking]).

Answer time: Time to generate 500 output tokens, based on output speed

Total Parameters

The total number of trainable weights and biases in the model, expressed in billions. These parameters are learned during training and determine the model's ability to process and generate responses.

Active Parameters at Inference Time

The number of parameters actually executed during each inference forward pass, expressed in billions. For Mixture of Experts (MoE) models, a routing mechanism selects a subset of experts per token, resulting in fewer active than total parameters. Dense models use all parameters, so active equals total.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the most intelligent AI model?

Claude Opus 5.5 (Max, Default Fallback) currently leads the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with a score of 58, out of 76 models evaluated.

What are the top AI models?

The top AI models by Intelligence Index are: 1. Claude Opus 5.5 (Max, Default Fallback) (58), 2. Claude Opus 5.5 (Xhigh, Default Fallback) (57), 3. GPT-6 Astra (Max) (57), 4. GPT-6 Astra (Xhigh) (55), and 5. GPT-6 Astra (High) (55).

Which is the fastest AI model?

Celeris-1 is the fastest at 1,562.2 tokens per second, followed by K2 Horizon 375B A23B (929.5 t/s) and Mercury 2.5 (710.5 t/s).

Which is the cheapest AI model?

K2 Horizon 375B A23B is the most affordable at $0.00 per 1M tokens (blended), followed by Command A+ ($0.00) and North Mini Code ($0.00).

Which AI model has the lowest latency?

North Mini Code has the lowest time to first token at 0.49s, followed by Command A+ (0.51s) and NVIDIA Nemotron Nano 9B V2 (Reasoning) (0.55s).

Which is the best open weights AI model?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (Max) is the highest-ranked open weights model with an Intelligence Index score of 32. There are 20 open weights models out of 76 total evaluated.

What are the top open weights AI models?

The top open weights AI models by Intelligence Index are: 1. DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017 (Max) (32), 2. GLM-5.3 (Max) (32), and 3. MiMo-V2.6-Pro (30).

Which is the best reasoning model?

Claude Opus 5.5 (Max, Default Fallback) leads among 74 reasoning models with an Intelligence Index score of 58. Reasoning models use extended thinking to work through complex problems before providing answers.

How are AI models compared on Artificial Analysis?

Models are compared across multiple dimensions including intelligence (quality), pricing, output speed (tokens per second), latency (time to first token), end-to-end response time, and context window size. Performance metrics are measured directly using standardized prompts across 272 models.
