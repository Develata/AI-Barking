L24:   2. Introducing Canva Craft
L25: # Introducing Canva Craft
L26: 
L27: > Our first image model makes Canva Research the world's second most advanced AI lab for image generation.
L28: 
L29: * * *
L30: Today, we’re unveiling Canva Craft, our first state-of-the-art image generation model, developed and trained by 【9†Canva Research⁠(opens in a new tab or window)】, our in-house AI lab. Built specifically for design, Craft makes Canva the world’s second most advanced AI lab for image generation, as measured by rankings from DesignArena, the largest crowdsourced assessor of AI design.
L31: Available now in Canva and to developers through our public API, Craft generates stunning, high-quality images at 2K resolution in any aspect ratio. With precise control and editable outputs, Craft gives everyone from first-time creators to professional designers more flexibility to bring their imagination to life.
L32: 
L33: 【10†Image†content-management-files.canva.com】
L34: # Extraordinary image generation, now available to everyone.
L35: 
L36: Craft is the first foundation model built and trained from scratch by Canva Research, bringing together years of AI research and investment to deliver a deeper understanding of design, results that more closely match what you imagined, and the freedom to easily edit and refine everything you create.
L37: It debuted as the fourth most advanced image generation model on DesignArena, delivering industry-leading visual quality and accurate text rendering to support a wide range of creative tasks.
L38: 
L39: 【11†Image†content-management-files.canva.com】
L40: 
L41: It also makes Canva Research the world’s second most advanced lab for image generation.
L42: 
L43: 【12†Image†content-management-files.canva.com】
L44: Craft outputs rank #1 spot globally for both People & Portraits (out of 87 models) and Landscapes & Nature (out of 85 models). It also ranks #2 for Abstract & Patterns and #4 for Products, placing it among the world's best models across multiple subcategories.
L45: 
L46: 【13†Image†content-management-files.canva.com】
L47: With more than a quarter of a billion people using Canva to create over 400 designs every second, delivering frontier-level performance at scale requires breakthroughs in both compute efficiency and inference speed. Through advanced model distillation, we reduced the number of inference steps required by Craft to a fraction of the original model.
L48: We also developed a custom inference engine optimised for its planning stage, making Craft an orderof magnitude faster than our first internal version. These advances underpin Craft-fast and Craft-ultrafast, two upcoming variants designed to deliver high-quality image generation at a fraction of the cost of leading frontier models.
L49: # A new architecture for image generation
L50: 
L51: Unlike conventional image generation models that generate pixels directly from a prompt, Craft uses a two-stage architecture that separates compositional planning from image rendering. This allows the model to reason about the structure of an image before generating it, delivering greater precision, consistency and control over the final output.
L52: In the first stage, we designed an image planning model to interpret the prompt and write a detailed plan of the composition: which elements appear, their spatial relationships, relative scale, visual hierarchy, and any text that needs to appear in the image. In the second stage, a dedicated image rendering model translates this plan into a high-fidelity image at up to 2K resolution across more than 30 aspect ratios.
L53: By explicitly modelling composition and typography before rendering the final image, Craft can generate complex layouts with greater accuracy, including legible text, precise object placement and consistent visual relationships. This architecture also enables more granular control over individual elements, allowing targeted changes without unnecessarily altering everything else.
L54: The same underlying structure makes Craft outputs particularly well suited to Canva’s Magic Layers, which can transform generated images into fully editable design elements. It also provides the technical foundation for more advanced editing and reference-image capabilities coming in future releases.
L55: # Built for design, evaluated by real designers
L56: While most image models are trained primarily on photographs, Craft's training places a much greater emphasis on design. Its training data includes millions of human-made templates from Canva's content library, spanning professionally designed posters, social posts, presentations, infographics, menus and typography-rich layouts. Unlike flat images, these templates teach the model exactly how layout, hierarchy, and typography fit together.
L57: This gives the model a deeper understanding of the principles behind effective design, rather than simply learning what finished images look like.
L58: Following pre-training, we optimised Craft through supervised fine-tuning and reinforcement learning, incorporating feedback from Canva's professional designers to improve instruction following, typography accuracy, and overall visual quality. For our internal evaluations, Canva's professional designers worked alongside dozens of researchers to assess more than 25,000 pairs of images in head-to-head comparisons with leading models.
L59: When automated evaluations and human judgments differed, we sided with the designer.
L60: 【14†Image†content-management-files.canva.com】
L61: # Generate images with greater precision and control
L62: 
L63: Craft produces photorealistic images that bring together the fine detail, depth, and soft transitions of real-world imagery with the thoughtful composition of design: deliberate hierarchy, alignment, spacing, crisp edges and text that lands where it should.
L64: 
L65: With Craft, it’s easy to:
L66:   * Shape images your way. Craft treats the whole image as an intentional composition of layered elements. JSON boundary boxing makes it easier than ever to convert images into editable layers with Magic Layers, with more accurate and faster results.
L67: 
L68:   * Perfect every detail. Ask for a purple sky, long shadows and a lone tree, and you'll get all three, with the emphasis where you wanted it.
L69:   * Create in any size, for any use case. Choose from one of more than 30 aspect ratios, or enter your own to create everything from social posts or stories to posters or banners.
L70: 
L71:   * Prompt in your language. Prompts can be written in any language, and Craft can create text in 55 languages.
L72: 
L73: 【15†Image†content-management-files.canva.com】
L74: 
L75: As a result, Craft is built to support a wide range of creative work, from polished marketing assets to imaginative visual exploration:
L76:   * Social content and marketing assets: posters, social posts, flyers, presentation visuals, ads and more, with layouts, hierarchy and text that are ready to use.
L77: 
L78:   * Lifestyle and editorial photography: realistic scenes featuring people, places, food, travel and everyday moments.
L79: 
L80:   * Illustration and digital art: flat, vector, painterly and hand-drawn styles.
L81: 
L82:   * People and characters: portraits, groups and fashion, including complex scenes that stay coherent.
L83:   * Nature and environment imagery: landscapes, cityscapes, interiors and architecture.
L84: 
L85:   * Creative and conceptual work: imaginative compositions that bring real and stylised elements together.
L86: 
L87: Availability and how to access
L88: 
L89: Canva Craft is available starting today to all Canva users globally. To start ‘crafting’, open Dream Lab in the bottom left panel, or follow the link 【16†here⁠(opens in a new tab or window)†canva.com】.
L90: 
L91: 【17†Image†content-management-files.canva.com】
L92: Craft is also available via 【18†Public API⁠(opens in a new tab or window)】 and the Canva MCP Server, representing Canva’s push to develop AI models that extend the reach of our technology beyond our core product.
L93: 
L94: Public API: Developers and product teams can build Craft into their own tools, like marketing platforms, e-commerce sites, ad builders and print services. It’s also the quickest way for those to test Craft on their own prompts and compare it with other models.
L95: Canva MCP (Coming Soon): Craft also connects to AI assistants and automated workflows through Canva MCP, so agents can create images that are ready to use in a design. That means an AI assistant can make a launch graphic, a product shot or a social post as one step in a bigger task.
L96: # Backed by Canva Shield
L97: 
L98: Safety is built into every stage of how we develop and deploy AI at Canva. Our models undergo continuous testing and rigorous safety evaluations to identify and mitigate potential risks. Alongside visual quality, we evaluate brand safety, cultural context and the potential for harmful content to ensure our models meet our standards before they're released.
L99: Like all AI products on Canva, Craft is backed by Canva Shield, our comprehensive approach to AI safety. This includes robust input and output moderation, privacy and safety controls, and reporting tools that allow people to flag potentially harmful or misleading content.
L100: # What's next
L101: 
L102: This first version of Craft is just the start, and faster Craft models are already on the way. From here, we’ll keep raising the bar on quality, detail and control to improve things like fine print and technical drawings.
L103: 
L104: Now, we're excited to see what you create and what you think about it. Give it a go 【16†here⁠(opens in a new tab or window)†canva.com】.

L8: Canva Craft, our first image generation model, developed and trained by 【8†Canva Research†www.canva.com】, our in-house AI lab, is now available in the Connect API image generation endpoint. Craft was built from scratch by Canva Research and launched today in Dream Lab and the Public API. More on the model itself in the newsroom post: 【9†https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-craft/†www.canva.com】
L9: Our ranking on Design Arena leaderboard 【10†https://www.designarena.ai/leaderboard?tab=image&section=image†www.designarena.ai】
L10: 
L11: What’s new
L12:   * A new `model` value, `craft_v1_2k`, on `POST /v1/image-generations`. Pin it to generate with Craft.
L13:   * Craft renders at up to 2K and is built for design work: infographics, posters, slides, people and portraits, landscapes, abstract art and patterns, products, and marketing material.
L14:   * Prompts in any language, text in the image in 55 languages.
L15:   * On DesignArena it ranks first for People & Portraits and for Landscapes & Nature, second for Abstract & Patterns.
L16: 
L17: How to use it
L18: Same endpoint and flow as before, with the model pinned:
L19: 
L20:     `POST /v1/image-generations
L21:     {
L22:       "prompt": "A clean infographic poster titled 'Ocean Plastics 2026' with three stat callouts and a wave illustration",
L23:       "model": "craft_v1_2k",
L24:       "aspect_ratio": "portrait_2_3",
L25:       "idempotency_key": "9c1f2d3e-5b6a-4c7d-8e9f-0a1b2c3d4e5f"
L26:     }
L27:     `
L28: Then poll `GET /v1/image-generations/{jobId}` until `status` is `success`. The download URL is valid for 24 hours, or set `asset_upload.type` to `upload` to save the image to the user’s uploads.
L29:   * Craft supports every aspect ratio the endpoint offers: `square`, `landscape`, `portrait`, `more aspect ratios coming soon.`
L30:   * Requires a user access token with the `asset:write` scope. [confirm: client-credentials integrations stay on the allowlist until developer-funded billing ships; say “contact us on this thread” or leave out]
L31: 
L32: Usage notes
L33:   * Craft plans before it renders, so a job takes longer than the other models. Expect roughly 50 to 60 seconds end to end and poll with backoff rather than a tight loop.
L34:   * Starting a job uses the user’s AI credits, same as the other models.
L35:   * If Craft is unavailable when pinned the job fails with `model_not_available`. Retry, or drop the pin to let Canva pick another model.
L36:   * Prompts and outputs go through the same safety checks as the rest of the API. Rejected prompts return `unsafe_input`.
L37:   * Craft is covered by Canva Shield, including input and output moderation.

L42:   * Model descriptions and the full list of values are in the `model` field of the reference.
L43:   * Craft on the Canva MCP Server is coming soon.
