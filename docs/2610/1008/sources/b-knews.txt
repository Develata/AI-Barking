Anthropic to Enforce User Bans for Abuse of Claude AI

 [https://knews.media/author/knews/]

 Ashley Davis [https://knews.media/author/knews/]7 hours ago
0 40 Less than a minute 

Anthropic has announced that it will begin banning users from its Claude AI platform for abusive behavior and insults. These new regulations are set to take effect on November 12. The company aims to foster a respectful environment for users interacting with the AI.
