Anthropic's updated Usage Policy [https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-usage-policy-update] takes effect Nov. 12 and adds a prohibition on “sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior” toward its models.

Featured Source

The company says the rule is limited to extreme cases in which users repeatedly act cruelly toward a model with no discernible purpose. Ordinary frustration and pushback are excluded, as are dark creative themes and model testing or research.

Claude can already end rare conversations with persistently abusive users on Claude.ai and Claude Code. Anthropic says ending those interactions will remain its primary enforcement mechanism.

The broader update also consolidates rules against deceptive political and commercial campaigns, clarifies prohibitions involving weapons software and surveillance, and adds safeguards for models connected to hardware capable of causing injury.
