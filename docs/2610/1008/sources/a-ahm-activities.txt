Activities

The Association for Human Mathematics (AHM) is a professional organization run collectively by its membership. General meetings, open to all members, will be held regularly. The AHM has an Organizing Committee, run on a volunteer basis, and facilitates working groups, including on Teaching, Journals, Preprints, Careers, Coalitions, and Communications. Members can join these and other working groups when they sign up. Our organization is designed so that working groups can start their own projects and build community, while also issuing recommendations for norms and policies in the mathematical sciences in general.

Most working groups meet on a weekly basis. To join a working group, contact a facilitator.

Organizing Committee: Coordinates the overall activities of the AHM. 

Contact: Max Weinreich (CUNY Baruch College).

Communications: Writes materials for our website, communicates with media. 

Contacts: Lorenzo Riva (CMSA) and Gugliemo Nocera (IHÉS).

Journals Working Group: Currently discussing author consent for LLM reviewing and writing recommendations for journals.

Contacts: Joshua Grochow (University of Colorado Boulder) and Silvain Rideau-Kikuchi (CNRS, École normale supérieure).

Preprints Working Group: Currently discussing how to protect preprints from AI training and how to appropriately label preprints. 

Contacts: Alicia Lima (University of Chicago) and Cécile Gachet (Ruhr University Bochum).

Careers Working Group. Currently discussing identifying concerns and brainstorming ideas around careers, from the point of view of applicants, reviewers, and departments/institutions. 

Contacts: Heidi Goodson (Brooklyn College and The Graduate Center, City University of New York) and Vesna Stojanoska (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign).

Teaching Working Group. Discussing recommendations for undergraduate and graduate education.

Contacts: Benjamin Braun (University of Kentucky) and Jeremiah Heller (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign).

Coalitions Working Group. Outreach to professional organizations and pro-human academic organizations in other fields.

Contacts: Jean-Baptiste Cadart (Université de Brest Occidentale & Chalmers University of Technology) and Martin Cigarroa Urrutia (Queen's University).
