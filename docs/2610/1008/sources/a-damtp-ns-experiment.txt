                  1. P OTENTIAL MISTRANSLATIONS OF THE NAVIER –S TOKES NL PROOF

1.1. AI generated list (with some human input) of suggested mistranslations. This report details an ex-
periment done to compare OpenAI’s NL (natural language) proof Finite time blowup for Navier–Stokes with
the accompanying Lean formalisation at commit f9e8bc5. The motivation for this experiment comes from
the ideas discussed in the paper Navier–Stokes lost in translation – Why Lean verification of AI autoformal-
isation does not guarantee correct natural language proofs. The twelve comparisons below are generated as a
collaboration between ChatGPT and Claude, with some human input.
   Warning – Potential hallucinations: Since the list below is generated with the help of AI, and has not
been fully manually checked, hallucinations may occur. Hence, the list should be taken as a suggestion of
mistranslations that could be checked by others. The purpose of the list is to give an indication of potential
mistranslations.

6. Frozen scale jets and Euler derivatives give equivalent seminorms
Paper: The normalized profile estimates (5.42) and (5.46) use compositions of q∂q , ∂X and ∂η . Repeating
q∂q differentiates its coefficient q as well as the profile. The diagonal bound in (5.46) is imposed for n ≥
max{2, |I|}, where |I| is the derivative order.
Lean: SlowBorelBase.blownJet takes ordinary derivatives of F (qσ, X, η) at σ = 1, keeping the base
scale q fixed. For the pure scale directions, this means
                        ∂σ2 F (qσ, X, η) σ=1 = q 2 ∂q2 F,     (q∂q )2 F = q 2 ∂q2 F + q∂q F.
The AdmissibleScales.blown condition bounds the tensor of order m of the nth summand by 2−n q hn
for n ≥ 1, m ≤ n + 2, 0 < q ≤ 1, and (X, η) in the chosen compact profile set. The theorem
exists admissibleScales constructs a schedule satisfying these conditions.
Difference: The operators are unequal already for F (q) = q: the displayed second derivatives are 0 and q.
This example compares operators, not the selected profiles. At every fixed order the Euler and frozen derivative
families are related by an invertible triangular change of basis with constant coefficients. The same holds for
mixed profile derivatives. Consequently they give equivalent all-order O(q 2h ) estimates. Transferring the
displayed diagonal bound can introduce an order-dependent multiplicative constant; it does not automatically
retain the exact coefficient 2−n . No failure of either the paper’s asymptotic estimates or its numerical bound
is established here.

11. The local hierarchy is completed before global repair
Paper: Section 5 builds a formal expansion in powers q 2nh , n ≥ 0. Its coefficients are profiles of the velocity
and pressure in (X, η). At order n, Lemma 5.1 solves the equations near the axis using the already finalized
lower orders. Lemma 5.2 then extends the current profiles and imposes five integral moment conditions, which
confine their stress to an annulus. Only then does the induction proceed to order n + 1.
Lean: SlowRecursion.hierarchy first recursively constructs every local coefficient, without global
moment repair. schemeFromHierarchy cuts these local coefficients to produce the seeds for a second
recursion. In that global recursion, exists step repairs the current seed and recomputes its pressure source
and moments using the already repaired global predecessors.
Difference: The code separates the local and global inductions, whereas the paper interleaves them. The
second recursion takes a completed local hierarchy as input. The theorem profiles inner eq proves
agreement of all four velocity/pressure profile components on a common inner region, including the axis, and
profiles moments proves the global moment conditions. These are the connecting results needed for the
separated construction. This demonstrates a reorganization of the construction and its verification; it does not
by itself demonstrate that the resulting coefficients differ from an admissible implementation of the paper’s
induction.
