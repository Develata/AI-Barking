 Anthropic Bans Being Cruel to Claude

 New policy only applies in 'extreme cases,' company says

 By
 Rob Quinn [/bio/2261/rob-quinn.html]
 withNewser.AI [/policyai.aspx]

 Posted Oct 8, 2026 3:50 PM CDT

 [https://www.facebook.com/sharer/sharer.php]

 [sms:?body=I saw this Newser story and thought of you. Read it!%0d%0a%0d%0aAnthropic Bans Being Cruel to Claude%0d%0a%0d%0ahttps://www.newser.com/story/397823/anthropic-bans-being-cruel-to-claude.html]

 See 1 more photo

 Anthropic doesn't want you tormenting its chatbot. The AI company behind Claude has updated its usage policy [https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-usage-policy-update] to bar "sustained and needless abusive or cruel behavior" toward the model, the Verge [https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1008100/anthropic-new-usage-policy-abuse-claude] reports. The move is "meant to apply only in extreme cases, where users repeatedly act cruelly toward our models, with no discernible purpose," Anthropic said in the policy update posted Thursday. "It does not apply to common versions of user frustration, pushback, dark creative themes, or model testing and research."

 In an August update, Claude was trained to cut off conversations after "persistently harmful or abusive user interactions," TechCrunch [https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/anthropic-changes-usage-policy-to-ban-model-abuse-and-election-interference/] reports. Anthropic said ending interactions will be "the primary enforcement mechanism" of the policy. The company didn't say whether violating the policy could lead to other actions, including user bans.

 The broader update targets real-world risks, the Verge reports. It codifies bans on deceptive political or commercial campaigns, voter misinformation, and covert influence operations using fake accounts; expands its long-standing prohibition on weapons development to software that operates or arms drones and other systems; and tightens restrictions on surveillance, including tracking people without consent or using Claude to suggest who to investigate or arrest. 

 "We've further sharpened our section on elections, focusing it specifically on disallowing the use of Claude to deceive voters or disrupt elections (for instance, by spreading false information about candidates or how to vote, impersonating candidates or election officials, or trying to suppress turnout)," the policy update states. "We've renamed it Do Not Undermine Democratic Processes to reflect that scope."
