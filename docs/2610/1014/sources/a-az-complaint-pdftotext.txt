9                                I. INTRODUCTION

10  1. Generative artificial intelligence (“AI”) is set to define the twenty-first

11 century. Although the public had virtually no access to AI until November 2022, recent

12 reports indicate that about half of American adults have now used AI, with roughly one in

13 four Americans doing so daily.1 AI-related spending has also become a major growth

14 engine for the U.S. economy.

15  2. Nevertheless, the transformative power of AI also comes with significant

16 danger. Experts and insiders in the AI field nearly universally agree that AI companies

17 must implement reasonable guardrails to protect the public from the foreseeable risk of

18 harm. Even OpenAI and its CEO, Sam Altman, have warned that getting AI wrong could

19 have catastrophic results.2 And recent developments in AI have suggested that risk is both

20 credible and closer than anyone previously thought.

20 reaffirming its commitment to the public good and nonprofit nature. In its post, OpenAI
