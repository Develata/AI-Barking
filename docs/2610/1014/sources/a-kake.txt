DES MOINES (KAKE) — Kris Kobach, Kansas attorney general, announced Wednesday that he is suing OpenAI [https://www.kake.com/news/technology/openai-discloses-at-least-6-new-concerning-incidents/article_d98306b2-33b0-4344-ab1e-ce6e1a3603d4.html] for “deceiving Kansans about the safety of ChatGPT.”

Kobach wrote in a social media post that state attorneys general have “the power to move quickly to protect consumers” and a “responsibility to take action now, without delay.”

“While Congress has deliberated about AI for more than a year, significant harm has been inflicted upon our citizens by OpenAI,” Kobach wrote in an X post [https://x.com/KrisKobach1787/status/2110462900624326825?s=20].

Today in Des Moines, I announced a lawsuit against OpenAI for deceiving Kansans about the safety of ChatGPT. While Congress has deliberated about AI for more than a year, significant harm has been inflicted upon our citizens by OpenAI. State attorneys general have the power to move quickly to protect consumers, and I believe we have a responsibility to take action now, without delay.

— Kris W. Kobach (@KrisKobach1787) October 14, 2026 [https://x.com/KrisKobach1787/status/2110462900624326825?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw]

According to a petition, OpenAI misled “the public into believing that ChatGPT is a safe, teen-appropriate tool that puts safety first, OpenAI has caused consumers in Kansas and elsewhere to trust and rely on ChatGPT more. This deepened trust and dependence has driven up ChatGPT’s user base and engagement, enabled it to collect vast quantities of sensitive and personal information, increased.”

The petition also alleges that OpenAI representations to the public “do not align with reality.”

“Accordingly, Plaintiff the State of Kansas (“Attorney General,” “State of Kansas,” or “State”), ex rel. Kris Kobach, brings this action to compel Defendants to comply with their obligations under Kansas’s Consumer Protection Laws … and to hold Defendants accountable for the harms they have inflicted on Kansans, most notably vulnerable minors, as a result of their willful, dangerous, reckless, and unlawful conduct.”
