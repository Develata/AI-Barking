Beijing: Chinese artificial intelligence startup Moonshot AI apologised on Monday for rate limiting and ‌unnecessary quota consumption after its Kimi K3.1 Pro launch.

Here are some details:

Beijing-based Moonshot said on its Kimi X account that ⁠it was offering to reset the monthly quota of all subscribers by 25%, which it said equals one week's usage.

Subscribers with usage below 25% reset to zero.

Moonshot said it also adjusted parameters to reduce ‌unnecessary ⁠subagent calls and output length, referring to the delegation of tasks among AI agents and the total ⁠number of tokens generated in response to a prompt.

Moonshot previously had issues with ⁠computing capacity after K3 was launched.

Chinese AI labs are suffering ⁠from computing shortages following US chip export controls.
