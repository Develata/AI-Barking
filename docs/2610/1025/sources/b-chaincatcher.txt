 Kimi apologizes for the consumption of the K3.1 Pro quota and will restore 25% of the monthly quota

 2026-10-26 08:21:40

Kimi officially posted on X, apologizing for the usage frequency limits and unnecessary quota consumption of the K3.1 Pro subscription plan, acknowledging that the issue was caused by the platform itself and should have been resolved earlier.

Kimi will perform a one-time quota reset for all subscription plan users, restoring usage equivalent to 25% of the total monthly quota, which is approximately one week's quota; if the current used quota is less than 25%, the used amount will be reset to zero. The relevant adjustments will be reflected in the usage progress bar before the end of the day in Pacific Standard Time.

Kimi has adjusted the subscription plan parameters and will reduce unnecessary sub-agent calls and output length to lower quota consumption.
