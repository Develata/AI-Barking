BEIJING, Oct 26 (Reuters) - Chinese artificial intelligence startup Moonshot AI ‌apologised on Monday for rate ‌limiting and unnecessary quota consumption after its ​Kimi K3.1 Pro launch.

Here are some details:

• Beijing-based Moonshot said on its Kimi X account that it was ‌offering to ⁠reset the monthly quota of all subscribers by 25%, ⁠which it said equals one week's usage

• Subscribers with usage below ​25% reset ​to zero
