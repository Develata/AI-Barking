Moonshot AI Apologizes for Kimi K3.1 Pro Rate Limiting, Resets 25% Monthly Quota for Subscribers

PA一线·1 min read [/contributors/166]Follow

PANews, October 26: Kimi (Moonshot AI) said in a post on X that it apologizes for rate limiting and abnormal quota consumption issues with the K3.1 Pro subscription plans. Today, all subscription plan users will receive a one-time quota reset, restoring 25% of their monthly usage quota; users who have used less than 25% of their quota will have their usage reset to zero, equivalent to about one week's usage quota. Previously, Moonshot AI released the Kimi K3.1 Pro model on October 24.

Original link [https://x.com/Kimi_Moonshot/status/2114450610724708492]
