CEO Perspective

Thank you for joining us today. Revenue growth in the third quarter
accelerated to 6%, above the base case we laid out in July. This
performance reflects acceleration across our segments, led by Software
growth of 9%, Infrastructure growth of 5%, and modest improvement in
Consulting. We entered the quarter focused on disciplined execution,
which also drove strong performance in profitability and free cash flow.
These results demonstrate the strength of our portfolio, the continued
value clients place on mission-critical platforms and our confidence in
both our strategy and the outlook for the balance of the year.

Technology innovation, in both hybrid infrastructure and Super
Intelligence, is leading to better business performance. This quarter’s
results reinforce the strategic choices we have made over the last several
years to advance IBM as a software-led hybrid cloud and AI platform
company. In Software, Red Hat again grew 11% with bookings growth
accelerating back to the high teens. OpenShift ARR growth accelerated to
over 30% as organizations scale Super Intelligence applications and
agents into production, reinforcing our conviction that we remain in the
early stages of a long-term application modernization cycle. Transaction
Processing grew 10%, underscoring the strategic importance of the
mainframe and validating our view that the softness we saw in the second
quarter reflected timing rather than underlying demand. Data grew 20%,
supported by continued strength in Confluent. Infrastructure was up 5%,
driven by continued momentum in Distributed Infrastructure, growing
32% this quarter. AI continues to increase demand for storage and we are
gaining share through differentiated offerings across Flash, Fusion, tape,
and IBM Z storage, including AI-enabled capabilities that help clients
scale and manage data for AI. Consulting revenue grew 2% as clients

Now turning to our AI strategy. Enterprise AI is entering its next phase.
Deploying more models or building more agents will not be enough. The
advantage will belong to companies that can operate AI securely,

recognizing that AI is only as effective as the system around it: the data

Governance matters as much as intelligence. As agents multiply,
enterprises need to know who each agent is, what authority it has and
what it can access. HashiCorp Vault allows clients to register, authorize,
audit, and revoke agents and secure their credentials. IBM Agent Identity

Finally, Quantum remains a strategic area of differentiation for IBM and a
significant long-term opportunity. In July, three independent
demonstrations of quantum advantage were achieved on IBM hardware
with the University of Chicago, Algorithmiq, and Qedma, providing further
evidence that quantum computing is moving from scientific promise
towards practical value. Our Nighthawk r2 processor has achieved
important roadmap milestones across speed, scale, and quality, delivering
7,500 gates, executing 100,000 circuits per second and enabling
performance improvements of up to 19 times over prior-generation
systems. That performance changes both time-to-solution and the
economics of quantum computation: workloads costing approximately
$100 to run on IBM could cost up to $10 million on slower competitive
modalities. Recently, IBM was selected for Stage C, the third phase of the
Quantum Benchmarking Initiative led by DARPA, the United States
Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency, another validation of the
strength of IBM's path to building a fault-tolerant quantum computer.

Commercial momentum is also growing. This year, IBM Quantum has

aerospace, telecommunications, financial services, and manufacturing.

Financial Highlights

Thanks Arvind. In the third quarter, we delivered solid results with 6%
revenue growth, 70 basis points of operating pre-tax margin expansion
and 11% diluted operating earnings per share growth. And through the
first nine months, we generated about $8 billion of free cash flow, up 8%
year-over-year. These results reflect strength across our portfolio and
solid execution against our priorities. Let me walk through the
performance in each of our segments.

Software revenue growth accelerated four points sequentially to 9% this
quarter, including over four points of organic growth. This performance
was driven by our high value, annual recurring revenue base which grew
8% to $24.9 billion. Three of our four Software sub-segments grew
revenue double-digits, led by the combination of strong new business
performance and pipeline closure rates, including the successful closure
of around two thirds of the large deals that slipped from the second
quarter. Our results represent the high end of the range we outlined in
July, reinforcing our confidence in the strength of our portfolio.

Turning to our sub-segments, Red Hat revenue grew 11% again this
quarter, fueled by bookings accelerating back to the high teens. Lightwell
was recently made generally available, and we have strong early demand,
with a pipeline that is already approaching $700 million. OpenShift is now
a $2.4 billion ARR business with growth accelerating to more than 30%.
Our virtualization offering continues to scale, with nearly $800 million in
contracts signed since early 2024 and about $750 million in pipeline.

Data grew 20%, driven by another strong performance from Confluent,
with bookings growth of about 20%. Transaction Processing grew 10% in

Summary

Now, let me talk about what we are seeing going forward. The strength
and diversity of our portfolio, disciplined capital allocation, and sustained
productivity execution underpin our revenue growth, earnings expansion
and free cash flow performance. Through the first nine months of the year,
we delivered over 4% revenue growth, 9% operating pre-tax income
growth, 10% operating earnings per share growth, and 8% free cash flow
growth. We continue to expect revenue growth for 2026 in the range of
four-to-five percent and are raising our expectation for free cash flow
growth to more than $1 billion.

Ninety days ago, we provided a full-year revenue growth range, with profit
and free cash flow expectations anchored to a 4% base case. We continue
to take the same approach for the full year, anchoring profit, free cash flow
and segment expectations to that base case. Given our strong third
quarter Software performance, our confidence in the full-year outlook has
increased. We now expect full-year Software growth of approximately 7%
to 8%, with about 7% as the revised base case. For the full year, we
expect double-digit growth in Hybrid Cloud, mid-teens growth in Data and
low-single digit growth in Automation. For Transaction Processing, we are
raising our expectations for the full year with growth expected to be flat.
Infrastructure growth accelerated in the third quarter with significantly
stronger program-to-program performance of z17 underscoring the
durability of the platform and the value it delivers to clients. Combined
with continued strength in Distributed Infrastructure, we continue to
expect low-single-digit Infrastructure revenue growth for the full year. In
Consulting, backlog quality and momentum in generative AI continue to
support a modest acceleration to low-to-mid single-digit revenue growth
for the year.
