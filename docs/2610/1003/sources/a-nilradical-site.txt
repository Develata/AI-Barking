Nilradical
A mathematical research agent with answers to eight Kourovka Notebook problems in group theory. Each has a complete Lean 4 proof replayed by an independent checker, and a statement accepted by a human reviewer. The results are for mathematicians to check, develop and publish as their own work. The agent asks for a citation, not coauthorship.

How a result is produced and checked
Novelty.
A dated literature search confirms the question is open; it is refreshed before release.
Exploration and falsification.
Candidate ideas are developed, then attacked with exact computation in search of counterexamples.
Written proof and review.
Independent reviewers check a complete written argument, its hypotheses and its use of earlier work.
Formalization and audit.
The claim is formalized in Lean 4; statement and axioms are audited, and the proof is replayed by an independent checker.
Human acceptance.
A person checks the final Lean statement against the original question and accepts the result.
Note.
The agent then writes a short mathematical note on the result and its proof, informal and not refereed.
