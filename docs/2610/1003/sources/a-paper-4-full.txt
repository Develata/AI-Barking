       Abstract. Del Pia and Khajavirad [DPK26] introduced the complete edge relaxation for binary

       polynomial optimization and generalized triangle inequalities for length-three α-cycles. We study
       the relaxation obtained by adding all switchings of these inequalities to the complete edge relaxation
       on the completed support of a single length-three α-cycle. If

                                                           H = cl({e1, e2, e3})

       and

                            A = (e1 ∩ e2) \ e3, B = (e2 ∩ e3) \ e1, C = (e1 ∩ e3) \ e2,

       then the cycle-based relaxation is equal to the multilinear polytope if and only if

                                                           |A| = |B| = |C| = 1.

       The same characterization remains true for the stronger relaxation obtained by imposing the
       switched generalized triangle inequalities for every no-private length-three α-cycle contained in H.
       No restriction is needed on the common intersection or on the private vertices of the three maximal
       edges. The positive direction is proved by a slice-wise gluing argument, while the negative direction
       lifts a four-variable parity obstruction and shows that it survives every applicable switched triangle
       inequality. We remark on the use of AI in this research, see Statement of AI Use.

           The purpose of this paper is to determine exactly when the generalized triangle inequalities close
       the remaining gap on the completed support of a single length-three α-cycle. The corresponding
       characterization question is stated at the end of [DPK26, Section 5.2]. We consider two relaxations.
       The first adds all switchings of the generalized triangle inequalities for the distinguished private-free
       reduction of the three maximal edges. The second is stronger: it adds the same inequalities for
       every no-private length-three α-cycle contained in the completed support.

           For an α-cycle of length three, (e1, e2, e3), in a hypergraph H = (V, E), write
                                                             T = e1 ∩ e2 ∩ e3,

                             A = (e1 ∩ e2) \ e3, B = (e2 ∩ e3) \ e1, C = (e1 ∩ e3) \ e2,

                                            Di = ei \ (ej ∪ ek), {i, j, k} = {1, 2, 3}.
       Thus

                       e1 = T ⊔ A ⊔ C ⊔ D1, e2 = T ⊔ A ⊔ B ⊔ D2, e3 = T ⊔ B ⊔ C ⊔ D3.
       For a length-three α-cycle, the three pair-only blocks A, B, C are nonempty, while T and the private
       blocks Di may be arbitrary.

           Our main theorem is a sharp classification: both cycle-based relaxations are exact if and only if
       the three pair-only blocks are singletons. The common block and private blocks play no role in the
       answer. When T = D1 = D2 = D3 = ∅, the positive case is the ordinary graph triangle.

           The proof has two complementary parts, both relying on a probabilistic interpretation of the
       multilinear polytope originally developed by Pitowsky [Pit91], wherein a point of the multilinear
       polytope corresponds to a probability distribution on {0, 1}V . If one pair-only block contains
       two vertices, we embed a four-variable parity construction into the three maximal edges. It gives
       mutually consistent local laws with no global realization. We then show that the resulting point
       survives every switched generalized triangle inequality coming from every no-private length-three
       α-cycle in the completed support. Conversely, when A, B, C are singletons, we condition on each
       state of the common block T . In every slice, the three pairwise marginals on the remaining three
       variables admit a common extension precisely because the switched generalized triangle inequalities
       make a natural interval of feasible triple masses nonempty. The slice laws are then glued and the
       private blocks are restored by conditional sampling.

Human                                                  7. Conclusion

           For the completed support of a length-three α-cycle, the exactness of generalized triangle
       inequalities is controlled entirely by the three pair-only intersections. The complete edge relaxation
       together with all switchings of the distinguished generalized triangle inequalities is exact precisely
       when each pair-only block consists of a single vertex. Adding the corresponding inequalities from
       every other no-private length-three α-cycle in the completion does not enlarge the class of exact
       instances.

           The proof separates the roles of the different parts of the Venn decomposition. Private blocks can
       be removed and restored by conditional kernels, and the common block can be handled slice by slice.
       The obstruction appears only when a pair-only block contains two vertices, where a four-variable
       parity construction creates locally compatible edge laws that cannot be globally realized while
       remaining invisible to all of the switched generalized triangle inequalities considered here.

           A natural next question is whether analogous sharp classifications can be obtained for longer α-
       cycles or for stronger families of inequalities beyond the length-three generalized triangle inequalities.
