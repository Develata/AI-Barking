Comparison Summary

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) is amongst the leading models in intelligence and reasonably priced when comparing to other open weight models of similar size. It's also notably fast, however very verbose. The model supports text and image input, outputs text, and has a 1M tokens context window.

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) scores 39 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among comparable models (median: 19). When evaluating the Intelligence Index, it generated 250M tokens, which is very verbose in comparison to the median of 140M.

Pricing for DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) is $0.30 per 1M input tokens (moderately priced, median: $0.43) and $1.20 per 1M output tokens (moderately priced, median: $1.68). On average, it costs $0.49 per task to evaluate DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) on the Intelligence Index.

At 231 tokens per second, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) is notably fast (78).

Total Parameters

The total number of trainable weights and biases in the model, expressed in billions. These parameters are learned during training and determine the model's ability to process and generate responses.

Active Parameters at Inference Time

The number of parameters actually executed during each inference forward pass, expressed in billions. For Mixture of Experts (MoE) models, a routing mechanism selects a subset of experts per token, resulting in fewer active than total parameters. Dense models use all parameters, so active equals total.

Common questions about DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max)

When was DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) released?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) was released on September 10, 2026.

Who created DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max)?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) was created by DeepSeek.

How intelligent is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max)?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) scores 39 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, placing it well above average among other open weight models of similar size (median: 19).

How fast is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max)?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) generates output at 231.2 tokens per second (based on the median across providers serving the model), which is well above average compared to other open weight models of similar size (median: 77.9 t/s).

What is the latency of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max)?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) has a time to first token (TTFT) of 0.91s (based on the median across providers serving the model), which is very competitive compared to other open weight models of similar size (median: 1.69s).

How much does DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) cost?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) costs $0.30 per 1M input tokens (better than average, median: $0.43) and $1.20 per 1M output tokens (better than average, median: $1.68), based on the median across providers serving the model.

What is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) API pricing?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) costs $0.30 per 1M input tokens and $1.20 per 1M output tokens (based on the median across providers serving the model). For a blended rate (7:2:1 cache hit/input/output ratio), this is $0.18 per 1M tokens. Pricing may vary by provider. Compare provider pricing [/models/deepseek-v4-1-flash/providers]

How verbose is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max)?

When evaluated on the Intelligence Index, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) generated 250M output tokens, which is at the higher end compared to other open weight models of similar size (median: 140M).

Is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) a reasoning model?

Yes, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) is a reasoning model. It uses extended thinking or chain-of-thought reasoning to work through complex problems before providing an answer.

What input modalities does DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) support?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) supports text and image input.

What output modalities does DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) support?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) supports text output.

Can DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) process images?

Yes, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) supports image input and can analyze, describe, and answer questions about images.

Is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) multimodal?

Yes, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) is multimodal. It can process text and image input and generate text output.

What is the context window of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max)?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) has a context window of 1.0M tokens. This determines how much text and conversation history the model can process in a single request.

Is DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) open source?

Yes, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) is open weights. The model weights are publicly available and can be downloaded for self-hosting.

How many parameters does DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) have?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) has 552 billion parameters (16 billion active).

What are the active parameters of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max)?

DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (Max) is a Mixture of Experts (MoE) model with 552 billion total parameters, but only 16 billion active parameters are used during inference.
