DeepSeek 发布开源权重模型 DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017，在 DeepSWE 1.1 基准上取得 77% Pass@1，据称超过 GLM-5.3、Kimi K3、GPT-5.6 Sol 和 Opus 5。模型已上线 DeepSeek Chat、API 和 HuggingFace，官方称相比上一版性能更强、任务完成更快、输出更短，并在性能与成本上实现 Pareto 改进。

正文

BREAKING 🔥: DeepSeek released DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017, a new open-weight model with frontier performance.

It scores 77% on DeepSWE 1.1 benchmark, outperforming GLM-5.3, Kimi K3 and models like GPT-5.6 Sol and Opus 5.

The model is now available on DeepSeek Chat and APIs as well as HuggingFace.

It also extremely cheap! 👀

引用DeepSeek@deepseek_ai

🚀 DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017 is here!

Stronger benchmark performance, faster task completion, and shorter outputs than the previous version—a Pareto improvement in performance and cost. Now live on our website, app, and API.

🛠️ Optimized for @DeepSeekHarness, Tencent CodeBuddy, WorkBuddy, and more. Upgrade to DeepSeek Harness v0.2.1-rc.1 or later for the best experience with the new model.
