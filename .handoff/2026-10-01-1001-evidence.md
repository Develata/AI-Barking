### C. Anthropic 机器人就业暴露研究

页面 https://www.anthropic.com/research/what-work-can-robots-do （curl 可抓；已读全文）：

- 日期 “Sep 30, 2026”；有 “Download the PDF”。
- Key findings：“Robots … can perform three-quarters of physical tasks in the US, making up 34% of working hours, but mostly in limited settings.”；“Robots are cost-competitive for just 0.3% of job tasks. - 方法：约 900 个职业、约 19,000 个 O*NET 任务；“we have Claude score task descriptions”，Claude 联网搜索具体机器人并给出暴露等级 E0–E3（E1 专门搭建的机器人环境，如工厂流水线；E2 结构化的人类工作设施，如物流仓库；E3 非结构化环境，如城市道路）；7,594 个任务被判为 physical。Figure 2 文字：E0 约四分之一、E1 “half of physical tasks”、E2 22%、E3 “only 2%”。Figure 3：占全部任务时间，认知与人际 54%，物理 46%；E0 12%、E1 23%、E2 10%、E3 1%；“74% of physical work, or 34% of all work”。
- 成本：“For robots to be cost-competitive for 10% of human work today, costs would need to decline about 70%. At a 3% decline per year, that would take around 40 years.”；“robot prices have declined roughly 3% per year since the 1990s”；“cost-competitive for just 0.3% today. Still, that includes roughly 300,000 workers for whom robots can do 95% of their tasks.”
- 打包工例子（Figure 7 上方）：机器人“over $2 million to purchase and install, but replace the yearly work of around 14 workers”，10 年寿命、8% 资本成本，每年约 $45,000 对人工约 $49,000，“about $2,500 less per year”；“these cost estimates are approximate”；“Claude estimates annual costs”。

- 页面有 Appendix A–F 与 Figure 1–8（含 Figure 7 机器人成本、Figure 8 假设降价下的暴露工作）。
