## 已知情况（Claude 已读 Anthropic 页，须独立复核）

- https://www.anthropic.com/research/the-missing-map-of-the-sky ，页面“Oct 8, 2026”，published 2026-10-08T20:59:00Z（= 北京 10/9 04:59），modified 10-09T09:29:17Z。署名为 Brice Ménard，“an astrophysicist at Johns Hopkins University and a researcher at Anthropic”。
- 原句：“About a third of this map, including much of the galactic plane, was predicted with Claude Science using the method outlined in this post. Additional layers of the map label each pixel as “measured” or “predicted” and provide uncertainty estimates”。
- GALEX（2003–2013）约 38,000 次观测覆盖约三分之二天空，刻意避开亮星与银盘；Swift、FIMS/SPEAR 补了部分；“Roughly a third of the sky has never been observed in UV.”
- 方法：inpainting + 用已有 UV 的三分之二天空学 UV 与其他波段（可见光、红外、射电）的关系，推到其余三分之一；再叠加从 Gaia 推断的 1 亿多颗恒星的 UV。
- 验证：“estimate the hidden data to within about 10% of the real UV measurements, a difference almost imperceptible to the human eye.”
