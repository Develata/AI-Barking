## 已知情况（Claude 2026-11-03 已读，须独立复核）

- 一手：作者博文 https://rikiyat.github.io/blog/weight-space-programming/ （作者署名四人、单位 MIT CSAIL，须逐一核作者名与单位；页面日期与发布时刻未核）与实验日志仓库 https://github.com/RikiyaT/lm_understand-logs 。作者自己的研究博客，按 L2 记（研究者署名发言），不是 MIT 官方新闻稿；查 MIT/CSAIL 官网有没有官方稿，没有就写“未见”。OpenAI 是否参与或背书（作者是否 OpenAI 员工、是否用了 OpenAI 提供的权限）须查。
- Claude 已读到原句（须逐字复核）：“asked to make Qwen3.5-0.8B better at math without gradients, Astra identifies an issue in its weights causing reasoning loops that Qwen’s own model card warns about, and fixes this by natively editing 0.016% of the weights. With the same sampling settings, this weight correction raises MATH-500 from 7.9% to 48.1% and generalizes out of the box to instruction following, raising IFEval by 3x.”
- 同文实验 8 细节：“85 of its first 96 practice answers ran to the 32,768-token limit”；“subtracted that direction from 64 output columns in each (131,072 weights)”；“With 50 forward passes, MATH-500 rose from 7.9% to 48.1% and GSM8K from 13.8% to 67.1% … IFEval … rose from 21.0% to 62.3%”；“with 10 or 25 forward passes the loops barely went away, and with 50 it fell from 93% to 33%”；采样设置 “temperature 0.6, top-p 0.95, top-k 20, presence penalty 1.5. Qwen’s model card notes that this model is prone to thinking loops”。
- 作者自己的限定（最关键的吠点依据）：“Fixing loops, however, is not the same as making a model better at math: it lets the model finish answers it could already work out.” Gemma 3 270M（只有 8.5% 答案触顶）上 “Astra could not improve it … none moved MATH-500 by more than about a point”；“Editing weights could remove an obstacle that hid an ability, but it could not add an ability the model lacked.”
- 其他实验（博文前半）：从权重认出游戏、扩散模型画的形状、小语言模型的语言与 copying heads；“writing, from scratch, small game-playing networks that outplay networks trained with PPO for 100 million steps”；写围棋网络。数清共几个实验、哪些有工具（Python）、哪些禁止前向传播。
- 外部评论：Sean Goedecke https://www.seangoedecke.com/llms-are-not-superhuman-at-reading-and-writing-neural-network-weights/ （ChatGPT 扫描称其认为这是已有 steering/ablation 路线，非复现）。HN 50341174 “Astra can read and write weights to improve Qwen”（Claude 北京 11-03 19:00 现查 2692 分 813 评，HN 首页 AI 类第一）。
