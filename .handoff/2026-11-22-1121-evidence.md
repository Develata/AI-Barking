## 已知情况（Claude 2026-11-22 北京 22:45–22:55 已读，须独立复核）

- arXiv `https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06783`：标题“Truly Subquadratic 3SUM and Truly Subcubic APSP via Triangles in Sparse Lopsided Graphs”，作者 Josh Alman、Virginia Vassilevska Williams；“[Submitted on 5 Oct 2026]”，版本历史只见 v1；摘要：“we show how to deterministically solve 3SUM on n integers of polynomial size in O(n^{1.9992}) time and APSP on directed n-vertex graphs with polynomially bounded integer weights in O(n^{2.9995}) time.
