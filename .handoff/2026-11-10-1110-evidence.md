## 已知情况（Claude 2026-11-11 已读，须独立复核）

- Google 博文 `blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-4-carbon-now-available/`（作者 Koray Kavukcuoglu；Sundar X 帖 2026-11-10 16:04:43 UTC，GoogleAI 帖 16:12:06 UTC）：API 对“all paying developers and enterprises”开放，“at the same introductory price 1 as Gemini 4 Argon ($2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens)”；脚注 1“After the introductory period expires on November 30, 2026, the price of $4 per 1M input tokens and $20 per 1M output tokens will apply. Applies to the Carbon model intended for public access only.”；普通用户“Try Carbon starting today in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search if you're a Google AI Ultra subscriber”；“Two weeks ago, we released Carbon to a set of cybersecurity defenders via our Fairwind Program”。
- 同文：“Carbon ranks #2 while being more than 6X lower in average cost per task compared to the top performing model”（MKA 基准，与 AA 合作推出）。AA 文 `artificialanalysis.ai/articles/google-deepmind-multimodal-knowledge-agent`：Fable 5.2 (max) 57%、Carbon (high) 54%、Opus 5.5 (max) 53%；“Claude Fable 5.2 averages $31.97 per task, over 5× Gemini 4 Carbon ($6.29)”。31.97/6.29≈5.08，与“more than 6X”对不上；Google 用的哪个价格、哪个档位未说明。
- AA 10/28 文写“Google is offering a 50% launch discount, with no confirmed end date. All our charts and figures use standard pricing”。
- 同文 Project Fortify“100% recall on scanning and 95% precision on triage, on our internal benchmark”；另有 CursorBench 4.0、Perplexity WANDR、Zapier AutomationBench“more than 2X lower cost per task compared to the next best external model”，未给表。
- AA changelog 11/09：Fable 5.2 (Max) 综合指数 65，高于 Carbon 的 59。
- HN 50442303：282 分/155 评；高赞评论称 Gemini 应用里仍只有 3.1 Pro 等，像是只给 Ultra。
